## Answer first

Each on-hit rider gets its own simple price below (the most it is worth on a body, the larger of Level 1 and Level 5, as the ladder prices). 45 riders, each at N = 1, 2, 3 (or once, when it is an on/off rider). Measurement only: no price was changed.

- **Damage-over-time and stacking riders are under-priced, about 1.5-3x.** Bleed 4.7 / 10.0 / 12.0 (table 2.6 / 4.7 / 6.8), Poison 3.8 / 8.9 / 11.1, Breach 10.2 / 16.0 / 18.3 (table 3.0 / 6.1 / 9.1), Perforate 12.7 / 17.0 / 18.6 (table 3.9 / 7.9 / 7.9), Decay, Desecrate, Toxin, Greater Poison, Scar, Wither, Psychic. This matches the ladder (Poison, Bleed, Delivery over their prices).
- **Some riders do not grow with N.** Arson, Sabotage and Pickpocket are worth the same at 1, 2 and 3 (Pickpocket about 9, Arson / Sabotage about 6.5: the foe's resources run out); Sleep, Stagger, Stun on Hit and Tangle barely grow. Price them flat, not per N. Weaken, Stun on Hit, Tangle and Constrict at N 2-3 are over-priced (the table doubles them; their worth does not).
- **Riders worth under 1 point alone:** Brittle, Hemorrhage, Disarm, Detonate, Pulverise, Sequestration, Expose, Web, Pry. Most are enablers (Brittle needs Shatter, Hemorrhage needs Bleed, Disarm and Pulverise need equipment), so their worth only shows in a pair. They are the clearest case where a single price cannot capture the skill; price them at their partner's gain or leave them near zero.
- **Same rule under two keys:** Poison = Venomous, Breach = Puncture, Arson = Sabotage (here, where the foe's resources are mostly Lumber). One price each pair.
- Status riders printed as {chance, duration} were measured at chance 50, duration N (Freeze 2.7 / 3.7 / 3.6, Sleep about 3.4, Stagger about 2.4, Confuse 1.8-2.7, Shock 2.1-2.7, Blind 1.9-2.9, Paralyze 1.3-2.8, Tipsy 0.7-1.9).

Caveats: 400 paired fights a variant and three bodies a level (Attack 1, the middle, the most), not the ladder's 7-8, for time on shared cores; a rider whose best body is between them reads a little low. The json holds the mean over bodies too (BAL29's unit) and every body. A longer run: `node tools/bal31/onhit_2026_11_08.js run --games 800 --bodies all` (about 2.5 h on free cores) tightens the intervals and uses the ladder's bodies.
