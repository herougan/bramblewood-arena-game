VFX Playground

Working sketches for three places effects can go next: how cards look, what skills do on the board, and how maps feel. Every demo is live code that could be lifted into the game. It uses GSAP 3.13 (all plugins are now free) and WebGL.

Your system asks for reduced motion. The demos still run when you press a button, so you can judge them.

Cream pearl foil

A WebGL layer over the card: a cream, pearly base with paper grain, a soft rainbow that shifts as the card tilts, and specks that catch the light. Move your pointer over a card. This is the booster-foil look you asked for, and it would work as a card finish too.

Tech
Fragment shader + noise, tilt from pointer via gsap.quickTo
Cost
One canvas per foil card. For many cards, share one canvas (see notes).

Finish: Cream pearl

Burn-away death

The card dissolves along a noise pattern with a glowing ember edge, and embers drift off. It could replace the flat flip for fire kills (Ember Wisp, Magma Titan), with variants: frost shatter, poison melt, bleed tear.

Tech
Noise-threshold shader, GSAP tween on one uniform, Physics2D embers
Cost
One short-lived canvas per death

Living ink frame

A hand-inked rarity ring that wobbles like it's being redrawn. It's cheap enough to put on every Legendary on the board, and it's how a rarity could be shown on the border, as you asked, without a gradient.

Tech
SVG feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap, GSAP tweens the noise seed
Cost
Very low; filters are GPU-composited

Anti-Air boomerang

A spinning throw on a curved path that hooks up into the flier, knocks feathers loose, and swings back on a different arc. Curved paths make "this hits fliers" readable at a glance.

Tech
MotionPathPlugin (curviness, autoRotate) + Physics2D feathers
Cost
DOM only, ~15 elements per throw

Claw, bite and slash marks

The strokes draw on fast, glow, then fade, and the card shakes with a custom wiggle. Each hit style (claw, bite, sting, peck) gets its own drawn shape, and skins could recolour them (fire claws, frost claws).

Tech
DrawSVGPlugin + CustomWiggle
Cost
Very low

Big moments in type

Phase changes, leader summons and Shield Call each get a short, physical title: letters drop in on their own, or scramble into place. Only for rare moments, so they stay special.

Tech
SplitText, ScrambleTextPlugin, CustomBounce
Cost
Very low

Time of day on the map

The map is graded by a shader, from day to dusk to night. Lantern pools glow around the skirmish nodes, stars come out, and fireflies drift. The Arena's day and night phases could use the same light, and so could a map's "night fights" or a world boss approaching.

Tech
Fragment shader over the map art, GSAP tweens one time-of-day uniform
Cost
One canvas, already how map layers work
