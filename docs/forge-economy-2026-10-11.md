# Forge pacing: how much Dust is a week? (2026-10-11)

Your questions were: is a week of Dust enough to take a Common to level 10? (probably yes) A Mythic? (probably not) A rare Castle? This note answers them with the game's real numbers, then says what changed today.

## Where Dust comes from

| Source | Dust | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily notices | 2 to 100 each, about **5** on average | 6 a day (3 at midnight, then 6 am, noon and 6 pm). D–B ranks pay 2–4; S–SSS are rare jackpots |
| Repeat clears | Elite 2, Boss 4, Raid boss 6 (+25% to +100% for S–SSS) | Costs 2–3 ⚡ Energy each; Energy refills 1 every 5 minutes (12 an hour) |
| First clears | Elite 8, Boss 20, Raid boss 35, Campaign boss 60 | One-off; with 175 fights on the road this is a steady stream while progressing |
| Weekly quests | 70 a week | Three tiers: 40, 20, 10 |
| Raids, Gauntlet, Ghost runs | 3–30 a run | Each mode has its own payout |
| Disenchanting extra copies | Common 5 … Mythic 150 | Mostly from packs |
| Arena (PvP) | 0–1 a win | Your rule from 2026-10-10: Laurels are the Arena's reward |
| Achievements | 10–200 each | One-off |
| ~~Any non-Conquest win~~ | ~~+8~~ | **Removed today** for modes with their own payout (see below) |

### A week, three kinds of player

| Player | Day | Dust a week |
|---|---|---|
| **Light** (two short check-ins) | 3 notices (15) + ~40 ⚡ of repeat clears (~45) | **~420** |
| **Regular** (about an hour) | 5 notices (25) + ~80 ⚡ (~90) + weekly quests | **~850** |
| **Heavy** (2 h+, raids, Gauntlet) | 6 notices (30) + ~150 ⚡ (~170) + modes + weekly | **~1,500** |

While a player is still pushing the road, first clears add a lot on top (a map's elites and boss pay about 60 Dust).

## What levelling costs now

Levels 0 → 10 cost **690 Dust and 460 Maple Leaves** for a Common (the old flat price for every card). From today, rarer cards cost a multiple:

| Rarity | × | Dust to Lv 10 | Weeks for a Regular player |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 | 690 | **~0.8** ✓ "a week is enough" |
| Uncommon | 1.2 | 828 | ~1 |
| Rare | 1.5 | 1,035 | ~1.2 |
| Epic | 2.5 | 1,725 | ~2 |
| Heroic | 3 | 2,070 | ~2.5 |
| Unique | 3.5 | 2,415 | ~3 |
| Legendary | 4.5 | 3,105 | ~3.7 |
| **Mythic** | 6 | 4,140 | **~5** ✓ "maybe not" |
| Ancient | 8 | 5,520 | ~6.5 |

Special rarities (Quest, Event, Dev) use the tier they stand in for.

**Castles** level to 20. A Common castle costs 1,760 Dust in all; rarer castles cost more, less steeply than cards:

| Castle rarity | × | Dust to Lv 20 | Weeks (Regular) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common (the plain Castle) | 1 | 1,760 | ~2 |
| Uncommon | 1.15 | 2,024 | ~2.4 |
| **Rare** (Outskirts, Hedgerow, Tidewall, Bellhouse) | 1.35 | 2,376 | **~2.8** |
| Epic (the later map castles) | 1.7 | 2,992 | ~3.5 |
| Legendary (Coral Spire) | 2.5 | 4,400 | ~5 |

Every castle used to be Unique. They now have rarities by how far along the road they come (listed above).

## What changed today

1. **Level costs scale with rarity** (the table above). Already-levelled cards keep their levels.
2. **Castle costs scale with castle rarity**, and castles have rarities.
3. **The free +8 Dust per win is gone** from every mode that has its own payout (Arena, Gauntlet, Ghost runs, Raids, Dungeon runs). It was the biggest faucet in the game: farmable without Energy, and it broke your "Arena Dust should be 0–1" rule. Quick battles against the computer keep +2 Dust and +10 Maple Leaves a win.

## So it "sticks": ideas for the next step

- **Material gates on the last levels.** Levels 7–10 of Epic-and-up cards also need a Blacksmith material (a Tempering Stone), and Mythic level 10 needs a **Dragon Scale** (the hidden substory). Dust alone never finishes a top card.
- **A weekly Forge cap is not needed.** Energy already caps Conquest Dust; the notices and weeklies are capped by design.
- **Watch the heavy end.** A Heavy player finishes a Mythic in about 3 weeks. If that feels fast, the material gate above is the lever, not the Dust price.

## Built today alongside it

### Blacksmith (Forge → ⚒️ Blacksmith)
- **Materia are cards** you can't put in a deck: ⛓️ Iron Shard (Common), 🦪 River Pearl (Uncommon), 🔥 Ember Core (Rare) and 🐉 Dragon Scale (Legendary). They show in the Inventory and the Codex, not in the Nest.
- **Where Materia comes from:**
  - Elite first clears: 1 Iron Shard.
  - Boss first clears: 2 Iron Shards and a River Pearl.
  - Raid and Campaign bosses: 3 Iron Shards and an Ember Core.
  - Any repeat win: 15% chance of an Iron Shard.
  - An SS or SSS rank: 10% chance of an Ember Core.
- **Six recipes**, each Materia + one card + Dust/Leaves → an equipment card:

  | Equipment | Rarity | Gives the unit | Recipe |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Bark Buckler | Common | +0/+5, Armor 1 | 2 Iron Shards + Bramble Sprout |
  | Netmender's Harpoon | Uncommon | +2/+0, Reach | 2 Iron Shards + Otter Netmender |
  | Quill Spear | Uncommon | +3/+0, Pierce 1 | 2 Iron Shards, River Pearl + Quillguard |
  | Sunfeather Plume | Rare | +1/+2, Flying | 2 Iron Shards, River Pearl + Petal Fletcher |
  | Siege Hammer | Rare | +2/+0, Demolisher 3 | 3 Iron Shards, Ember Core + Badger Sapper |
  | Dragonscale Mail | Legendary | +0/+12, Armor 2 | 4 Iron Shards, Ember Core + Dragon Scale |

- **Fuse:** melt 3 spare copies of a card into 1 random card a rarity higher (up to Epic). You always keep one copy.
- **Equipment in a fight** (my reading of "equipment can't block attacks"):
  - Equipment never takes a place on the field. You drag it from your hand onto one of your units.
  - It costs its Lumber and uses your play for the turn. One piece per unit.
  - When the unit dies, the equipment goes to the graveyard with it.
- **Demolisher N:** +N damage against Structures. It's on Badger Sapper (2), Mouse Sapper (1), Beetle Battering-Ram (3) and the Siege Hammer (3).

### Nest
- **The hatchery:** seven hay nests in a hexagon under a windy sky, with gusts and drifting leaves.
- **Types:** every egg and baby shows its type (Woodland, Bird, Otter, Scaly, Bug, Water, Beast). It grows into a random card of that type, Common to Rare and never a Base card. A ring around each nest fills as it grows.
- **Where they come from:**
  - The first egg is a Speckled (Bird) Egg on Map 3, Lilypad Reach.
  - A boss's first clear leaves an egg of its people's type 30% of the time.
  - Any won fight has a 6% chance of a **rescued baby** of the enemy's main type. Babies grow in 4 hours; eggs take 6–10.
- **More than seven:** extras wait in the basket for a free nest.

### Changeling 🎭
- **What it is:** a new type of kind little mimics. A Changeling shares a type with every unit, both ways, so it counts for Swarm and for every type-based trigger.
- **"Slips in anywhere":** three of them, one in each kind of pool.
  - Puddle Changeling (Pack 1).
  - Hedge Changeling, which also has Swarm (the shared Riverbank pool of both faction packs).
  - Glass Changeling (the Mystery Booster).
- **Not built yet: disguise and detection.** On the enemy's side a Changeling would look like an ordinary critter until it's hit, and "Keen Eye" units would spot it on sight.

### The Scale Trail (hidden)
- **Where the scales are:** eight fights hide a Dragon Scale, all of them harder elites, half of them on side maps (4-A-4, 9-7, 10-7, 13-7, 16-A-5, 17-A-4, 19-9, 20-8). An S rank or better there finds it, once.
- **Hidden until found:** nothing mentions the trail until the first scale. After that, the other fights glint on the map, and the Inventory keeps a journal.
- **The story:** each scale adds a line. Together they follow the dragon that took the Crown.
