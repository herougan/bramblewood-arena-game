## How it works

**Map spots.** A map spot appears once its map is open. Clicking it unlocks the feature and plays a short conversation. New conversations were written for:
- the Codex;
- the Quest Board;
- the Forge;
- the Gilded Caravan;
- the Wanderer's Camp;
- the Gauntlet;
- the Test Lab.

**What existing saves keep:**
- A player who already had the Armoury keeps the Codex and the Forge.
- A player who already had the Arena keeps the Gauntlet.
- A player already past Map 9, 13 or 21 gets those spots' features straight away.

**Free packs:**
- They come on the first clear of the map's boss, or of its last fight when the map has no boss.
- They wait under 🎁 Your packs in the Shop.
- They open for free even before that pack is on sale.

**Cards revealed (the Codex):**
- A player's Codex shows only cards they own or have seen.
- A pack's cards also show once its seller is open: the Cart for the basic pools, the Wanderer's Camp for Mystery cards.
- Admin sees everything. Materia never appear in the Codex; they live in the Inventory.

**Errands (the Quest Board).**
- From Map 9, a Conquest win sometimes (30%) ends with someone on the road asking a favour.
- Say yes and it's pinned under Errands on the Notices. Let it pass and it waits there as an offer until the day ends.
- You can hold up to 3. Each pays 4–8% of a Materia Unit in Dust and Maple Leaves, and sometimes an Iron Shard.
