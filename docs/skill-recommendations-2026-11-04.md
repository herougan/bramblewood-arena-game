How the rows were chosen:
- One job per card, two skills at most (plain Flying, Tide, Diurnal, Nocturnal free), from the card's name and animal first, then the map's field or rule, then variety against the cards around it.
- Vanilla has value (DESIGN1-2): the starters, House Cat, Mangrove Monitor and Stag Champion stay plain as baselines. A starter that gets an ability gets it at Level 1 (DESIGN1-2, ENG22-4), so its Level 0 copy is unchanged. DESIGN1-8 c (rarity is stats) holds: a Rare gets a job here only for its name or a near-twin, never for its rarity.
- Budget columns come from `tools/skill_costs.js` (`budget()`) on `canonical/cards.json` as it is on this branch, with the card's printed stats and the proposed skills. ENG22's counter skills and Fortify use ENG22's theoretical prices (Tally 3.28, Almanac 4.0, Onslaught 2.8, Riled 2.4, Fortify 3.04 at the owner's example). Skitter, the Fast Forward token and the Crepuscular split have no price yet.
- ENG22 (Build / Fortify, Delivery, Taunt and Guardian on shots, Carnage and its counters Tally / Almanac / Onslaught / Riled, Cricket Drummer Inspiring 1, the Level 1 rule) is built on its own branch and not yet merged into batch-1023e; Skitter, the Crepuscular split and the Fast Forward token are decided and not built. The rows that need them say so.
- Not about when a skill is introduced (the owner: "Not the same thing as when to introduce too"). Where a row puts a skill on a map before the road teaches it, the "?" rule (ENG22-4) covers the tutorial and Map 0.

## 5. Skills on no card, and overused skills

Counted on this branch over the 365 in-play cards (every card on a Map 0-24 list, side maps and SPECIAL included; the endless dungeons left out) against the 392 skill entries of `canonical/skill-costs.json` (plain keywords, triggers as `On X > Do Y`).

**On no card at all (82 entries).** Most are a part of another skill (Arrow / Fire Arrow / Fire Bolt riders, Hunter's status, Bloom's conditions, Dive uses, Swimmer's and Full Moon's grants, Salvage / Scavenge every turn, the transforms Gilded / Championed / Veteran / Matured, Kingly, the field hazards). - Bodies and defence: Bolster, Formation, Shieldwall, Shield Call, Spellward, Swift, Swimmer, Anchor, Satiety (Well Fed), Shedding, Injured.

## 6. Budget and what applying it takes

- Nothing here changes stats. Each "over" row needs the owner's re-fit by `bramblewood-budget.js` (BAL-OWNER-1); "frees room" rows can gain a stat point. The skills with no price yet (Skitter, the Fast Forward token, the Crepuscular split) are priced first by the 4-round rule or the pipeline (`tools/pipeline/run.sh measure`).
- Order if adopted: merge ENG22 (Build, Fortify, Delivery, Taunt, the counters); build Skitter, the Fast Forward token and the Crepuscular split; then the rows by map, each card through `docs/workflows/card-data.md` (cards.json, texts in 12 languages, the card matrix).
- The road decks are not touched. Where a road deck holds a changed card, its fight moves; re-measure those maps after (`tools/skirmish_pass.js measure`).
- Two rows go against an earlier placement and say so: Moon Jelly loses Moonpull (the 10-16 note said it waits past Map 10-20), and Pack Mule trades Bounty for Delivery (BAL2-4 keeps Bounty where thematic; a mule is a carrier).
