# Siding with Otters or Hummingbirds: proposal (2026-10-08)

**Your ask:** "I want to make decisions important: slowly siding with Otters or Hummingbirds gives you different rewards."
**Status:** proposal, waiting on you (decision G2). Nothing is built yet.

## The idea in one line
You're a traveller both peoples want on their side. Every choice you make nudges a **Standing** meter between the Otters (Rivergate Legion) and the Hummingbirds (Sunfeather Tribes). Where you stand decides which rewards, titles, characters and late-game paths open up. You can always drift back, but it takes effort.

## The Standing meter
- One number from **−100 (Otters)** to **+100 (Hummingbirds)**, starting at the side you picked in the tutorial (Otters −10, Hummingbirds +10, both 0).
- Five bands: **Sworn Otter (≤ −60) · Otter friend (−59…−20) · Neutral (−19…19) · Sunfeather friend (20…59) · Sworn Sunfeather (≥ 60)**.
- Shown as a small two-coloured banner on Profile and the Conquest map, with the band name. Hovering explains what moved it lately.

## What moves it
| Source | Effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dialogue choices | ±2 to ±8 | The scenes already have choices (e.g. the Old Well). Choices get a quiet "leaning"; nothing says "+5 Otter" on the button. |
| Which side's skirmishes you take | ±1 per first clear | Many maps have fights against one people. Fighting Otters drifts you towards the Hummingbirds and vice versa. |
| Side quests (new) | ±5 to ±15 | A few per map: escort a Legion patrol, carry a tribe's message, return a lost egg. Often mutually exclusive. |
| Neutral acts | toward 0 | Helping dogs, cats, road-folk, the Traveller pulls you gently to Neutral. Neutral is a real path, not a failure. |

Drift is slow by design: roughly 2–3 maps of consistent choices to reach a "friend" band, most of the campaign to be "Sworn".

## What it changes
**Rewards (the core of the ask)**
- **Reward cards by band:** some skirmishes offer two reward cards, one per side; you get the one matching your band (Neutral gets a third, road-folk or dog/cat card).
- **Side shops:** the Legion Quartermaster and the Tribe Trader appear on later maps with different stock (Otter structures, discipline skills; Hummingbird beast-bond and flight skills).
- **Titles and character looks:** "Legion Outrider", "Friend of the Tribes", "Wanderer of Both Banks"…
- **Materia leaning:** each side's crafting nudges your Hero's skill choices (Otter: Shield/Formation; Hummingbird: Flight/Bond).

**Story**
- Dialogue lines change by band (warmer from your side, colder from the other).
- **Map 6+ fork:** a key skirmish plays differently depending on your band (who asks for help, who you face).
- **Neutral ending track:** keep both sides at peace through a series of tests. Hardest, with unique rewards.

**What it never does**
- It never locks you out of a whole people's cards permanently: off-side cards stay obtainable through packs and the Market, just not as story rewards.
- It never punishes the tutorial pick: you can move anywhere from your start.

## Build plan (if you approve)
1. Standing data + banner on Profile and map; dialogue choices get hidden weights (Old Well first).
2. Two-sided reward cards on 3–4 skirmishes of Maps 1–3 (picked with you in the skirmish editor).
3. Side quests: 2 per map for Maps 1–3.
4. Side shops (Map 4+), titles, Map 6 fork.
