# Which cards belong where (2026-10-15)

A proposal only. Nothing moved on the card sheet, the maps or the code. The detail is in `docs/card-placement-proposal-2026-10-15.xlsx`:
- **Placement:** 472 rows, one per enemy card per map, Maps 0–21 and all 15 side maps.
- **Skirmish rarity curve:** rarity counts per 20-card deck.
- **Skill curriculum (thematic):** which skills each map teaches, and why.
- **Cards per map count:** new vs reused cards, pack vs map-only.
- **Biome supply vs need:** surpluses and gaps.
- **Babies & eggs.**

The order of priorities was theme first (who actually lives there), then your skill list, then rarity.

## The short version: biggest moves

1. **Map 0 is the basic 10 plus ants.** 0-1 is the basic 10. 0-2 is a weak ant line with the Earthworm (Regeneration). 0-3 adds **one uncommon**: the Smokescreen Raccoon, the heist's getaway trick. 0-4 is a Fox Den and adds **one more**: the Fox Kit (relabel it Uncommon).
   - These leave Map 0, because each leaks a later skill: Bee Sentry (Poison), Beetle Grunt (Armor), Bee Forager (bee Swarm), Fleetfoot'd (Quick, Tide) and Glacier Wolf Pack (Frost, Bleed).
2. **Sunken Hollow (Map 4) becomes beaver country.** It is a flooded lowland, so the beavers flooded it. The whales, orca and kraken go to the open sea: Coral Current and the Sunken Depths.
3. **The mountain birds leave Gannet Cliffs** (condors, vultures, avalanches, the Eyrie Warden) for Maps 11 and 21. The cliffs get the sea birds instead. Gannet, Puffin and Peregrine don't exist yet: they're the biggest gap in the set.
4. **Rainforest animals leave the savanna** for the Canopy. Apes and panthers leave the Redwood for the Sundered Peak. Village dogs and cats leave Clovermead for the Long Grass.
5. **Magic and the weird stuff is parked for Map 50+.** That means the Basalt Foundry's devils and imps, the Eye of the Volcano's undead, the Phoenix, the Fire Drake Hatchling and the Blessed Avatar.
   - The Foundry and the crater are rebuilt from fire animals (geckos, centipedes, hornets, salamanders).
   - The Caldera Wyrm stays as the one dragon-shaped exception (it is your Map 20 world boss).
6. **The Tutorial keeps the basics only.** The "come back later" exam role of the Odd Lot and the Final Exam moves to **The Old Bramble**, which already unlocks late. Its boss is the Ent, and it introduces Counterattack on the Thornwood Palisade.
7. **12 rarity relabels are needed later.** These are babies and small animals that are "Rare" today but are really filler: Fox Kit, Bear Cub, Fawn Scout, Stable Colt, Plains Zebra, Dust Hyena, Mountain Goat Climber, Bog Leech, Cave Flitter, Grass Viper, Rockslide Ram and Warren Scout. Without these relabels, any rarity curve breaks.

## 1. Rarity scaling, skirmish by skirmish

The rules (the full table is on "Skirmish rarity curve"):
- **Regular skirmishes never carry Epic, Heroic or Unique.** Not on Map 40 either. Rares come in slowly:
  - Maps 0–1: none.
  - Map 2: the first "rare spawn" (one copy, in one fight).
  - Map 10: 2 per deck.
  - Map 16: 3 per deck.
  - Map 20 adds one Very rare.
- **From Map 1 the last fight is the boss, and it gets the rarity pass:** one Heroic (the boss card itself) plus 2–6 rares. That is the only place high rarity lives on Maps 1–3.
- **Elites start on Map 4** (the fight before the boss). They sit halfway between a skirmish and the boss: about +2 rares, and from Map 10 one Very rare.
- **Commons never drop below a third of a regular deck** before Map 30. Pack commons stay the backbone of what you fight.

## 3. "Commons in packs won't be seen, rares will?" That would topple the imagination economy

You're right that it would, if rarity decided where cards appear. So let **role** decide, not rarity. Three layers:

1. **Pack commons are the filler you fight all the time.**
   - Rule: *seen before sold*. Every non-faction pack common shows up in at least one skirmish of its biome, ideally before its pack is on sale.
   - Example: you fight Trash Panda Tricksters on Map 0, and later open one.
   - This proposal places 22 of the 23 non-faction pack commons. The one left is Glowworm Cluster, because its Prayer belongs to Map 21.
2. **Pack rares are glimpsed, not farmed.** They appear once, as a one-copy rare spawn or in an elite. Examples: Truce Heron in the Map 4 elite, Dolphin Knight as a spawn in 10-5, Shieldbearer Hound in the 15 elite. 18 of the 22 non-faction pack rares are placed this way. The 4 left (Weredog, Two-Headed Calf, Glass Golem, Glass Changeling) are oddities that can stay pack surprises.
3. **Map-only trophies are never in packs.** That covers boss heroics, submap uniques and map rewards. This is what makes a map worth replaying and gives the base set its identity.

The result: commons build recognition ("I know that guy"), rares build desire ("I saw that once"), and trophies give each map its pride. Today only 28 of the 88 pack cards appear in any fight. This plan raises that to 48 of the 57 non-faction pack cards.

## 4. Is Quick on Map 3 too much?

**As a definite skill, yes. As a single maybe, fine.**
- Arrow, Pounce and Evasive already make the cliffs the busiest map so far, and they hang together: birds dive, birds shoot, goats slip away.
- No sea-cliff animal in the set is Quick. The honest carrier is a **Peregrine** (it nests on sea cliffs and is the fastest animal alive), and it doesn't exist yet.
- My proposal: if you make the Peregrine, it is the one-copy rare spawn in 3-5 and sits in the boss deck. Otherwise Quick is taught on **Map 9** by the Mangrove Mudskipper, and the Savanna Cheetah repeats it on Map 14.
- One wrinkle: Map 0's rewards already hand out Quick (Tomcat, Chipmunk Cavalry), along with Stealth (Owl Nightwatch) and Guardian (Shepherd). You said to ignore Source for now, but the reward pass will need the same curriculum check.

## 5. "Too many cards, but do we have enough?"

Both. There are **479 cards that could be enemies**. This plan uses **339 distinct cards** (333 existing and 6 new), against 244 in today's decks. Of the 147 existing cards left over, 77 are deliberately parked: 47 player-faction cards and 30 "late" cards (dragons, devils, undead, irradiated). That leaves about 70 truly idle cards, mostly pack oddities and extra woodland and bug cards.

**Surplus**, with more cards than the maps need:
- **Woodland & hedgerow (+41):** rabbits, chipmunks, mice, foxes, bears, deer, boars.
- **Undergrowth & fungi (+32).**
- **Freshwater (+24).**
- **Farm & village (+20).**
- **Sea (+17).** Enough for a second sea map in 22–40, e.g. the Abyssal Plains on Map 29.
- **Rainforest (+10 for one side map).** It deserves a main map in 22–40.

**Gaps:**
- **Shore and cliffs (−11):** no Gannet on Gannet Cliffs, no Puffin, no Peregrine, no Archerfish.
- **Mountain (−3):** nothing carries Volley or Twin Shot, which belong on a Chough Flock and a Kestrel. The mountain also has no young.
- **Savanna:** 10 cards for an 8-fight map. There are no lions or wild dogs below boss level and no gazelle, so Pack has no savanna carrier.
- **Volcanic:** exactly enough, but only once the devils and undead are parked.

New cards proposed: **6.** They are Gannet, Puffin and Peregrine (Map 3), Archerfish (Map 9), and Chough Flock and Kestrel (Map 11). Two babies are optional extras (see below).

## 6. Skirmish variations as energy barriers

Agreed: not every fight needs to be new. A pattern for each main map from Map 5:
- **1 teach fight:** the new skill on its own natives, with nothing else new.
- **1–2 mix fights:** the new skill plus the biome.
- **About a third of fights are variations of an earlier one.** Change exactly one knob: the field (rain, fog, moon), the time of day, +1 level, the deck order, or one card swapped. Name them as pairs: "Leech Bog" / "Leech Bog at Night".
- **The elite**, which carries the rarity.
- **The boss.**

Side maps are pure exams: one unique card each (most have 1–3), otherwise reused cards. Every one of the 15 side maps has at least one unique.

## 8. Babies and eggs

**Eggs: add none.** The six generic eggs already cover every family:
- Woodland (your "Green egg"; consider renaming it), Speckled, Leathery, Cocoon, Roe Pearl, and the Den Bundle (the mammal den bundle).
- **Retire the River Bundle.** It is the one species-specific egg (Otters only), and the Den Bundle already hatches Mustelids.
- Dragon eggs stay as the deliberate Map 50+ exception.

**Babies: about 20 exist,** counting the kits, cubs, fawn, colt, pup, hatchlings, fry, ducklings, hoglet, baby bat, capybara, grub and the starter fledglings.
