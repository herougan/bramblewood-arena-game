# Pack plan for Maps 0–20 (proposal, 2026-10-12)

Your plan: map cards tell the Otters-and-Hummingbirds story along the road. Each pack opens after a band of maps and holds that band's cards. The Mystery (advanced) pack holds unique cards. On top of that, some cards are only ever earned on their map.

## Proposed structure

| Pack | Opens | Pool |
|---|---|---|
| Pack 1 | After Map 5 | All map cards from Maps 0–6, except map-exclusive ones |
| Pack 2 | After Map 11 | All map cards from Maps 7–11, except map-exclusive ones |
| Pack 3 | After Map 17 | All map cards from Maps 12–17, except map-exclusive ones |
| Pack 4 | After Map 20 | All map cards from Maps 18–20, except map-exclusive ones |
| Mystery (advanced) | Wandering trader | Unique cards found nowhere else |

### How it works

- **Pools follow the maps.** A pack's pool is worked out from each card's map source, so a card you move between maps in the Ledger lands in the right pack automatically. Nothing has to be listed by hand.
- **Map-exclusive cards.** A new per-card flag, "Map-exclusive", shown as a checkbox in the Ledger, keeps a card out of every pack. Quest cards and skirmish-only rewards would use it.
- **Booster-only cards.** Today's 43 pack-1 cards each get a band (1–4) based on their biome, and stay pack-only inside that band's pack. This covers your "Booster cards" category.
- **Shop tiers.** The current shop items (Sprout Pouch, Acorn Chest, Golden Bramble Case) become size tiers of the current band's pack: 3, 5 or 8 cards. The pack you buy is chosen by how far along the road you are.
