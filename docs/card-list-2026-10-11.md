# Card list v2: the 21-map road and the faction packs (2026-10-11)

A thinking document, not a spec. Nothing on the map side is wired yet. The faction packs are wired, using the cards in section 3, so you can open them and feel the odds; every stat there is a first guess for you to change.

**How to read it:** C/W is Lumber cost / Wait. A/H is Attack / Health. "reuse" means the card exists with art, "from Pack 1" means it moves out of Pack 1, and "new" means it needs art and data. Stat budget from the last list: A+H ≈ 6 at 0/0, +4 per Wait, +6 per Lumber, about 3 per keyword.

## Decisions I need from you

1. **Map 1 and Map 12.** Thistle Fields is grassland, but your road has Forest at 1 and Shrubbery at 12. My suggestion: re-skin Thistle Fields as **Thistlewood** (Map 1) and keep its easy fights. Shrubbery (12) gets the thorny late-game creatures: porcupines, quill boars and hedge rodents.
2. **Two Swamps (7 and 9).** Is Map 9 meant to be a repeat? I'd make it **The Mire**, the haunted deep swamp of the Forgotten (Bog Revenant, The Unremembered, the Heron Hag). Map 7 stays the living swamp (gators, toads, leeches).
3. **Where the sea goes.** Atoll (6) and Kelp Swamp (8) stay shallow: reefs, crabs, mangroves. Orcas, narwhals, krakens and the Reef Admiral move to a post-20 **Open Sea** map.
4. **Where rainforest goes.** Jaguar, poison dart frog, mandrill and sloths have no main map. I suggest a Savannah sub-map, **17-A Canopy**.
5. **Faction pack odds.** "1% from the other side's pool" is built as the other side's **10 own cards** (their shared 5 are already in your pool).
6. **Food tokens.** I priced the Hummingbird pack at **5 🍯**. You get 1 for every first clear and a 10% chance on a repeat clear.

## 3. Faction packs (live in this build)

**Rules:**
- 2 cards per pack.
- For each card, roll where it comes from: **89%** your side's 15 (10 own + 5 shared), **10%** Pack 1, **1%** the other side's 10.
- Rarity inside a pool uses the normal pack weights (Common 6, Uncommon 3, Rare 1.5, Epic 0.6, Legendary 0.25).
- **Bonus card:** if neither card is Uncommon or better, a third card is rolled from your side's Commons and Uncommons. With these pools that happens in a little under half of all packs (47% in a 20,000-pack test).

**Where to get them:**
- Neither pack is sold for Maple or Gold Leaves.
- Elite-and-up fights drop one on their first clear, and 5% of the time on a repeat. You get the Otter pack against the Rivergate Legion and the Hummingbird pack against the Sunfeather Tribes; against anyone else you get your own side's pack.
- The Hummingbird pack can also be bought in the Shop for 🍯 Food tokens.
- Unopened packs wait in the Shop under **Your packs**.

- Today's Map 3 (Pebble Beach) becomes Map 4, and the Grotto becomes 4-A.

- Today's Map 5 (Ashen Peak) splits up: the frost fights go to the Tundra (16) and the quill fights to the Shrubbery (12).
- Today's Maps 6–10 (Caves, Savanna, Tundra, Coral, Swamp) become Maps 10, 17, 16, Open Sea and 7/9.
