# Bramblewood lore bible (2026-10-05)

**What this is:** the reference for who lives in Bramblewood, how they look and how they fight. Use it when generating art, designing skirmishes or writing cards and their lines. It supersedes the tone of `lore-otters-vs-hummingbirds.md` where they differ. The Crown of Currents plot, Whisper-of-Ash and the Twin Bloom still stand; the Otter and Hummingbird *cultures* are re-cast below.

## The world in one paragraph

Almost every animal in Bramblewood can talk, but talking doesn't make it civilised. It's still an animal-eat-animal world: most creatures live by tooth, claw and appetite, and only a few have built anything like a society. The two that have built the most are the **Otters**, a disciplined river legion, and the **Hummingbirds**, a loose federation of brightly painted tribes. They've gone to war. Everyone else is caught between them:
- the **mice and rats** who wander the roads;
- the **other mammals** who sell to, mend and feed both armies;
- the **beasts**, who mostly just want to eat.

## The peoples

### Otters: the Rivergate Legion
**Culture:** regimented, pre-Roman-empire. A young republic becoming an empire:
- Built: roads, river forts, bridges and stone-and-timber camps.
- Ranked: centuries, centurions, standard-bearers, a Marshal.
- Bound together by oath and drill.

They win by formation, not ferocity. They tame and train **fish** as companions and mounts, the way Rome had war-dogs and cavalry: pike lancers, carp couriers, trout shield-mates.

**Look:**
- Bronze and iron; red-brown wool tunics; rectangular shields with a wave or fish emblem.
- Crested helmets; standards topped with a bronze fish.
- Neat ranks; river forts with palisades.
- **Palette:** bronze, oxblood red, river blue, cream.

**On the board:**
- Guardian, Armor and shield-wall effects; Rally and formation bonuses.
- Discipline: steady stats, Wait 0–1.
- Fish companions that spawn or fight beside them.
- Few tricks.

**Voice:** clipped and formal, like military orders. *"Shields up. Hold the line. Advance on my mark."*

### Hummingbirds: the Sunfeather Tribes
**Culture:** tribal, proud and loud with colour. Many tribes, each named for its feather colour: Crimson Wing, Cobalt Talon, Gold-throat, Mosswing, Violet Vane, Voidfeather, Sunthroat. They're bound by kinship, song and the Twin Bloom gatherings, not by an emperor. Their elders lead by voice and deed.

They are **friends with the large beasts**: the great stags, bears, boars and big cats will fight beside a tribe that sings to them. That's why a Hummingbird war-band can field things far bigger than itself.

**Look:**
- War-paint and feather headdresses; beads, totems, woven banners.
- Flower garlands, nests woven into cliffs.
- Movement everywhere, very bright.
- **Palette:** saturated jewel tones (crimson, cobalt, gold, violet, emerald) on warm cliff stone.

**On the board:**
- Speed: Flying, Evasive, Swift, Quick.
- Colour or tribe synergies; songs and blessings as buffs.
- Beast-bond: large beasts get bonuses alongside Hummingbirds.
- Glass-cannon attackers.

**Voice:** chant-like and boastful, nature-flavoured. *"The Bloom sings for us today."*

### Mice and rats: the road-folk
**Culture:** travellers and nomads. Caravans, peddlers, smugglers, rumour-sellers, scavengers and the occasional rat-king of a road gang. They belong to no side and sell to both. The **Wandering Traveller** in the shop is one of them.

**Look:**
- Patched cloaks; oversized packs and bedrolls; lanterns and walking sticks.
- Carts pulled by beetles; trinkets everywhere.
- **Palette:** dusty browns, faded indigo, lantern amber.

**On the board:**
- Economy and theft: pitch bonuses, stealing resources, Bounty.
- Moving and swapping cards; scavenging from the graveyard; cheap fodder.

**Voice:** haggling, sly, chatty. *"Everything's for sale, friend. Even advice. Especially advice."*

### Other mammals: the supporting folk
**Who:** badgers, moles, beavers, rabbits, chipmunks, deer folk, sloths, porcupines, turtles and the like. They're the craftsmen, builders, farmers, smiths, healers, ferrymen and shopkeepers who keep both armies fed and housed.
- Some are hired muscle (badger sappers, rabbit scouts).
- Most just want the war to go somewhere else.
- **Beavers** build for the Legion.
- **Chipmunks** stockpile and sell.

**On the board:**
- Support: heal, buff, build structures; generate Lumber or Stone.
- Defensive bodies; reliable, middling stats.
- Mercenary versions exist (Badger Berserker), but they fight for pay, not a flag.

**Voice:** practical, folksy, a bit weary. *"Another army through the turnips. Lovely."*

### Carnivores: the beasts
**Who:** wolves, foxes, wild cats, bears, snakes, crocodiles and monitors, scorpions and spiders, and raptors when they hunt.
- They talk, but they don't negotiate. They're hunger with a vocabulary.
- They form packs, not nations.
- The big ones can be befriended by Hummingbird song. Otters fight them, or wall them off.

**Look:**
- Lean and scarred; glowing eyes in the dark.
- Lairs, bones and claw marks.
- **Palette:** shadowed greens and browns with a single hot accent (eye glow, blood, ember).

**On the board:**
- High attack; Frenzy, Bleed, Ambush, Pack bonuses.
- Little or no support text; "feral" means no leader synergies.
- Big ones are natural Hummingbird beast-bond partners.

**Voice:** short, hungry, menacing. *"You smell like supper."*

### The few societies
Only a handful of creatures live in true societies: **ants, bees, termites and other hives**. They're collective and tireless, with a strange sort of fairness, all for the hive. They're neutral powers with their own territory (the hills, the hives).

**On the board:** spawning, swarming, sharing (colony and nest effects).

**Voice:** *"We."* Never *"I."*

### Fish and other water folk
- **Most fish** don't speak much and are tamed by the Otters as companions.
- **The deep things** (kraken, cuttlefish, whales) are old, strange and independent. Raid bosses live here.

## Using this

### For art (PixelLab or image prompts)
Start every prompt with the people's look line above, then the subject. Examples:
- *"Pixel art, pre-Roman otter legionary, bronze crested helmet, oxblood tunic, rectangular wave-emblem shield, river fort behind, bronze/oxblood/river-blue palette."*
- *"Pixel art, hummingbird tribal warrior, crimson war-paint, feather headdress, bead necklace, flower garland, cliff nests, saturated jewel tones."*
- *"Pixel art, mouse peddler, patched cloak, huge travelling pack with pots and lanterns, dusty road, lantern-amber light."*
- *"Pixel art, feral wolf, lean and scarred, glowing eyes, dark forest, single ember accent."*

**Map backgrounds:**
- Otter territory gets roads, forts and bridges.
- Hummingbird territory gets painted cliffs, totems and blossoms.
- Beast territory gets wild ground, lairs and bones.
- Road-folk get caravan camps.

### For skirmishes
Every skirmish should say **who** you're fighting, and the deck should match:
- **Otter Legion patrols:** disciplined decks (Otters, fish, Guardian). Lines are orders.
- **Hummingbird war-bands:** fast decks (Hummingbirds plus a big beast). Lines are boasts.
- **Beast hunts:** wild decks (one predator type, a pack). Lines are hunger.
- **Road-folk ambushes:** cheap thieving decks (mice and rats). Lines are haggling.
- **Hive defences:** swarm decks (ants, bees). Lines are "we".

Bosses are named characters from the plot where possible: Shieldback, Vesper, Aurelia, Whisper-of-Ash.

### For card design
- New cards get one people (above). Their keywords should come from that people's "On the board" list.
- Carnivores should not get support text (heal, resource generation).
- Mice and rats should not get heavy armour.
- Otter cards should not be fast and flimsy; Hummingbird cards should not be slow walls.

## Known mismatches in the current roster (proposals, nothing renamed yet)

| Card or thing | Why it doesn't fit | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Dominion Nestguard; "Sunfeather Dominion" in the old doc | "Dominion" reads as an empire; the Hummingbirds are tribes now | "Sunfeather Nestguard"; call the faction "the Sunfeather Tribes" |
| The old caste system (rank by colour) | Tribal, not a caste hierarchy | Keep the colours as **tribe** names (Crimson Wing tribe, etc.) |
| Stag Knight, Bee Knight, Dolphin Knight, Cataphract Destrier | Medieval knights don't fit a pre-Roman world | Stag → "Stag Champion" (beast-bonded); Bee → "Bee Warden"; Dolphin → "Dolphin Outrider"; Cataphract → "War Destrier" |
| War Hound, Armored Mastiff, Shepherd's Bark | Carnivores should be beasts, but these read as tamed | Either the Otters' kennels (like Roman war-dogs) or make them wild |
| Raccoon Warlord, Raccoon Bandit | Raccoons aren't covered by your list | Treat raccoons as road-folk (bandits and thieves), alongside rats |
| Rodent King | Fits as a rat road-gang boss | Keep; flavour as "king of the road" |
