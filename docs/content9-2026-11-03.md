## 5. Two bramble abilities (DESIGN1-10, draft; decision CONTENT9-2; not built)

Owner: "Depends, maybe we can invent it for 1 or 2 brambles. Also a bramble with devour/sacrifice (pitch a card to it to heal it and increase attack of all allies by +1)."

Today's 43 brambles have 12 passive kinds, every number 1 or 2, paid for with Health against their rarity (for example Ashen Bastion, Epic, 32 Health, first unit +2 Attack; Ballista Keep, Epic, 22 Health, a 2-damage bolt a round; Coral Spire, Legendary, 24 Health, units +2 Health). Both drafts add a decision during the fight, which no bramble does now.

### A. The Gnawed Larder (the owner's Devour bramble)

- **Rules text:** "Feed: once a turn, instead of pitching a card for Lumber, drag it onto your bramble. The bramble eats it: Repair Bramble 3, and your units on the field get +1 Attack for the rest of the fight. Three feeds a fight."
- **How it plays:** it replaces the pitch, so each feed costs the turn's Lumber; units played after a feed don't get that +1. The decision: Lumber now, or Health and a wider army's swing; and when, since a feed is worth most with a full board.
- **Engine fit:** the pitch path (a hand card to the Graveyard) with a bramble target, the Devour event and log (`devour`, `fireOnSacrifice` with 'devour', so Sacrifice - {} cards trigger off it), Repair Bramble's wording (EN8-3). New: a per-fight counter and the bramble as a drop target.
- **Price estimate:** a feed gives up about 2.65 stat points (the measured worth of 1 Lumber on Ready, `bramblewood-budget.js` BAL14 measures) and returns 3 bramble Health plus +1 Attack on about 3 units for the rest of the fight (about 2 x 3 = 6 points by the budget's 2A+H, half that on a thin board). Net about +3 to +6 a feed, +9 to +18 over three, against about +4 for Ashen Bastion's first-unit +2. So: **Epic, about 18 Health** (Ballista Keep's 22 minus a little for the snowball), and capped at three feeds; at one feed a fight it would be a Rare at about 22.
- **Where it could appear:** the Gnawed (lore bible: "they gather and grow", Devour) on the Sable Swampmire (Map 6): a drop from 6-8 The Alligator King, and the enemy bramble of one Gnawed fight there so the player sees the CPU feed it first. Its owner side: Hollow-belly Rat (Devour) is already on Map 1's list, so the idea is not new to the player.
- **Owner calls inside it:** three feeds or one; +1 Attack to units on the field now (as drafted) or to every unit for the rest of the fight; Repair 3 flat or the eaten card's Lumber cost + 2.

### B. Ballista Keep: Aimed Bolt (an active on an existing bramble)

- **Rules text:** "Aimed Bolt: once a fight, tap the bramble on your turn and pick an enemy column: its bolt hits the unit there for 4 (the bramble if the column is empty). It doesn't use your play. Its random bolt each round carries on."
- **How it plays:** the one bramble that already shoots gets the choice DESIGN1 problem 9 asked for: snipe a support card, finish a wounded wall, or save it for the boss's arrival.
- **Engine fit:** the castleBolt shot with a chosen target (the castle shot event and its log line exist); a once-a-fight flag; the AI aims it at the highest-Attack unit it can kill.
- **Price estimate:** 4 damage once is about 4 to 5 stat points (a Pounce-style hit). Ballista Keep stays Epic and goes from 22 to about 19 Health, or keeps 22 and the shot is 3.
- **Where it could appear:** where Ballista Keep comes from today; the same pattern could later give Thorn Cage "Briar Snare: once a fight, Tangle the unit in a column you pick for a round".
