# FXAUD1 phase 1: visual effects and sound audit (2026-11-18)

Report only. No game code changed (another package is in `arena_app.js`). Owner ask (11-17 23:41): "audit that our vfx and sfx is good." The polling cut is POLL1 (`docs/perf/poll1-2026-11-17.md`); not redone.

## Owner summary

- **ENG50 mechanics all have a look and a sound, but three sound wrong or are weak.** Paralysis Powder (yellow spores, own crackle, then the Stun cue), Rend, Enfeeble, Weaken, Grant, Armourer, Underwater Currents and Inspire's horn each show something and play something. Problems: Rend, Enfeeble and Exploit share one very quiet cue (Rend makes the target take double, Enfeeble makes it deal half, and they sound identical); the double-damage hit on a Vulnerable unit plays the **Shocked** cue and a hit by a Feeble unit plays the **armour ting** (bugs, S fixes); the Weaken float and the Vulnerable badge both use the broken heart.
- **Inspire's horn works** (once per burst, silent when hidden or muted) but is the loudest sustained cue in the game: about 10 dB above the median cue and 3 dB above a normal hit. The new ENG50 cues are among the quietest (4 to 7 dB below the median): Rend/Enfeeble, Weaken, the powder puff, the Wave splash.
- **Coverage:** of 394 event kinds the engine can send (191 played from real battles, 203 built from the source), every one draws something except a handful; about 60 of the 191 real ones make no sound of their own (list below; several are projectile or follow-up beats whose cue is on the paired hit, so a short check each, not 60 fixes). 2 cues are one-shots longer than 2 s (wolf and moon howl); no cue loops.
- **Cost:** at 1x no effect call or long task is over budget (calls 1 to 19 ms median, none above 50 ms long task in the median). Over the 16.7 ms frame: Armourer's buff (`namedBuff`, 29 ms) and `evolve` (28 ms). At 4x CPU (phone), 21 of the 40 heaviest events show a long task over 100 ms in at least one of three runs (32 over 50 ms); the repeat offenders are `pierceHQ`, `earthquake`, `hitHQ`, `explode`, `dynamite`, `overdraw`. The box was loaded (load average 6 to 10 from other packages' test runs) so these are ranking, not absolute numbers; rerun on a quiet box before spending effort on any single one.
- **Guardrail misses:** `fireball`, `meteor`, `meteorBurn` (and `spawn|bloom`, `poisonTick` less clearly) build their full particle effect at Effects None and with the tab hidden (about 40 nodes, 43 to 66 tweens each). Elite cards run an infinite `background-position` loop with `mix-blend-mode: soft-light` during fights (guardrail: decorative loops pause while a fight is live).
- No audio plays while the tab is hidden and none at SFX volume 0, for all 394 kinds (hidden pass: 0 audio nodes). No page errors in the three full passes.

## Method and limits

Scripts are in `tests/e2e/fxaud1_*`; data in `docs/perf/fxaud1/`. Chromium headless shell with the WebGL flags, 1366x860, fight board of `fx10_2026_11_13.py`.

1. `fxaud1_collect.js` ran about 3,450 engine boards (every fieldable card against two opponents, plus ENG50 scenarios) and kept one real event per (type, kind, field, element): 191 keys, `event-samples.json`. `fxaud1_synth.py` read every `{type:'x', ...}` literal in the engine for the other 203, `event-synth.json` (shape only: a synthetic event can miss a field its handler wants, so its "silent" is a flag, not a finding).
2. `fxaud1_probe.py` plays each through the game's own `renderVfxForEvent` and records the named cues (wrapping `SoundKit` and `SoundKit.prim`, which the effect modules build on), WebAudio nodes, DOM nodes, tweens, CPU (CDP `Performance.getMetrics`) and long tasks. `fxaud1_table.py` writes `coverage-table.tsv`.
3. `fxaud1_loud.py` plays each of the 190 core cues alone through a tap on the audio destination (music off) and measures peak and RMS. Effect-module cues (powder puff, Ignite, Snow ...) register later and are not in it.
4. `fxaud1_gates.py`, `fxaud1_gatecmp.py`: Effects None, tab hidden, SFX 0.

Limits: the box was shared (load 6 to 10), rAF stalled (frames seconds apart), so the probe pumps GSAP's ticker by hand and costs are CPU time, not frame time. Frame rate was not measurable here. Visual detection is "something added to the DOM or a tween started", so a card that only changes a number (Inspire's plate bounce) reads as "no visual" (false negative). Event-level silence misses a cue played from another event.

## 2. Quality

Reproductions run the real `renderVfxForEvent` on the fight board; use `python3 tests/e2e/fxaud1_gates.py` for 1 and 2, `fxaud1_leftover.py` for 3.

1. **Vulnerable hit plays the Shocked cue.** `dnModKey(['vulnerable'])` returns `weak`; `dnMarkSoundKey` then returns `m.includes('weak') ? 'weak' : 'shocked'`, and `['vulnerable']` has no `weak`, so `markShocked` plays (the 💢 glyph is right). Printed: `vuln: ["weak","shocked","markShocked"]`.
2. **A hit by a Feeble unit plays the armour ting.** `feeble` is in `DN_REDUCED` but not in `DN_MARK_REDUCED_ORDER`, so `find` returns undefined and the key falls to `'armor'`: `feeble: ["reduced","armor","markArmor"]`. A Feeble attacker sounds like it struck armour.
3. **Nothing stuck.** After 12 s, 11 events that looked like leaks (fortify, pierceHQ, volleyHit, grit, consternation, rain, wind, swipeLumber, boomerangThrow, diveLand) left only persistent UI (HP numbers, status badges, weather leaves, the hand strip). `fieldTick tideSpout` (synthetic shape) leaves `.tide-scene` and `unit-wet` on every card; a real tide ends with `fieldEnd`, not confirmed here.
4. **Weaken and Vulnerable share 💔** (float, log, badge): one looks like the other.
5. **Fireball, Meteor, Meteor burn, Spawn bloom ignore Effects None and a hidden tab** (section 3 and fix list).
6. **Text lags a rename and a trait:** log and floats say "Wave" for the awash field (`arena_app.js` 51009, 54561, 54629) while its card says Underwater Currents; Armourer's log line ends in 🐝 and prints "+0⚔". These log lines are hard-coded English (not TX keys) for `wave`, `awash`, `namedBuff`, `weaken`.
7. **Probe artefacts, not game bugs:** `irradiation` and `spell` threw on synthetic shapes (`ev.points` / `ev.targets` not arrays); real events from the engine did not throw. No page errors in the three full passes.
8. In a fight with nothing happening: the hidden Home tab's tip ticker (`#homeTipTicker`) swaps a line and the energy HUD re-renders every few seconds (seen as DOM additions in a standing fight). Small; a POLL1 follow-up.

## 3. Cost

Per event, 1.1 s window, three runs each at 1x and 4x CPU slowdown, medians: `docs/perf/fxaud1/cost-table.md`. CPU = script + layout + style. Idle window: 20 ms at 1x, 44 ms at 4x.

| Event | CPU 1x | CPU 4x | call 1x / 4x (ms) | worst long task at 4x |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pierceHQ | 81 | 752 | 7.5 / 77 | 244 |
| earthquake | 324 | 721 | 4.7 / 26 | 323 |
| hitHQ | 92 | 664 | 2.3 / 7 | 389 |
| mindControl | 88 | 629 | 8.6 / 43 | 181 |
| wave (awash tick) | 154 | 619 | 11.5 / 62 | 175 |
| meteor | 142 | 522 | 3.8 / 9.5 | 109 |
| explode / dynamite | 110 / 114 | 360 / 323 | 2.5 / 17, 3.6 / 25 | 345 / 360 |
| overdraw | 33 | 183 | 1.8 / 9 | 541 |
| namedBuff (Armourer) | 73 | 272 | **28.8** / 122 | 184 |
| evolve | 84 | 236 | **27.8** / 143 | 166 |
| death | 90 | 282 | 13.4 / 81 | 88 |
| devilry | 87 | 325 | 13.0 / 69 | 104 |
| statusFx rend (ENG50) | 40 | 401 | 3.0 / 31 | 111 |
| powder paralysis (ENG50) | 69 | 217 | 6.5 / 23 | 70 |

- Over budget at 1x: only the call time of `namedBuff` and `evolve` (above 16.7 ms). No long task over 50 ms in the 1x medians.
- At 4x the run-to-run spread is 2 to 3x (e.g. `pierceHQ` 361 / 752 / 846 ms), so the order above is indicative. `pierceHQ`, `hitHQ`, `earthquake`, `overdraw` have a low call time but a long task: the cost is in layout and style after the call (castle HP bar, hand redraw), not the effect code. Worth a trace on a quiet box.
- Always-on loops in a fight: `idleBreathe` (translate only, as the rules ask), `elitePolish` (`background-position` plus `mix-blend-mode: soft-light` on each Elite card, `arena_template.html` line 872, not paused in fights), `fx9GuardSheen` on Guard units (`will-change: transform`), `lavaBreath` on the map. `fxaud1_loops.py` measured the elite loop at 351 ms vs 146 ms CPU per 4 s at 1x, but the three runs spread 229 to 891 ms under load and 4x did not agree: **inconclusive**, recommend the rule-based fix (pause it while a fight is live) and a quiet-box remeasure.
- Frame time was not measurable here (rAF stalled on the shared box).

## 4. Sound

190 core cues measured alone, music off (`loudness.json`). Median peak -23.9 dBFS, median RMS -37.8.

- **Loudest by RMS:** `boom` -25.6, `earthquakeTone` -26.4, `burnCrackle` -27.2, **`rallyHorn` -27.5** (sustained 0.65 s), `siegeTone`, `leaderSlam`, `bossSting`, `tapeRip`, `forgeHit`. `hit` is -30.6. The horn plays on every Inspire burst, far more often than a boom.
- **Highest peaks (near clipping, -8 to -15 dBFS vs median -24):** `shellCrackTone` -8.3, `iceShatter` -9.3, `burnCrackle` -9.3, `igniteWhoosh` -11.5, `tapeRip`, `radSizzle`, `boom`, `earthquakeTone`, `burnAway`, `forgeStrike`, `markFragile`, `siegeTone`.
- **Quietest (peak -31 to -48):** `pawnStep` -47.5, `bowCreak` -42.9, `boomerangWhirr` -41.4, `featherFlutter`, `riffle`, `storyArrive`, `voiceTone`, `spitfire`, `skitterPatter`, `dragonGust`. ENG50 cues by RMS: `exposeTone` -41.6, `corrodeTone` -42.2, `sporePuff` -44.6, `splash` -44.9 (all 4 to 7 dB under median).
- **Longer than 2 s:** `wolfHowl` 2.5 s, `moonHowl` 2.4 s; both one-shots. **No cue loops.** Music and ambience loop by design, gate on hidden and volume 0, and the master fades to 0 when hidden (but their scheduler keeps running with the context alive; see fix list).
- **Hidden tab:** 0 audio nodes for all 394 events (UI27 gate holds, including effect-module cues and `rallyHorn`). **SFX volume 0:** 0 audio nodes for the 12 events tried (hit, death, rend, weaken, powder, wave, inspire, stun, earthquake, meteor, Armourer buff, fireball).
- **Effects None:** cues stay for gameplay events (rend, weaken, wave, inspire ...). Exception: the three powder cues (inside the puff).
- Music settings are three keys (`bw_musicVolume`, `bramblewood_battle_music`, `bramblewood_calm_music`); the first alone does not silence the fight music (found while measuring). Not a bug, but the volume slider and the fight music do not obviously map.
- `voiceTone`, `barkBabble`, `babble` need arguments; not measured (`barkBabble` and `babble` returned no audio when called bare).
