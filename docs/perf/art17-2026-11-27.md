# ART17-T: one-card living art feasibility (Abyss Lantern Squid), 2026-11-27

Standalone prototypes in `docs/perf/art17/` (no game file changed). Open `a-layered.html` and `b-flat.html` over http (`python3 -m http.server -d docs/perf/art17`); `?force` plays motion even with reduced motion on. Control page `c-control.html` = flat art + the same tilt loop.

## What I tried
- Source: `assets/cards/abyss-lantern-squid.webp` (256, RGB). Local matting only, no model needed: the squid is the only warm-coloured thing on dark blue water, so the key is hue/saturation (orange, `R > B + 6`) plus the pale fin tips and the cyan spots filled inside the body hull, then largest connected component (+ pieces within 10 px), closing and hole fill (`tools/matte.py`).
- Layers at 512 (nearest 2x, so the pixels stay square), `tools/build.py`: `bg` (squid removed, grown 6 px, Telea inpaint: dark water, reads clean even when the body is shifted 14 px), `body` (hard alpha trimmed 1 px, edge-ring colour pulled in from the core), `glow_lure` (lantern glass bloom, bg depth), `glow_body` (spots and eye highlight bloom, body depth). Glow layers are normal-blend bloom images pulsed with opacity: no blend mode, no filter.
- No eye glow exists in this art (the eye is a white dot with a pupil), so "eyes" became the cyan body spots plus the eye glint; a real eye-glow card needs painted eyes.

## Quality gate: conditional pass
Crops: `crops/edge-light-400px-3x.png`, `crops/edge-dark-400px-3x.png`, `crops/body-light-400px.png`, `crops/body-dark-400px.png` (body only on light / dark, 400 px, bilinear; edge crops at 3x).
- Dark background (its own water, the only place it is shown): clean, no halo; shifted-body check showed no holes. Pass.
- Light background (stress test): stepped pixel edge as the art itself is, a few dark leftover pixels in the shadow gap between the tentacles, and the grey fin membrane reads slightly "window-like". Not a fringe of the water colour, but not studio-clean. A hand touch-up of the mask would fix it; a generated clean split would be better.
- Verdict: passes for parallax of at most 6 px over its own bg. It would not survive being used as a sticker on another background. This card is the easy case (one strong hue against the scene); a creature whose colour matches its background (green frog in leaves) will fail this key and need generated layers.

Both under the 2 ms script per frame target; the plan's "no style pass per frame" is not met by A: its style cost is 5x the control (5 inline transforms written per frame plus CSS animations). Next step if shipped: drive the 4 layers from one CSS variable pair on the card, or cut the layer count by baking `gl` into `bg`.
- Memory is the estimate from the layer tree (sum of drawn layer areas x 4 B), 15 composited layers in both (the 3D context splits layers); 4 x 512x512 = 4 MB for the textures themselves. One card at a time.
- Disk per card: A adds 32 KB (bg 14, body 10, glow 3 + 4; the flat 11 KB stays for tiles). B adds 0 KB per card (reads the flat art; script and caustic tiles are shared).
- A caveat: headless Chromium runs software compositing; real GPUs will move the compositing cost off the CPU. Treat these as relative, not absolute.

## Recommendation
- Path: A (layered) for the cards where the matte is clean, B as the default for everything else. A is clearly the "living" result (depth plus glow); B is cheaper to author (zero art per card) but reads as an overlay on a still picture.
- Suits: single dominant-hue creature over a contrasting scene (this squid, fireflies, lanterns, eels, glow things). Fails: creature colour = background colour, fur against similar fur, translucent creatures. B suits any card with bright glow pixels (it keys cyan; each card would need its own hue key).
- Cost per A card: 4 layers, about 32 KB on demand, about 4 MB texture while open, about +0.65 ms a frame CPU (1x), 20 to 40 min of mask touch-up each if the key works; hours if layers must be generated (PixelLab is blocked).

## What it would take to ship
- Files: `bramblewood-fx-livingart.js` (new, loader + one rAF loop, tilt from `--ctTiltX/Y` and gyro), CSS block in `arena_template.html` (from `card.css`), per-card layer files `assets/cards/living/<id>-{bg,body,gl,gb}.webp`, a list of living ids in `canonical/cards.json` (field `living:true`), and the pack reveal / Detail hook in `arena_app.js`.
- Where it runs: Detail card first; pack reveal for rare and up second; hero plinth last. Never board or hand tiles. One card animating at a time; stop when `ui27Away()`, reduced motion shows still bg+body, Effects below `med` shows the flat art (`fxAtLeast('med')`).
- Gates: re-measure inside the real Detail card (extra layers, glare, existing tilt) at 4x before enabling; the control shows the tilt alone is already 110 ms/s at 4x.
