## Mechanism
`bwLoop(fn, ms, gate, idleMs)` in `arena_app.js`: runs while visible and the gate is open; hidden -> no timer; gate closed -> the loop joins ONE
shared 2 s look (not one timer per loop); `bwLoopWake()` on visibilitychange, #appWrap / body class change, a click or key (250 ms later), a tooltip showing.

## Finding: the fight ticks are timing-sensitive
Gating `autoplayStep`, `fightWatchdog` or `playAnimWatchdog` on a match made `tests/e5det-2026-11-16.js` fail (raid game path, same seed, different fights; in 3 of 3 runs
of the 30-fight setting, and under test-suite load with only playAnimWatchdog gated; the old build and the build with all three free-running pass). So the phase of those ticks reaches the fight. They stay as they
were (commented in the code). Next suspect: make the fight independent of tick phase; then all three can sleep outside a fight and while hidden (about 430 fires/min),
and the 150 ms battle music tick (about 400/min in a fight) can follow.
