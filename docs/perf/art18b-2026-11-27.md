# ART18b: cheap skin for Golden Eagle (static bird + speed lines), 2026-11-27

Prototypes in `docs/perf/art18b/` (no game file changed). Serve the repo root (`python3 -m http.server`) and open `/docs/perf/art18b/v1-radial.html`, `v2-wind.html`, `v3-smear.html`; `c-control.html` is the flat art. `?force` plays motion with reduced motion on. Rebuild layers: `python3 -I tools/build.py`; numbers: `tools/measure.py`; frames: `tools/frames.py`.

## Source and matte
`assets/cards/golden-eagle-diver.png` is 117x117, fully opaque (a scene, not a cutout); the card shows it at 400 px (358 on a 390 phone), non-integer 3.4x, `image-rendering: pixelated` like the rest. The eagle is the only warm thing on blue sky and white-blue cloud, so a hue key (`R > B + 8`, plus the dark brown shadow pixels), closing, largest component plus pieces within 3 px, hole fill gives a clean 1-bit cutout (`layers/bird.png`, 3557 px, 5.8 KB). Checked on magenta: no halo, no holes; the sky gap between the legs stays inside the cutout (invisible, the art is the same blue). That cutout lets lines run behind the bird. All layers are 117x117 pixel-grid PNGs (same grid as the art), tiny.

## Variants (all CSS animation: `opacity` / `translate`, no rAF, no filter, no blend mode, bird itself never moves)
- V1 radial: 3 pre-drawn sets of white streaks pointing at the eagle from the card edges, flickering in hard steps (~8 fps, comic burst) under the bird. Reads "zoom in".
- V2 wind: 45 degree streaks (1-2 art px wide) in a seamless tile drifting up-right in 3 px steps (the bird dives down-left); one layer behind the bird, a sparser faster one over it. Reads the most "fast flight / dive".
- V3 smear: four stepped pale silhouette copies trailing up-right behind the bird (two sets alternate at ~8 fps) plus a few short streaks drifting off. Subtle, the most "motion blur"; the smear reads slightly hazy on the cloud, and has the least line energy.
Reduced motion: V1 shows one fixed set, V2 the tile at rest, V3 the first smear set. All shown still.

## Recommendation
V2 wind as the Golden Eagle skin: strongest speed read, cheapest art (6.4 KB, one generated tile each), still cheap at 4x (0.7 ms a frame). V1 is the fallback for cards whose motion direction is toward the viewer, and the cheapest at 4x; V3 does not read as speed strongly enough alone, but its trail could be added to V2 for impact (pack reveal).

## Plug-in as a skin
- Data: per-card `skin:{id:"speed", dir:[1,-1], ...}` in `canonical/cards.json` (or `living`-style flag as ART17-T proposed), plus `assets/cards/skins/<id>-{bird,wind_back,wind_front}.png`. Tile and cutout come from `tools/build.py` (needs a clean hue key; the hue-matching-background cards need a generated mask).
- Code: the CSS block (`card.css`, ~25 lines) goes in `arena_template.html` with a dated comment; the bird cutout and two tiles are added as children of the Detail card art on open and removed on close. No rAF, no JS loop.
- Where: Detail card first, pack reveal (rare and up) second, hero plinth third. Board and hand tiles stay flat (no extra layers per tile).
- Gates: `fxAtLeast('med')` else flat art; `reducedMotion()` stills the lines (media query already does it); remove when `ui27Away()` / `document.hidden` (`animation-play-state: paused` via a body class from `syncPageFlags`), and never while `matchState` is live. Re-measure inside the real Detail card (glare, tilt) at 4x before enabling.
- The PixelLab animated eagle (another package) would replace this for the premium skin; this stays the cheap tier.
