# Ideas to consider (2026-10-14, your 21:06 and 21:31 notes)

Nothing here is built yet. It is a pool to draw from when we design new maps, factions and cards.

## Card ideas

- **Killer veggies with flowers:** flower-killer veggies that usually can't attack. They work through auras, thorns and traps (fits the Flora line, Maps 20+).
- **Bloom** for algae, fungal, coral and bacterial cards, not just flowers.
- **The pitch-black fish:** a 0/2 fish with no skills. In pitch black it deals 5 to a random enemy every round. At Level 12 it is 0/20 and deals 11.
- **Slimes:** one that splits when hit or on death, and one that eats (absorbs a weaker unit and grows).
- **Pesky Bee:** a green bee in the spirit of Minecraft's Creeper, which walks up and bursts.

## References to look at

Minecraft (Creeper), Trickster Online, MapleStory (a slime in a mushroom mask), Tyrant Unleashed, Dragon's Nest.

## Kinds of play we want, overall

- Abilities that grow with multiples of the same kind on the field.
- Zeitgeber (cards keyed to time, day and night, tides).
- Resource-based abilities, and channelling mana.
- Voltron: pile buffs onto one unit, with Trample.
- Stax: slow the opponent down with taxes and locks.
- Revivification.
- Mathematics (odd/even, counts, exact totals).
- Positioning like chess pieces.
- Go wide.
- Control.
- Reactive play, e.g. a card that makes all three opposite units susceptible when it's played.
- Elemental combos.
- Alternate win conditions.
- Area control: summon drones or golems onto the opponent's field that hurt their own castle.
- Life points as a resource.

## Strategy lines a deck can follow

- Build a field, dominate it, then damage the enemy Bramble.
- Stealth units that deal direct damage.
- Area damage that punishes over-commitment.
- Cards that give the enemy a special resource (with a catch).
- Damage equal to the Lumber the opponent holds.
- Sacrifice a unit to open a spot.
- A win decided elsewhere (a side track or counter).
- Deck-out.

## Names and themes

- Cool names carry cards (Mallard Marauder is the model).
- Themed sets: an Oriental painting theme, a Spy theme.

## Economy notes

- Keep the cost of switching decks and leaders low.
- Upgrade gain per level nerfed to 0.5.
- Disenchanting an upgraded card returns 97% of what went in.
