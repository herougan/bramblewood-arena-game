# REF5: the progression reference, "Reference (Random)" (2026-10-30 / 31)

Owner 10-28 22:50 (action items R1-R5) and 10-30: "a reference deck made from optimising standard draws + random booster pack openings appropriate for that map ... do this process a few times to generate a few different decks based on randomised pack rewards".

Data: `docs/balance/ref/ref5-prog.json` (the set: 5 decks a map, with per-card levels), `docs/balance/ref/ref5-prog-pools.json` (each seed's collection), `docs/balance/ref5-2026-10-30.json` (everything below). In the game: the PRO / X-REF "You play" picker and Admin's reference switch.

## 1. The economy (R1, R2)

One Otter player per seed (seeds 1-5), walking the road map by map. All amounts come from the game's own tables (read through `tools/lib_bramble.js`), never from older docs.

**Income counted.**
- First clears: Leaves and Dust. ECON1 does not halve them.
- Fight reward cards and first-clear pack cards.
- An elite or boss first clear's base-track card.
- Each map's end pack and its free pack (`MAP_FREE_PACKS`).
- The faction track (`rw5_faction_pacing` run, per seed).
- The level track, from XP: tutorials 100, 28 per first clear, 300 per map cleared, the collection milestones.
- The Trail Mix Trek's free lane, at a stated pace. Halved by ECON1. Pace: one map a day, 60 Stickers a day, plus 100 a week.
- Every pack opens through the game's own `rollPackCards`, seeded. Its free level-up (`levelChance`) and its bonus copy apply too.

**Spending.** All income is multiplied by 1.5.
- 1.0x of the Leaves (and Gold Leaves) buys packs at each map's arrival. It buys the map's own series first, then the next series down when that one is not on sale or not affordable. Merchants: the Pouch from Map 5, the Chest from Map 8, the Case from Map 13. Bundle discounts apply.
- 0.5x of each currency goes to the Forge.

**Left out:**
- the cards in faction packs from the level track and the Trail;
- the general card of a faction pack;
- achievement currency;
- every repeatable income (the extra 0.5x stands in for it).

**The Forge schedule (my choice):**
- Shut until Map 11. Its share is banked until then.
- From Map 11, each map buys the Forge tier first: the Iron Forge from Map 13, the Ember Forge from Map 16, if the bank pays for it.
- Then it levels the map's deck, one card level at a time, most deck copies per Dust first.
- Every level stays inside the tier's cap and rarity band, the level gates and the Base card Lv 4 cap.
- The deck is chosen first, at the levels the cards already have.

**What this means.**

- **The Pack 1 pool saturates by Map 6.** About 50 cards, mostly at their copy caps. From then on, buying more packs adds almost nothing. The seeds' collections differ only by a few Pack 2/3 cards from the end packs and the level track.

## 2. The decks (R3, R4)

**The search.** `deckopt.js --mode prog` searches each seed's collection: a card is capped at min(its rarity cap, the copies owned); Base and starter cards are unlimited. It plays your cards at their own levels, against deckopt's gauntlet at the Map reference's level, and seeds its search with the Map reference deck and the player's previous deck (both repaired into the collection).

**The budget, limited by machine time.** The machine was shared, at load 8-14 on 2 cores, and the container restarted once.
- Maps 0-6: 3,000 search games a seed. A gauntlet entry is one fight's deck in the fair mirror, or one road fight.
- Maps 7-20: 3,000 games with population 8, 1 game a gauntlet entry on the first rung and gmax 8. That is only 1-3 generations.
- The Pool reference used 16,000-32,000 games a map. **These decks are not converged**: they are good decks from each collection, not the best.

**How different the 5 decks are.** "Shared" is the mean number of copies two of the map's decks have in common, out of 20.

Maps 0-2 have nearly the same collection on every seed, so their decks differ only through the search. From Map 9 on, the collections are large (190-345 cards) and the short search lands in different places. That spread is mostly search noise, not pack luck. ## 4. In the game (R5)

**The "You play" picker** (X-REF panel and Admin's reference switch), in order:
1. **Reference (Random)**, the default.
2. Reference 1 of 5 ... Reference 5 of 5.
3. Map reference.
4. Reference-2.

**Reference (Random)** deals one of the map's five decks at random each time a fight starts. A toast says which, for example "You play Reference 3 of 5 ...". The REF tag and the result line use the same name.

**Other rules:**
- A side map plays the decks of the map it opens on.
- Each deck plays its own per-card levels.
- A choice saved before REF5 moves to Reference (Random) once.
- New texts: `ui.ref5-random`, `ui.ref5-deck`, `ui.ref5-dealt`, in 12 languages.

## 5. Decisions for the owner (docs/decisions.json REF5-1..4)

- **REF5-1:** No pack costs Dust. Should the unused 1.0x Dust (8,775 by Map 20) also go to the Forge? That would roughly triple the Forge's Dust and lift late levels toward the reference's.

- **REF5-3:** The Forge schedule (tier first, then most copies per Dust): keep it or change it.
- **REF5-4:** Re-run the 5 x 21 searches at the Pool reference's budget (16,000 a map, about 10 hours of machine time) before this set judges difficulty.
