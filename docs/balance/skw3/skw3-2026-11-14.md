# SKW3: re-fitting cap 1 to SKW2's shape, on paper (2026-11-14)

Study only (decision SKW2-1 b). No games played, nothing changed. Tool `tools/skw3/skw3.js` (about 3 s) uses a local copy of the cap left sides, checked card by card against `bramblewood-budget.js`. Data: `skw3-2026-11-14.json` (per-card gaps, moves, fits). Live = measured, in play, has Health: 472 cards, 283 of them first listed on Maps 0-20. Over = a cap broken at the start level or at Level 1.

## 1. Where cap 1 binds today
- **Cap 1 is the binding cap on all 472 live cards.** Cap 2's use, read in stat scale as sqrt(x2/N2), peaks at 0.95 and is at most 0.82 of the card's cap-1 use. Cap 2 limits no live card today.
- Maps 0-20 (283): mean use 0.873. Gap x1 - N: median -2.3, p10 -6.6, p90 +0.2, range -20.0 to +3.8. 153 cards sit more than 10% under N.
- **31 are over** (Starter/Common 19, Uncommon 8, Rare/SR 2, VR+ 2). The worst: cricket-drummer +3.8, alley-cat +3.3*, ironclaw-fiddler +3.0, worker-ant +2.8*, cypress-root-lurker +2.7*, armadillo-guard +2.6, raccoon-nightcrew +2.3, caldera-wyrm +2.3* (on purpose). The other 23 are +0.02 to +2.1. 11 of the 31 are owner-set (*), so they are flags, not refits.

N, the cost and Wait side, still ranks cards against their value: per-copy value falls as N rises (rank agreement -0.15 / -0.25).

**Recommendation:** drop the degree-2 form. Take "cap 2 alone, cap2Mult x 0.34" to the owner as SKW2-1 a. It is the only candidate that ranks cards better than today on both contribution runs, but it puts 43 Maps 0-20 cards over. geo is the gentler fallback (22 over), with a clear gain at bramble 15 only. Before any change, run a paired road check of the cards each form would move.
