# Special monsters against the stat budget (package RJ, 2026-10-19)

The owner (15:48): *"Justify special monster stats like Raid monster stats. What would that card's BR be then?"* An analysis: no stats change here. Regenerate with `node tools/special_monsters.js` (it also writes `canonical/special-monsters.json`, which the hub's Units tab reads).

## Findings

- **The elders are near their budget, and the raid's BR weighs them more than their stats do.** A fresh Kraken Brute fights at 3/50 (from its ~raid copy: +1 Attack, Health up to the raid's 50 ceiling): 1.05× an Epic's N, a Level 3 Epic with Wait 2, Lumber 3; as small as a Lv 6 Common (BR 2.6), BR 17.8 as a player card. On the boss side each one counts 45.8 (an Epic × (1 + bonus / basic Health)), 5 a fight = 229 of the Left Tentacle's 401. Both fall into band as the raid falls (the Health ceiling and the weakening).
- **The boss's row-deck copies are tiny next to their cards, but the BR counts their printed rarity.** At 20% Attack / 15% Health every copy is in band at its strongest point; 18 of 18 fit a Level 1 Common. The Goliath Broodmother fights at 5/4 (a Level 1 Common's size, BR 1) and still adds 21 a copy (a Legendary) to the Core's BR. The raid's BR is a count of rarity × 60 cards (3 rows × 20), not of strength; that is the BR package's rule ("no fight's BR counts stats"), so it reads high against what the cards do.
- **The brambles are the boss.** Each arm and the tail is a 420-Health bramble a fight (2.8× the raiders' merged 150), the Core 200 behind its stumps; as a 0-Attack wall card it exceeds any player budget by 3.12× (9.4× a Lv 11 Legendary with Wait 0, Lumber 0). It is allowed because the part is 200,000 raid Health shared by an instance of 250, and no BR counts a bramble.
- **The Core's stumps** fight at 5/11 on a fresh Core (Thrashing's +4 Attack, then the weakening): 1.01× a Common's N, a Level 2 Common. Their job is the shield, so a little over is fine.
- **The Commons are not called in today** (tuning.reinforce.max 0); at 20% / 15% they would be 1-Attack Commons.
- **Async raid bosses** (raid-bosses.json and the road's 11-R / 20-R) are brambles of 100–350 Health (1.85–6.48× the biggest player bramble, 54) behind decks of base-level player cards (BR 20–68): every card is a player card; only the bramble exceeds a player's.
- **Conquest, Tower and dungeon specials are player cards at the level they fight at**, nearly all in band. The Tower's Legendary heads are in band for their Level 13–20 except The Caldera Wyrm (flagged). The ones over their N are mostly player cards over their own budget as printed (listed after the flags: the card pass's, not this doc's).
- **Flagged: The Caldera Wyrm.** See Flagged below.
- **canonical/unit-themes.json's nonPlayer bodies are stale** for Kraken Brute and Orca Warlord and Broodmother Ascendant: it lists ⚔10 ❤87 fresh (the printed card × 3), while the fight builds 3/50 (the ~raid copy at 20% / 15%, then the elder rule). tools/unit_themes.js should read the built fight (left as is here: another package owns it).

## How each number is worked out

- **Stats** are the ones the game builds for the fight, not the printed card: the raid's from `buildRaidFullFight` (the boss copies at 20% Attack / 15% Health, then the stage's +Attack, then the weakening 0.6 + 0.4 × share; the elders' Health from 50 down to basic with the raid's share; the stumps' 14 Health), the road's from `levelledEnemyDeck` at the level each card fights at, the Tower's from `towerFloorDeck` + `statsBeyondLevel`.
- **Score**: 2A+H+S and A·H+S, ×1.5 per half-miss skill (`SkillCosts.budget`, `canonical/skill-costs.json`). Skills with no entry in the table cost 1 + ⌊N/2⌋ (the old default).
- **N needed**: max(2A+H+S, 3·√(A·H+S)); a card is in band when its N is above both.
- **N**: `bramblewood-budget.js` with the table's budget numbers: N = (Base + Level/10) × (1 + Level/30), Level 0: Base − 1; Base = 20 + Rarity + 6×Wait + 0.5×Wait² + 4×Lumber + 0.4×Lumber² + Ritual + 0.2×Map (v5). The **v6** column is the formula in progress: (Base + L/10) × (1 + L/30), Base + 0.2 × Map (the helper's Map term). Map: the map it is met on (the raid: Map 13, where the Raid Banner opens).
- **Equivalent player card**: its own Wait and Lumber; its own rarity, then each rarity above it up to Legendary, at Levels from its start level to 11. When none holds it: "exceeds any player budget by Y×", Y = N needed / the biggest N any player card has at Level 11 (Glacial Ape-King: 134.62, v6 140.08); the second number is against a Level 11 Legendary with its own Wait and Lumber, and the Level is where the Legendary's N line (past 11, as the Tower's levels go) would hold it.
- **BR as player**: the battle rating that equivalent card adds in a player deck (rarity weight × level factor; past Level 11 on the Tower's straight line). **BR as boss**: what one copy adds on the boss side now, and the card's share in all: the raid's `raidBossBR` (each row a 20-card deck, set-up cards 2×, the elders as Epics × (1 + Health bonus / Health), Commons), the road's `enemyDeckLevel` (scaled to 20, leaders 2×), the Tower's `towerDeckBR`. No BR counts stats or brambles, so the raid's 20% / 15% stats and the weakening change nothing in the boss's BR.
- ✓ = in band at the level it fights at; otherwise its N needed ÷ its N. ⚑ = flagged: over its budget with no mechanic to account for it (a proposal follows the tables).
