## What moved it

BAL2 itself moved the road little: 29 -> 31 fights green of 179. The enemy level schedule makes Maps 15-19's elites and bosses stronger (Lv 4 copies arrive on their map's hardest fights), the DF-1 re-fit lifted many cards a point (both sides), and Base cards stop at Lv 4 (the reference deck's Base cards never pass 4.62 by Map 24, so this bites little yet). The wide gap is older: Gannet Cliffs, Gatorback Swamp, the Long Grass, the Glass Dunes and the Goldfeather Siege are 94-100% on nearly every fight, Pebble Beach, Sunken Hollow, the Morel Hollow and Eyrie Heights are well under. Owner call BAL2-3.

## The Pale Desert (T21-2)

`node tools/skirmish_pass.js measure 100 --only=mpd --tag=bal2-pd-2026-10-24`: every fight of the Pale Desert wins 94-100% (the commit before BAL2: 18-A-5 98%). Since it opens after Map 19 it counts as Map 19 for your reference deck too, so it lost the "a map ahead" step it was tuned with (the T21-2 note's band 61-77% was that step). 18-A-5 Vulture Road is retuned: 3 Trapdoor Spiders (one leads) and 2 Rabid Jackals for the Sand Widow, a Cinder Gecko, a Roadrunner and 2 Dust Hyenas, and a Shield of 60: 82.5% over 400 games (band 65-81%; the old deck 96%). Its bramble barely matters (Shield 20 -> 40: 1 point); cheap, early hitters do. The rest of the map wants the same pass (BAL2-3).
