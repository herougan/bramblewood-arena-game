# Skill worth in the halved world, every card re-derived, 2026-10-21 (package BF6)

Package BF6. The owner's rulings, in the order they came:
- **B11:** "a blanket halve [of skill numbers] might not balance it appropriately. I assume the N value takes into account the Map value." (It does: + 0.1 × Map now.) **B12:** "Okay whichever."
- **Injured N** (new): "comes in with damage already on it (can be healed). This counts as −N × 0.99." "Measure the worth of new skills. Remember that skills can be negative."
- **15:09, the halved N** (replacing BF5's 0.5 on the whole N): "all coefficients in calculating our caps are halved. So Base=10+Rarity*1+3*Wait... (I kept rarity at *1 not *0.5) and N=(Base + Level/15)x(1+Level/30). Base at lvl 0 is still Base(0) - 1."
- **15:15:** cap 2 is "AxH+SxS instead, so A and H are penalised for being too equal, and S is penalised at more equal weight to A and H" (S is the card's total; S·|S| so a drawback still frees room); "Rally: temporary increase in attack. War Banner: passive increase in attack while this unit is present."; "Shield call -> Armor".
- **15:21:** "Spawning Algo: Not always spawn left or right. Spawn closest slot to the spawner! 50% if tie."
- **15:22, the method:** "Simulations not needed right? ... Just go to each card, and toggle all the values as equally as possible. Then those with too many skills are put into a bucket, then the agent will find if removal is easily done without thematic loss, then removing them then balancing those few."

## The budget now

- **N(L) = (Base + L/15) × (1 + L/30)**, Level 0 **Base − 1**; **Base = 10 + Rarity + 3×Wait + 0.25×Wait² + 2×Lumber + 0.2×Lumber² + 0.5×Ritual + 0.1×Map**; scale 1 (kept as a parameter); BF5's multipliers stay (no abilities ×1.05, light ×1.02, Fragile ×0.75). `bramblewood-budget.js` DEFAULTS, the `budget` line of `canonical/skill-costs.json`, the Test Lab and the card editor's formula line.
- **A Common, Wait 0, Lumber 0, Map 0:** Lv 0 / 1 / 10 was 10 / 10.9 / 14.67 (BF5), now **10 / 11.44 / 15.56** (before its multiplier; a vanilla card ×1.05: 10.5 / 12.01 / 16.34). Rarity weighs twice what it did under BF5: a Rare W1 L1 at Map 10 is 18.6 → 20.17 at Lv 1, a Legendary 21.7 → 26.37.
- **Cap 1:** 2A + H + S ≤ N. **Cap 2:** A·H + S·|S| ≤ (N/3)² (was A·H + S), S the card's total skill worth, both ×1.5 per half-miss skill. `productScore` in the helper; `tools/skill_costs.js`, the Test Lab, the card editor and the hub's Budget & stats and Skill costs tabs.
- **Cap 2 is the binding rule now.** A Common's (N/3)² is about 16, so a skill set worth S 3.5 leaves A·H about 4, and S 4 or more leaves none: that is what fills the bucket below.

## Injured N

- **Name:** Injured (the owner's working name; it fits house style beside Fragile, Drunk and Purblind). Key `injured`, TX `ui.skn-injured` and `ui.sk-injured` ("Comes into play with {n} damage already on it (at least 1 Health left). It can be healed."), in the 12 packs (`tools/i18n_2026_10_21_bf6.py`).
- **Rule** (`bramblewood-engine.js`, makeBoardCard): every unit made with it enters with Health max − N, never below 1; its max stays, so heals bring it back. Played, spawned and revived copies alike; arrival bonuses after it add to both.
- **Worth:** the owner's −0.99 a point (owner-set). Measured in the halved world: −1.29 at N 1, −1.93 at N 2, −3.39 at N 4 on 2/7 (−0.88 a point, high confidence), so the owner's number is about right. A drawback: S is negative, it lowers cap 1's score and, through S·|S|, frees cap 2's room too.
- In the card editor's "+ Add Skill" list and the Codex skill book (PASSIVE_DEFS, before SKILL_DEFS is built); icon 🩹. No card has it yet.

## Rally, War Banner, Shield Call, spawns (owner 15:15, 15:21)

- **Rally N** is temporary now: when it enters play, each of your units on the field (itself too) gains +N Attack until the end of the next round (`rallyUp`, Shell's timing). Back in the editor; TX `ui.skn-rally` / `ui.sk-rally`, icon 📣. Worth **3.13** a base (half scale; high).
- **War Banner N** is the passive Attack aura: your other units have **+N Attack** while it lives (was +N/+N; Herald keeps +N/+N). Worth **5.04** a point (was the estimate 8). The **Parish Sergeant**'s Rally 1 is War Banner 1.
- **Shield Call → Armor:** the Shieldbearer Hound has Armor 1 instead; the Magnate Duck loses Shield Call and keeps his Armor 3 (no room for more). Shield Call is retired (hidden) in the registry; the engine's rule stays so an old save or a server override with it still works.
- **Spawns:** every unit an ability puts on an open board's front row (Spawn, Spawner, Brood, Twin, Sprout, Duplicate, a death's tokens, On Attacked spawns, Mirage, a Relic) goes to the free slot nearest the spawner, a tie a coin flip on the fight's seeded RNG (`nearestFreeSlot`); a raid's middle / back rows keep the same rule within their row. Spawn re-measured after it: 4.08 a copy.

## How the worth was measured

- **What was simulated:** only the entries with no measured worth yet or whose rule changed (owner 15:22): Injured, Rally, War Banner, Brood / Twin / Sprout / Spawn after the nearest-slot rule, SK3's actions and keywords, EQ's skills, On Enter Slot and On Any Death. 165 runs, 30 games a point, 58,320 games, 20 minutes, one job (`docs/balance/skill-worth-2026-10-21.json`, checkpoint `skill-worth-bf6-new.jsonl`).
- **The halved world** (`tools/skill_worth.js --world=half`): the baselines' stats halved (1/4 W1, 2/7 W2, 3/11 W3) and both brambles 50 (the engine's 100 at BRAMBLE_SCALE 0.5). Every other entry keeps its 2026-10-19 (full-size) measure: the owner's method skips them. A full re-measure was started first and stopped at 387 of 1,477 runs when the method came (kept, not applied: `docs/balance/skill-worth-bf6-partial.jsonl`).
- **Not re-measured, as the method says:** Meteor (20 → S 11), Fireball (15), Trap, Pounce, Sweep, Armor, Quick (7.55), Rabid keep their 10-19 worths; under cap 2 they are what sends most cards to the bucket (Quick alone 7.55 → 57 against a Common's 16).
- **Calls on loose measures:** War Banner (low, but its three baselines and N 2 agree) and Spawn (the halved 1/1 bee: 4.08 a copy, was 5.26) are applied; Brood and Sprout re-measured within noise of their values, kept; Twin is a full copy of the card, off the scale (S 20 on b2, 36 on b3), kept at 1 and its two cards held; Silence, Exhaust, Weapon Master, Pry, the hazards, Amplifier and Broodkeeper read 0 because the plain decks have nothing for them to work on, so their estimates stay.
