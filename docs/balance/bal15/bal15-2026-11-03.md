## 2. Royal Guard's "On Play: +3 Shield to every ally", as its own skill item

Pipeline ad hoc trigger item `{"on":"onSpawn","do":"grantShield","allies":3}`, at bramble 15, on the canon's vanilla carriers. Worth **6.08 [5.28, 7.25] stat points**, converged at 400 paired fights. It had been priced at 10.21 (1.2 a point x 3 x On Spawn 1.05 x the On Spawn > Grant Shield pair's x2.7).

- `canonical/skill-costs.json` action `grantShield.allies`: 1.2 -> **0.72** a point (= 6.08 / (3 x 1.05 x 2.7)).
- The pair stays x2.7. The price tool now prices a Grant Shield field (adjacent / surrounding / allies) from its own trigger item and leaves that item out of the pair (`ownFieldOf` in `tools/skill2_apply_2026_10_31.js`). Pooled into the pair, the item would have pulled the pair down to about x0.6, and with it the price of Grant Shield on itself.
- Royal Guard 3/6: S 10.21 -> 6.12, cap-1 use 0.98 -> **0.82**, still in band. No re-fit. It is on no road deck (a faction-pack card).
