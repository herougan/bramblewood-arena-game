## 1. The budget at 3A + H (X16, RECOST1-6)

`bramblewood-budget.js` (the one copy the game, the tools and the hub use):

- cap 1: `f x ((3A + H) x m + S) <= N` (`attackWeight` 3 in DEFAULTS, was the literal 2). The same weight prices stats inside braces (context skills, the threshold skills, Bloom's copy) and the time-of-day bonus (`zeitS`), and the Forge curve's bank (a level's gain spends 3 points per Attack).
- **N(L) = 1.13 x (Base + L/15) x (1 + L/30)**, Level 0 `1.13 x (Base - 1)`; Base and its coefficients are unchanged (`scale` 1.13).
- cap 2: `A·H x m + S·|S| <= 1.57 x (N/3)²` (cap2Mult 2 -> 1.57 = 2 / 1.13²): cap 2 has no Attack weight, so its limit stays where it was in absolute terms.
- Skill prices stay in Health points (see BUDGET2-1 below for what the measure says about that).

**How N was fitted.** Population: the 330 in-play cards with stats, each at its start level for its first map (`tools/skill_costs.js budget`). Under 2A + H their median cap-1 use (left side / N) was 0.906. Under 3A + H with N unchanged, 190 of them were over. Fits tried (scratch runs, summarised):

| N form | over | well under (<=60%) | median use | spread (sd of log use) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2A + H (before) | 1 | 0 | 0.906 | 0.115 |
| 3A + H, N x 1.13 (chosen) | 38 | 1 | 0.905 | 0.116 |
| 3A + H, N x 1.135 | 32 | 1 | 0.900 | 0.116 |
| 3A + H, base 12 and every coefficient x 1.05 (near the least-squares line N_new = 1.049 N_old + 1.69 over the 252 cards at 80-100% use) | 37 | 1 | 0.901 | 0.117 |
| 3A + H, base 12.5 (Base + 2.5) | 34 | 3 | 0.899 | 0.120 |

The spread of use is the same within 1-4% for every form, so the one-number form that keeps the owner's coefficients won; c = 1.13 is the factor that keeps the median use where it was. The ratio (3A + H + S) / (2A + H + S) falls slowly with N (1.17 at N 10-15, 1.10 at N 30+): small cards carry more of their points in Attack, which is why the trims fall mostly on small, Attack-heavy cards.

## 2. The re-fit (tools/budget2_refit_2026_10_31.js, tools/budget2_apply_2026_10_31.py)

- **Which cards:** every card the budget measures that the new formula pushed out of tolerance: inside under 2A + H, outside under 3A + H (over: cap 1 or cap 2 broken at the start level or at Level 1, or at Level 0 for the seven T-1 / T-2 cards, SKL0-1; well under: cap 1 at or below 60% of N). 152 cards were outside already under 2A + H (eggs and equipment read "well under" by design; bee-drone and worker-ant held by RECOST1-5; ...): left as they were. The five Dragons and the Caldera Wyrm stay owner calls (Black Dragon 10/10 is 109% of N, not trimmed).
- **The fit:** the smallest change by relative size |ΔA|/A + |ΔH|/H (a 4/10 loses 2 Health before 1 Attack: its shape is kept), then the fewest points. An over card only loses, an under card only gains; a card with Attack keeps at least 1, a 0-Attack wall stays 0. A cut of a point of Attack frees 3 points, more than most cards were over by, so it gives Health back while the card stays inside and at or below its old use (the formula turning Attack into Health at 3 for 1): Honey Bee 2/3 -> 1/4, not 1/3.
- Mean use of the in-play trims: 0.967 before (2A + H), 0.950 after (3A + H). Each changed card's creatorNote carries a dated "BUDGET2 re-fit" line.
- The post-balance queue (135 -> 120 cards) and LISTFIX1's pending list (now empty) are rebuilt.

## 4. The sims' biome tables and Anadromous (ANAD-1)

`tools/lib_bramble.js` evaluates `fightRules` from `arena_app.js` but did not load `EN6_HOT_BIOMES`, `EN6_COLD_BIOMES`, `EN6_SALT_BIOMES`, `EN6_WEATHER_BIOMES`; `fightRules` reads them inside a try, so the error was swallowed and no sim fight ever had `hotMap`, `coldMap`, `saltMap` or `weatherMap`. Of 417 fights: 0 of each before, now 105 hot, 77 cold, 26 salt, 24 weather. Every tool built on lib_bramble (deckopt, the bench, the road measures) now plays Heat / Frost / Salt / Drought {…}, Hibernate and Anadromous' sea half as the game does. Earlier measures of those skills (EN6's uptimes were measured with the game's own rules, not this lib) are unaffected; bench and deckopt runs before today under-read them.

Anadromous under 3A + H: +1 Attack at sea is 3 points, +2 Health elsewhere 2. Salt-water share of the fights: 26 / 417 = 0.062 (Pebble Beach, Map 2, 5/5; the Kelp Mangroves, Map 9, 7/7; Coral Current, Map 10, 9/9, and its side map the Sunken Depths 5/5). Price 3 x 0.062 + 2 x 0.938 = 2.06, set at 2.1. Measured (canon tool, 200 paired fights on 6 vanilla carriers, global fields): worth 2.31 [1.88, 2.93] by the stat yardstick, consistent with 2.06. On a sea map's own list it would be worth up to 3.

## 5. The canon skill set (X14)

**Anchors** (`canonical/skill-costs.json`, `canon: true`, `canonAt`, `canonReason`): Armor (1, 2), Poison (1, 2), Bleed (1, 2), Regen 2, Pounce 2, Quick, Sweep 2, Fork, Rage, Inspiring 1, Gold on Ready 1, Shell 2: 12 skills, 15 points, prices 0.94-4.87. Chosen: high-confidence measured prices equal to the table value, on 8+ cards, plain mechanics with no field, partner or map condition, one or two per family (defence, status, healing, timing, reach, area, aura, economy).

**Tool:** `node tools/skill_canon_2026_10_31.js measure --skill <id>[=N]` (or effects JSON; `check`, `anchors`; procedure in `docs/evaluation-protocol.md` section 5). Six vanilla carriers (a Common and an Uncommon / Rare in each of three shapes); each plays as printed, with each anchor, with the test skill, +1 Health, +1 Attack, on the same 200 fights (the bench's paired design and cache). The test's worth is read two ways: the median rate (gain / price over the anchors) and the ruler (the anchors' prices made monotone in gain, the test's gain read between its neighbours). Intervals by a bootstrap over fights.

## 6. The held decisions, answered with evidence

- **DECK1-1** (cards picked almost everywhere). Contribution after the re-fit (`bench.js contrib`, 200 fights, 6 copies, global context; against a fair line fitted over a seeded sample of 40 in-play cards + these 5, `bench-contrib-global-2026-10-31-budget2-sample.json`): Eagle-Owl Quartermaster +5.2 per copy over the line (z 8.4), Ashbone Ghoul +4.7 (7.8), Wolf Spider Mother +3.7 (6.1; RECOST1's run +5.5, z 9.2, before its 3/10 -> 3/9 trim), Grave-Grin Hyena +3.2 (5.2), Leafcutter Gardener +0.1 (0.2). All four of the first are inside their budget: their strength is in under-priced skills (Brood, Fletcher, Remember's trigger, Executioner; the same-card synergies RECOST1-4 flagged). Recommendation: re-price those four skills with the canon tool, then re-fit the four cards; leave the Gardener.
- **DECK1-5** (Gannet Cliffs, Map 3, no hard deck). Its 13 list cards in the same contribution run (`bench-contrib-global-2026-10-31-budget2-map3.json`): 4 sit above the fair line (Conch Crawler +1.2, Gannet Nestweaver +1.0, Tomcat +0.6, Kittiwake Flock +0.4; none significant), 8 below, 5 of them clearly (z below -2.5: Peak Condor -2.3, Hawk Arrowkeeper -2.2, Red-billed Chough -2.2, Peregrine Stooper -1.9, Conch Herald -1.5). So the answer to "are the cards strong enough": no, most of the map's flyers are weak for their budget. Recommendation: lift those five (+1-2 Health or +1 Attack each, within budget) in a card-data package, then re-run DECK1's Map 3 search; the extra 2-Lumber card is the second step if the ceiling stays low.
- **SKL0-3** (Bee Knight in the T-1 / T-2 decks). BUDGET2 changed the tutorial cards: your T-1 / T-2 decks lose Attack (Pond Trout 3/4 -> 2/5, Cottontail 4/2 -> 2/5, Pond Duck 3/4 -> 3/3, Acorn Chipmunk 3/4 -> 3/3), the rival's Earthworm gains (0/6 -> 0/7), its Meadow Rabbit and Pond Duck lose. Bee Knight itself is unchanged (1/1, no skill at Level 0). The tutorial's win rates (TUT2: T-1 93% / 100%, T-2 76% / 95%) were not re-measured here: the tool TUT2 used is not in the repo. Recommendation: one tutorial package re-measures T-1 / T-2, retunes the decks, and adds one Bee Knight to your T-2 deck in the same proposed SQL (needs your "apply it").
