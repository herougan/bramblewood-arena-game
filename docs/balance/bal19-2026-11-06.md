Road fight data only: decks, leaders and the enemy Shield. No card stat, skill, list, reward, bramble Health or bramble ability changed (owner 11-04 22:34 "Don't lower bramble health anymore"; BAL17-1 b: the owner re-tunes bramble abilities). Decision BAL19-1 records the levers.

## Method
- Before: CONTENT12's road measure (`docs/balance/skirmish-pass-content12-road.jsonl`, copied as `skirmish-pass-bal19-before.jsonl`; the same game, so not re-played). Three fights re-measured on this branch read the same.
- 1. `skirmish_pass.js weak 40 --gap=20` and `curve 50 --decks-only --keep-leaders --keep-first --keep-size --pool --distinct --strict` on the named fights and the six worst maps (`docs/balance/bal19-weak.jsonl`, `bal19-curve.jsonl`).
- 2. `tools/bal19/road_tune_2026_11_06.js`: CONTENT12's local search for any fight plus two levers, a leader (another list card, or none on a skirmish, whose leader is hidden; namesake leaders kept; no leader added where there was none) and the Shield (an override from 0 up to the rule). A step screens every move at 20 games (tune seeds 7300 + 37g), re-reads the best 4 at 60 or 120, keeps one that closes the gap by 2+. Rounds: `docs/balance/bal19/tune`, `tune-r2` (still off after round 1), `tune-r3` (other fights still beyond 20), `tune-k14` (six fights re-run from the original, see below).
- 3. `tools/bal19/confirm_2026_11_06.js`: every changed fight against the fight as it was (HEAD), 200 games on the road seeds (9100 + 37g); kept only if 2+ points nearer the target. 14-4 failed (31 -> 32) and was put back. Files `docs/balance/bal19/confirm-2026-11-06-0.json` / `-1.json`.
- 4. The whole road again (`measure 40 --maxmap=20`, `skirmish-pass-bal19-final.jsonl`; totals `docs/balance/bal19/road-totals-2026-11-06.json`).

The rules each pick keeps: the fight's own list (`fightPool`), the road's rarity cap and theme, no other fight's reward card, size and first-clear cards kept, copy caps, a challenge's selfish boon on, the Pale Desert's bramble by rule, and K14 no worse per card. K14 per card was tightened mid-package: `curve --strict` counts violations only (19-A-1 / 19-A-10 Beaver Lumberjack went x3 -> x4), and a skirmish's hidden leader is its deck's showcase, so a deck move or a leader swap could take a card's leader exemption away (19-A-3, 19-A-7, 19-A-9, 5-1). Those six and 10-A-2 (its Rising Tide boon went off) were re-run from the original decks. `tests/bal19-2026-11-06.js` checks every rule; `bal7_k14_decks check` lists the same 6 K14 fights as on the batch head.
