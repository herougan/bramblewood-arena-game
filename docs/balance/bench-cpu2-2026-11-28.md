# BENCH-CPU2 (2026-11-28): the turn planner at the Basic CPU (decision CPU2-1)

Question: how much stronger is the road CPU on Maps 0-20 if the Basic level (aiLevel 0) also runs the turn planner (`aiPlanPlay`, fix #1 of `docs/design/cpu2-2026-11-28.md`)? No game code was changed. `tools/bench_cpu2/bench.js` patches the `planOn` line only inside its own process. Each game is played twice on the same seed (`playReferenceGame`, seeds 9100 + 37g): once with the baseline CPU, once with the planner. The Basic CPU keeps its noise.

## Method
- 223 fights on Maps 0-20 (the `skirmish_pass --maxmap=20` list). 82 already use the Smart CPU (elites and bosses from Map 5) and were skipped, since the switch does not touch them. 141 Basic fights remain, 131 of them on the curve.
- Screen: 100 paired games per fight (games 0-99). Follow-up: the 19 fights beyond 2 SE got 400 more paired games on fresh seeds (games 100-499), which replace their screen numbers. 19,800 paired games in all.
- Paired SE = SD of the per-game differences / √N. Band = `skirmishTargetBand` (± 8), with challenge fights 10 points lower, as in `skirmish_pass`.

## Results (player win rate, planner minus baseline)
- Mean over the 141 Basic fights: **-2.9 points ± 0.4 SE** (the CPU is about 3 points stronger). Mean over all 223 fights: -1.8. 42 fights got easier, 91 harder, 8 did not change. The median per-fight SE at 100 games is 4.7.
- Beyond 2 SE: 12 fights after the follow-up, every one harder for the player.
- Curve fights in band (Basic 131): 107 → 84. 30 leave the band and 7 enter it. Only 9 of the leaves are beyond 2 SE: 1-2, 1-3, 1-4, 8-5, 1-A-2, 14-A-1/2/3 and 18-A-1. No entry is beyond 2 SE, so the other crossings are mostly 100-game noise. Within 10 of target: 115 → 97. Beyond 20: 5 → 9.
- Map 1 takes most of it: 1-2, 1-3 and 1-4 drop 28-32 points. Without Map 1 and 1-A, the mean is -2.1.

## Reading
- The effect is smaller than the design's guess of 3-6 points and lands unevenly. It is large on a few early fights (Map 1's skirmishes and the 1-A, 14-A and 18-A side maps) and small elsewhere. Adopting CPU2-1 a) would need a road retune, mostly on Map 1, before it ships.
