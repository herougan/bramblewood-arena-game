## 1. The anchors, re-set from one shared measure (BUDGET2-2)

**Currency (decided here):** one skill-price point = one 3A + H stat point, the worth of +1 Health on the canon's six vanilla carriers (house-cat, bramblewood-lynx, constrictor-coil, badger-berserker, rivergate-recruit, tusked-vanguard).

**Measure:** `tools/skill_canon_2026_10_31.js check` at 1,000 paired fights a variant (108 variants, about 108,000 games). The stat yardstick is 3.59 [3.21, 3.96] win-rate points a point.

Before the re-set, κ was 0.50 [0.46, 0.55] and 14 of the 15 anchors sat outside the range the others gave them.

**The placebo:** adding a key the engine does not know gains 1.5 win-rate points, or 0.41 [0.21, 0.60] stat points. The reason is that the CPU's `cardValue` adds 1.5 for each effect key, so it pitches a card with a skill later. That gain is noise: every reading now subtracts it, once for each key the variant adds. The canon tool 1.1.0 does this itself; `--no-placebo` turns it off.

Each anchor's price = (its gain - the placebo's gain) / the yardstick. After the re-set, κ = 1.00 [0.92, 1.09] and all 15 anchors sit inside their range.

| Anchor | Before | SKILL2 (95%) |
|---|---|---|
| Armor 1 / 2 | 1.68 / 4.53 | 2.65 [2.39, 2.96] / 4.23 [3.83, 4.67]: 2.65 x N^0.675 |
| Bleed 1 / 2 | 2.05 / 3.11 | 2.47 / 4.51: 2.47 x N^0.87 |
| Poison 1 / 2 | 3.00 / 4.87 | 2.27 / 4.35: 2.27 x N^0.94 |
| Fork | 1.27 | 4.92 |
| Inspiring 1 | 2.30 | 3.13 |
| Gold on Ready 1 | 2.31 | 5.21 |
| Pounce 2 | 1.52 | 2.65 |
| Quick | 1.12 | 2.02 |
| Rage | 0.99 | 3.50 |
| Regen 2 | 0.96 | 3.45 |
| Shell 2 | 0.94 | 2.72 |
| Sweep 2 | 1.10 | 4.82 |

The cheap area, reach, economy and swing skills were priced at a quarter to a half of their worth. Poison and Armor 2 sat about right. For entries with a single canon point, the curve was refitted through that point using the other measured N (SKILL2 refits at those N):
- Inspiring: 3.13 x N^0.58 (Inspiring 2 7.08 -> 4.68).
- Sweep: 3.52 x N^0.45.
- Pounce: 1.66 x N^0.68.
- Regen: 2.19 x N^0.66.
- Shell: 1.99 x N^0.45.

## 3. The re-fit

The rule is BUDGET2's: cards the new prices push out of band (over a cap, or at or below 60% of N) get the smallest relative stat change, an over card only loses, and the Attack it gives up is handed back as Health. The plan is built against the cards as they were before SKILL2, so a re-run gives the same plan.

- 45 in-play cards were pushed out. The 36 trims and 1 lift are in section "What changed for players".
- **Held** (SKILL2-1), because no stat line fits or only one that takes more than 40% of the card: Alley Cat, Tide-pool Crab, Owl Nightwatch, Bee Forager, Conch Crawler, Gatling Hog, Ant Warrior (only 1/1 fits). Each one's new skill price alone breaks cap 2, since S·|S| grows with the square of the price. The Caldera Wyrm stays the owner's.

  - the pool is the map's list + SPECIAL only, within its cap;
  - the deck rule is 6 different cards / 4 copies;
  - search: 6,000 games a map, 24 a gauntlet entry for the score.
- **Result:** 113 decks on 35 maps (2-4 a map, a focus each). The best deck per map scores 53-96% on the gauntlet (median 83%).
  - The Old Bramble has no BPRO: its list is over its fights' Common cap.
  - Every previous BPRO deck is under `history` (DECK1-2 counted 46 of them that held a road-deck-only card).
  - The weakest best deck is Clovermead (53%).
