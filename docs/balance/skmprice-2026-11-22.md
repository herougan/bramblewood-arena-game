## How each price was set
- The reading is the shared measure `global|<key>` worthRuler in `docs/balance/pipeline/measures.json`. Where `docs/balance/skm/confirm-pipeline-2026-11-21.json` (SKM2) has a 1,000-fight reading and measures.json a shorter one (Pack 1, Corrode 1, Keen Sense, Darksight), the 1,000-fight reading wins. Every SKM4 flag reads at 1,000 fights. A few second points (N 2) come from 200 fights.
- The price at the measured N = the reading. If one N was measured, the entry's power is kept and its value scaled. If two N were measured, a v x N^pow curve goes through both. A shared factor (pair, shorthand, modifier) = reading / price, least squares when it has two readings.
- The half-miss and body multipliers (Swift, Offspring) are set from the canon carriers' stat base (3A + H = 19): multiplier = 1 + reading / 19.
- Check: on a 3A + H = 19 carrier (before the Purblind hold), 75 of the 84 readings behind these rows sit inside their 95% interval after the change, against 7 before.

Notes:
1. Own Shield is priced twice today: as 0.8 Health a point in the stat terms (FIN2-c, whose design says its table price is 0), and as 2.53 in the table (a SKILL2 pipeline write). With 0 in the table, Shield 3 costs 2.4 (reads 2.35) and Shield 4 costs 3.2 (reads 2.78).
2. The Archer's Attack is left out of its budget (it never swings), so Archer 3 (drop) costs -4.9 on the canon carriers. The drop style's +6.45 is the gap on those carriers. Stonedrop Vulture is 11/11, and on that body the flat price takes it from 74% to 102%. The style probably uses its Attack, so this is a body effect that CARRIER-1's static price cannot follow (owner call).
3. Fog and Storm were read with no Mist or storms. FIELDMIX (MAPCTX-1) re-measures field skills and may move them back.
4. On Death Spawn has one price for every token and count. Worker Ant x3 (7.47) stays under its price, and Bramble Sprout (3.54) goes over it.
5. On Ready > Damage: the least-squares factor fits Damage 4 (3.61, in range) and leaves Damage 2 at 1.81 (reads 2.67, 200 fights).
