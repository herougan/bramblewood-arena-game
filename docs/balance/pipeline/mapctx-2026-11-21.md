# MAPCTX: skills measured on their own map (2026-11-21)

A new kind of skill measure. Until now every skill was measured on one shared setup (every road card, every road field, Level 1, bramble 15).
Here each skill is measured on a map where players meet it: that map's own card pool, its fights' fields and its reference level
(`tools/pipeline/mapctx.sh`; keys `docs/balance/pipeline/state/mapctx-NN.json` = the skills carried by that map's pool; results in
`measures.json` under `mapN|<key>`). Late maps' pools hold no skill-less card, so every map uses the shared setup's six carriers
(soldier-ant, glass-golem, blessed-avatar, stable-colt, rivergate-recruit, lucky-hopper) played at the map's level. Early stopping as usual
(200 / 400 / 700 / 1,000 fights; most items converged at 200).

## Confirm: the 89 close calls at 1,000 fights
Every flagged item whose gap was under half its price and that had stopped at 200 fights was played again at 1,000
(`MAPCTX_SET=mapctx-confirm MAPCTX_ARGS="--fights 1000 --force" bash tools/pipeline/mapctx.sh ...`). **86 of 89 kept their verdict, none
flipped, 3 moved into range:** Daunt 2 on Map 11 (2.47 vs 2.56), Bulldoze 1 on Map 12 (2.32 vs 2.23, so Bulldoze leaves the
"cheap everywhere" list), Trap 6 on Map 18 (3.82 vs 3.74). The 200-fight readings are saved in `mapctx-rows-2026-11-21.json`, the confirmed
ones in `mapctx-rows-2026-11-21-confirm.json`. The early stop at 200 fights is trustworthy for these verdicts.

## What it means for balance
- A context skill's single price is wrong on most maps: too cheap at home, too dear away. Decision MAPCTX-1.
- 32 skills are off the same way on every map they meet: those can be re-priced once, with SKM4-1's 60 combat flags.
- Parasite (-9 to -10 on Maps 7 and 12) is the largest single miss: as a drawback it costs far more than its +1 price assumes.
- Map 18's high gap may come partly from its ranged skills being worth far more there than they cost (both sides carry them).
- Nothing here changes a card: prices only move the budget, and card stats stay until the owner asks.
