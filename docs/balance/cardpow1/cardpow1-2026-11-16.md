# CARDPOW1: card power and budget fill, every card (2026-11-16)

Every card of `canonical/cards.json` that can be on the board (875; test cards, Hall of Fame variants and materia left out), read as the card editor reads it: **fill** = B / N, B the cap-1 score f x ((3A + H) x m + S), N at that level for the map where a player first gets it; stats and skills at each level from the game's Forge curve (statsAtLevel, effectsAtLevel). Band (checklist B1): 85-100% at Level 1 with cap 2 kept. **Power** = the card power test (`docs/balance/bal25/card-power.json`, 2026-11-16, 400 games a card), win % minus the median Maps 0-20 card's (58.55%), as the editor shows it.

Data: `docs/balance/cardpow1/cardpow1-2026-11-16.json` and `.csv`. Workbook: `/home/claude/out/CardPower - Bramblewood.xlsx`. Tool: `node tools/cardpow1/cardpow1_2026_11_16.js --xlsx`. Proposals only; the stats are the owner's.

## The Level 1-10 curve (B2)

- Level 1 starters (380 in play, Maps 0-20): the Forge curve is a saw, down a few points a level as N grows and back up on the majors 4, 7, 10; median range 8.9 points, Level 10 vs Level 1 median 1.3 points. No card stalls (a level 10+ points under its L1 fill and under the band).
- Level 0 starters (18: tutorial, Base and Baby cards): Level 1 spends a third of the 0 -> 1 gain (it is no major level), so Level 2 (minor) catches up: median +12.3 points from Level 1 to 2 (Level 1 starters -2.9). 16 of the 18 jump flags are this. A curve call, not a card fix: make Level 1 a major for Level 0 starters (bramblewood-budget.js levelKind), or leave it.
- The other jumps: Hedge Changeling (L10 59.2% (11.5 over its L1 fill)), Rootworm Colony (L1->L2 +19.6; L10 108% (27 over its L1 fill); L2 over budget (100.6%); level skill at L2).
