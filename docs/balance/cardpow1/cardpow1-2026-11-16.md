# CARDPOW1: card power and budget fill, every card (2026-11-16)

Every card of `canonical/cards.json` that can be on the board (905; test cards, Hall of Fame variants and materia left out), read as the card editor reads it: **fill** = B / N, B the cap-1 score f x ((3A + H) x m + S), N at that level for the map where a player first gets it; stats and skills at each level from the game's Forge curve (statsAtLevel, effectsAtLevel). Band (checklist B1): 85-100% at Level 1 with cap 2 kept. **Power** = the card power test (`docs/balance/bal25/card-power.json`, 2026-11-16, 400 games a card), win % minus the median Maps 0-20 card's (58.6%), as the editor shows it.

Data: `docs/balance/cardpow1/cardpow1-2026-11-16.json` and `.csv`. Workbook: `/home/claude/out/CardPower - Bramblewood.xlsx`. Tool: `node tools/cardpow1/cardpow1_2026_11_16.js --xlsx`. Proposals only; the stats are the owner's.
