## L2: field effects (built)

### What counts
A map-level field effect is one that every fight of the map has: the map's own tables in `fightRules` (Mist, River Rapids, wind, darkness, the volcano), the effect every node shares, and the Tundra-family Frigid that the board adds on `MAP_KIND` 5 maps (`fieldEffectOf`). Fight-only effects (a boss's surge, a selfish boon, one fight's hazard) do not count. Tide and High Tide count as one family (the beach and reef maps run the Tide in skirmishes and High Tide in elites).

## L7: map card pools (proposed, not applied)

### Why it is not applied
The pools are not "a map's old and new cards" today: each main map's MAP tier is nearly all of that map's own pack cards (12-26 cards, 0-2 carried). Capping them at about 7 new and 3-5 old, by the rules in the brief, takes 269 cards out of the lists, and 187 of those are in no other list. By the brief's rule those 187 would wait for a source (`awaitingSource`), that is, leave the packs: 82 Common, 70 Uncommon, 34 Rare, 1 Starter, about a third of all pack cards. Applying that in this package also breaks things the package may not fix:
- `canonical/pro-skirmishes.json` (not this package's file): 235 PRO / BPRO checks fail, because the PRO and BPRO decks hold trimmed cards (BPRO plays the map's own pool only). The decks would need a fresh `pro_skirmishes` run.
- The budget's Map term: a card waiting for a source counts as Map 0 (`card-obtain-maps.json`), so some trimmed cards read over budget (`tests/eng10-2026-10-26.js` flags several).
- Recent placements pinned by tests (CONTENT2, CONTENT3, EN4, EN8, N221's homes).

I applied it once, ran the directly affected tests (20 of 37 failed), and put the files back. The plan, the script and the apply step are here for the owner's go (CONTENT4-1):
- `node docs/balance/content4-map-pools-trim-2026-10-28.js` writes `docs/balance/content4-map-pools-plan-2026-10-28.json` (the strict cap); with `--keep-pro` it keeps every card of the map's PRO / BPRO decks (`...-keeppro.json`).
- `python3 docs/balance/content4-map-pools-apply-2026-10-28.py <plan>` applies a plan to `canonical/map-pools.json` and the cards' sources; then `node tools/forge_curve_2026_10_19.js maps`.

### The rules the plan uses
- Every main map's MAP tier and every side map's own list, in road order (a side map at the number it opens on). The endless dungeons and the tutorial keep their lists. SPECIAL lists are not touched. Skirmish decks are not touched.
- New = no earlier list still holds it (after the earlier trims), and not Base; the rest are old (carried).
- Caps: 7 new everywhere; 3 old before Map 10, 5 from Map 10.
- Always kept: the map's skirmish rewards, its first-clear pack cards (`SKIRMISH_FIRST_PACK_CARDS`), its showcases, other non-pack sources, leaders. Then the cards its own fights play most, then the lower rarity. An old card stays only if a fight of the map plays it. From Map 10 a list with fewer than 4 old cards takes earlier cards its own fights already play (only one exists: the Ancient Sloth Titan on the Sundered Peak), so the main maps carry 0-2 old cards, not the owner's 4-5: their fights are built from their own pool.
- A pack card whose map no longer lists it moves to the first later list that still holds it (pack tier by that map; 11 cards), else it waits for a source (187). Nothing moves earlier.
