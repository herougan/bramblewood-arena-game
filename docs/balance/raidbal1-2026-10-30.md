## Why these levers
- **The warm-ups are a race.** At 20% / 15% the boss's cards are 1/1 to 2/4; you and the allies clear its board by turn 6-10 and never lose bramble, then hit its bramble for about 30 a turn. A bramble only changes the result once it is big enough to push the kill past turn 20: +46% Health (410 → 600) took Left only from 81 to 76, 780 takes it to 60.
- **The Core kills raiders.** Its three stumps (20 Health, outside the boss's stat shares) shield the bramble, and the Beak Strike and smacks wear the raiders down (they last 13-17 turns in the bad cases). A lower Core bramble and +60 raider bramble move it; the raiders' bonus does nothing on the warm-ups (they keep their bramble there).
- **Tried and dropped** (`runs` tA-tK in the JSON): raising the boss's card strength (`bossAtkPct` / `bossHpPct` 0.3-0.4) moved the warm-ups only 5-20 points (tC, tD) while the Core fell to 3% for Reference-2 and the optimizer deck (tD); swapping the Core's attackers for defensive sea cards changed nothing measurable.
