# SKW2: what +1 Attack is worth in Health, at bramble 15 (2026-11-14)

Measurement only (decision SKW1-5 a). Tool `tools/skw2/skw2.js` (`run`, `report`); raw games `skw2-2026-11-14.jsonl` (one line a point, every game kept); tables `skw2-summary.json`. Game fingerprint `919420caf9d7` (bench: engine, app, cards, budget, map pools). 404,736 games, 70 min, 2 workers.

## Method
- The road as the game builds it (`referenceFightSetup` / `playReferenceGame`): every road fight of Maps 3, 8, 13, 18 (7-8 each), CPU decks, leaders, fields and bramble Shield layers as built; your seat the Map reference deck at `referencePlayerLevel` (0, 0, 3, 3.54), no leader; both brambles' Health 15.
- 17 commons, cost = Lumber + Wait 1-6 (commons stop at Lumber 4), per cost a fragile, a middle and a sturdy body. 5 copies (the copy cap) replace the same 5 reference cards on a map.
- Paired variants (same fights, seeds, deck order): base, +1 Attack, +3 Shield, +h Health. Bisection on h (6 first, then the bracket's midpoint, doubling past the top; 4 points at most). 128 games a fight a point (896-1024 a point). w = the h where the isotonic Health curve meets the +1 Attack gain; '>' = beyond every measured h (capped at 60). 95% intervals: bootstrap over games, stratified by fight.

- **What it depends on: the unit's Health against its Attack, not its cost.** Spearman per unit: cost rho 0.00 (p 0.99), Health 0.78 (p < 0.01), Attack -0.54 (p 0.03); against H/A 0.72 (64 unit-maps, p < 0.01). Fit on the 41 uncensored: **w = 0.82 x (H/A)^1.01**, i.e. w ≈ H/A: the product law A x H (the budget's cap 2), not a flat weight (cap 1's 3A + H).
- Hitters with H/A <= 1.2 (5/4, 7/7, 3/3): median w 0.41, a point of Health is worth more than a point of Attack. Walls with H >= 13 (2/12 aside): extra Health is worth almost nothing at bramble 15 (+12 or +24 Health gains -6 to +10 points, mostly less than +1 Attack); the sturdy medians are censored.
- By map the pooled rate stays 2.4-4.5; the per-map medians move with which bodies have room (Map 13's base win is 58%, several fragile units there gain nothing from +1 Attack).
- **Shield**: +3 Shield is worth 1.00 Health a point (median, 65 unit-maps; fragile 0.83, middle 1.01, sturdy 1.25), against the budget's 0.8.
- **Why SKW1 read 4.3-9.9**: four of its six carriers have H/A 4.3-17 (2/9, 3/13, 2/12, 1/17; the others 3/3, 5/7), where w ≈ H/A is high and its +3 Health ruler buys little; this run reproduces that on rivergate-recruit (2/12: w 3.6-5.3, Map 13 > 26).

## Caveats
- The reference seat is the engine AI: it pitches its lowest `cardValue` (A + 0.45 H) card, so a stat change also changes what it pitches. 5 unit-maps lose win rate from +1 Attack (fox-warchief Map 8 -7.6, Map 13 four units -1 to -4); 21 of 68 +1 Attack gains are inside noise. Berserker-boar (Rage: +Health delays its Rage) is left out of the shape fit.
- Per unit-map intervals are wide (see `skw2-summary.json` rows); the pooled and median rows are the reliable ones.

## For SKW1-5
The average weight at bramble 15 on the road is 2.9-3.2, the budget's 3: no evidence for raising cap 1's Attack weight. The real miss is the shape (w ≈ H/A): fragile hitters' Health and walls' Attack are under-priced by a flat 3A + H; cap 2's A x H already carries that shape.
