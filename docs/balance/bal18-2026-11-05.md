# BAL18: stale ladder decks, the Lodge Matriarch, road re-measure (2026-11-05)

Deck changes only. No card stat, skill, list, reward, bramble, boss gimmick or Supabase row was changed. No road deck was changed either: 2-A-7 has no legal deck that is better (section 2).

## 1. The reference ladder: 85 stale decks rebuilt

`tests/bal13-2026-11-02.js` reported 85 ladder decks on 32 maps holding a card that has left the map's map-true pool (after CONTENT10's list changes). Examples: Grey Seal Bull, Squire's Mount, Coral Polyp Colony, Pinecone Sapper, Gale Swift.

Tool: `node tools/bal18/ladder_stale_2026_11_05.js list | build | collect`. It runs BAL13's own ladder code (`tools/bal13/ladder_2026_11_02.js`, compiled in place, not copied):
- **Candidates**, per map with a stale deck: BAL13's anchors (the Map reference, the Pool reference, the Reference (Random) decks, the map's PRO decks, each kept only if legal) and its 24 sampled decks. BAL18 adds each stale deck repaired the way BAL13's `fix` repairs one: its out-of-pool copies go to the pool's nearest card.
- **Measure**: as BAL13 measures. The map's road fights, 6 games a fight, ladder seeds 5300 + 37g, at the Map reference's level, with the ladder entry's own leader.
- **Choice**: each stale slot gets the unused candidate nearest that slot's old selection win rate, at least 4 copies from every other deck on the map (BAL13's rule). The map is then ranked weak to strong again by selectWin.

Result:
- 85 slots filled: 17 by a repaired deck, 56 by a sampled deck, 12 by an anchor.
- 315 decks unchanged (counts, name and selectWin).
- Mean distance from the old selection win: 4.2 points.
- 17 decks changed rank.
- Builds are in `docs/balance/bal18/ladder/<map>.json`. Slot by slot: `docs/balance/bal18/ladder-stale-2026-11-05.json`.
- `ladder_2026_11_02.js check`: ok, 40 maps, 10 decks each. Baked with `tools/opt/ref_decks.js --apply` (`--check`: up to date).

Where the replacement is much weaker than the deck it replaced, the map's pool gives nothing stronger now. The Canopy (`my`) is the clearest case: its old rank 10 was 86.7%, and the best legal candidate is about 30%. The map's ladder now runs 10 -> 70 instead of 1 -> 87.

## 2. 2-A-7 The Lodge Matriarch: no legal deck reaches the band

Size 14, leader (Mayfly Dancer) and first-clear card (Riverworks Bastion) kept. Copy caps. Boss distinct count 5-9. K14 no worse (0 problems).

For example, Lodge Engineer 4, Victoria Regia 4, Old Sturgeon 1, Riverworks Bastion 1, Box Turtle, Water Shrew, Muskrat Reedgatherer and Duckling reads 97.5% at 200 games, against 99% for the current deck. That is within one standard error, so **the deck is left as it is**.
- **Spot checks outside the rules** (`tools/bal18/levers_2a7_2026_11_05.js`, 200 games, not applied):
  - K14 broken (4 Box Turtle, 3 Water Shrew): 95.5%.
  - The four reward cards in: 95.5%.
  - Shield 20 or 40: 99% / 98.5%. The Shield reaches only 10 / 20.

Why she cannot be hard:

- Lilypad Reach plays Map 6's reference deck (stage "road 6"), against a Map 2 side map's cards at their base level and a Basic CPU.

Decision BAL18-1. BAL16-4 a (exempt 2-A from K14) alone does not fix her.
