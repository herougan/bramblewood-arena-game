**Rule.** y(N) = the mean over L1 and L5 of the mean-over-bodies worth. A rider is re-priced when its table price is outside both levels' 95% interval at some N (BAL29's test). Flat (y(3) < 1.2 y(1)): one fixed price, the mean of y. Otherwise value x N^pow fitted through y. On/off riders: fixed at y. Status riders printed as {chance, duration}: the table charges them its value whatever the object, so one flat value, y at duration 2 (chance 50).

**What the mean says about damage over time.** On the mean-over-bodies unit, Poison and Bleed are close to their prices (Bleed a little under at N 2, Poison a little over at N 3: both re-fitted, the canon Poison included), Breach and Perforate are under-priced (raised), and Decay, Desecrate, Toxin, Wither, Greater Poison and Strangle are over-priced at N 2-3 (the half-scale table doubles them; the worth grows about N^0.3-0.7). The "1.5-3x under" in the BAL31 report compared the table with the best body, a unit about 1.7x the table's (BAL29).
