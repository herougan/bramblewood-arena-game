# Skill re-price, 2026-11-09 (package BAL32)

The owner's BAL31 answers (docs/decisions.json BAL31-1/2/3, 2026-11-09 03:55) applied to `canonical/skill-costs.json` (old values in `bal32Was`). Built by `python3 tools/bal32/reprice_2026_11_09.py`; data in `reprice-2026-11-09.json`. Prices are in 3A + H points, the mean over bodies averaged over Levels 1 and 5 (BAL29's unit). No card stat changed.

**Input (BAL31-2):** `docs/balance/bal31/onhit-2026-11-08.json`: 400 paired fights a variant, three bodies a level (L1 1/20 4/11 7/2, L5 1/24 4/15 8/3), fingerprint `ea252135c2bf`. Note: the file on the batch says 400 fights and three bodies (its 11-09 04:18 commit re-rendered the table prices; it is not the 800-fight all-bodies run).

**Rule.** y(N) = the mean over L1 and L5 of the mean-over-bodies worth. A rider is re-priced when its table price is outside both levels' 95% interval at some N (BAL29's test). Flat (y(3) < 1.2 y(1)): one fixed price, the mean of y. Otherwise value x N^pow fitted through y. On/off riders: fixed at y. Status riders printed as {chance, duration}: the table charges them its value whatever the object, so one flat value, y at duration 2 (chance 50).

**What the mean says about damage over time.** On the mean-over-bodies unit, Poison and Bleed are fairly priced (inside their intervals), Breach and Perforate are under-priced (raised), and Decay, Desecrate, Toxin, Wither, Greater Poison and Strangle are over-priced at N 2-3 (the half-scale table doubles them; the worth grows about N^0.3-0.7). The "1.5-3x under" in the BAL31 report compared the table with the best body, a unit about 1.7x the table's (BAL29).
