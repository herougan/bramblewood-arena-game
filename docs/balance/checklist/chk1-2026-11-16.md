## B12 Economy: PASS
Command: `node tools/econ2/econ_sim_2026_11_03.js --days 30 --out docs/balance/checklist/chk1-econ-sim-30d.json`. `--days` was added in this package. The play rules are the same as ECON2: the game's own tables, expected values.

| Player (30 days) | Gold Leaves in a day | Maple Leaves in / out a day | Dust in / out a day | Map reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dedicated | 1.05 | 850 / 530 | 753 / 744 | 19 (Map 10 on day 3) |
| Casual | 0.51 | 518 / 323 | 498 / 495 | 15 (Map 10 on day 7) |

- Gold Leaves: 1.05 a day matches ECON2-12 ("about 1 a day"; casual about 0.5). The sim never spends them, because packs are not modelled.
- Dust runs out (in ≈ out, the Forge cliff accepted in ECON2-1). Maple Leaves pile up at +320 a day for the dedicated player, 9.6k by day 30. That is the known ECON2-4, deferred by the owner.
- Infinite loop: none found.
  - In the sim, every sink (Forge, Forge tiers, Max Energy, Nest) pays nothing back, and the wallets grow linearly.
  - In the game, the only refund is a melt, which returns 97% of what was put in (`meltCardValue`). Disenchanting gives Dust only, and no path turns Dust back into Maple Leaves or Gold Leaves. Packs are one-way.
- Files: `chk1-econ-sim-30d.json`, `chk1-econ-sim-30d.txt`.
