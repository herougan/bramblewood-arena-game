# ECON1: the brambles' own resource, halved income, the first two daily dungeons (2026-10-30)

Owner decision BRUP-1, 2026-10-30 10:34: "Their own resource & a bit of normal resource. The bramble resource comes from winning fights. Dealing damage to bramble does net you a tiny bit even on loss. Overall - help me half all the resources you get, (except first-time rewards). Because, i want to make the first two daily dungeons: one gives bramble resources, the other gives normal resources. - grinding that = lvl 0 common unit levelled to lvl 10 per week."

Background (10-29 23:01): "I don't want people too tied up between choosing to level brambles (looong way) versus cards."

All numbers below come from `canonical/upgrade-costs.json` and `arena_app.js` (`castleLevelUpCost`, `levelUpCostRaw`, `DAILY_DUNGEON_PAY`, `ECON_INCOME_MULT`, `BRAMBLE_WIN_BLOSSOMS`). `tests/econ1-2026-10-30.js` re-checks the dungeon arithmetic from the same tables.

## 1. Briar Blossoms 💮 (placeholder name)

A bramble's Reinforce now costs mostly **Briar Blossoms** (💮, `myCurrencies.blossoms`), plus a quarter of the Dust and Leaves it used to cost. The name and icon are placeholders the owner can change (`CURRENCY_META.blossoms`, TX key `ui.econ1-blossoms`, `tools/i18n_2026_10_30_econ1.py`). "Thorns" was not used because Thorns is a keyword; "Berry" is Foraging's token.

A step's old price (`castleLevelUpCostRaw`, unchanged): (15 + 12L) Dust and (10 + 8L) Leaves for Levels 0-9, three times that for Levels 10-19, times the bramble's rarity multiplier. The new price of the same step:

| Paid in | Amount | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 💮 Briar Blossoms | 1 × the old Dust | `bramble.blossomsPerDust` |
| ✨ Dust, 🍁 Leaves | 25% of the old Dust and Leaves | `bramble.normalShare` |
| Materials (Levels 20-25) | unchanged (Ember Cores, a Dragon Scale) | `castleLevelMats` |

| Bramble | Lv 0 → 10 | Lv 0 → 20 |
|---|---|---|
| Common (×1) | 690 💮 + 175 Dust + 120 Leaves (was 690 Dust + 460 Leaves) | 2,760 💮 + 690 Dust + 470 Leaves |
| Uncommon (×2) | 1,380 💮 + 350 Dust + 230 Leaves | 5,520 💮 + 1,390 Dust + 920 Leaves |
| Rare (×4) | 2,760 💮 + 690 Dust + 460 Leaves | 11,040 💮 + 2,760 Dust + 1,840 Leaves |
| Epic (×11) | 7,590 💮 + 1,895 Dust + 1,270 Leaves | 30,360 💮 + 7,590 Dust + 5,070 Leaves |
| Legendary (×21) | 14,490 💮 + 3,625 Dust + 2,420 Leaves | 57,960 💮 + 14,490 Dust + 9,670 Leaves |

**Levels already bought stay.** **The castle credit** (from fusing spare brambles) was banked in Dust and Leaves. On first read it moves over once (`castleCreditMigrate`, marked `v:2`): its Dust becomes the same number of Blossoms, and its Dust and Leaves shrink to 25%. The credit therefore pays off exactly the same steps it paid before. A newly fused spare banks 1/8 of the new road in all three currencies.

### Where the player earns them
- **A won Conquest fight:** `BRAMBLE_WIN_BLOSSOMS` by kind (skirmish 4, elite and mini-boss 8, boss 15, raid boss 20, final boss 30), +10% per map number (`BRAMBLE_MAP_STEP`). On Map 28 a skirmish pays 15 and a boss 57; on Map 40 they pay 20 and 75. A Sweep win pays the same.
- **A lost or drawn fight:** a quarter of the win's Blossoms (`BRAMBLE_LOSS_SHARE`), times the share of the enemy bramble's Health you took, and at least 1 if you hit the bramble at all.
- **The Undercroft** (daily dungeon, Map 11): see section 3.
- Fights pay Blossoms only once the Forge's Reinforce tab is open (`RES3_FORGE_TAB_MAP.castles`, Map 28), so nobody earns a currency they can't spend yet. The Undercroft pays them from the start. Decision BRUP-2 asks whether Reinforce should open earlier.

### Where the player sees them
- The results' Rewards row (💮 pill; its hover says whether it came from the win or from the bramble damage on a loss).
- The Forge wallet, on the Reinforce tab only; the Reinforce button's cost pills; a note under the button.
- The Inventory wallet and the Profile wallet, once Reinforce is open or you hold some (`blossomsShown`).
- The node panel of an Undercroft floor ("63 Briar Blossoms for every floor won").

### Cloud
`player_currencies` keeps one column per currency (checked read-only on 2026-10-30: gold, gems, dust, metal, raid_points; `raid_shards` is still only proposed). `supabase/proposed/20261030_bramble_resource.sql` adds `blossoms integer`, with a per-write and hourly gain guard. It is **not applied**. Until it is, the client's first push gets "column blossoms does not exist", and from then on that session sends the row without the column. Blossoms stay in the browser, the way Kibble and Beer do. Once the SQL is applied, they sync with no client change.

## 2. Income halved, except first-time rewards

Fixed tables are halved once, where they are defined, so the screen shows what is paid. A computed amount carries its fraction into the next payment (`econPay`), so a 1-Dust win pays 1 Dust every other time.

**Not halved (first-time or not income):** first clears (Leaves, Dust, Metal, packs, cards, Kibble, items), a daily dungeon floor's first-clear reward and its 10 Gold Leaves, achievements, the level track's Leaves, the Scale Trail finale, Base growth, one-off gifts (the backpack note, the well, the birthday gift), disenchanting and fusing, market sales. Energy, card drops, packs, Kibble, Beer, Laurels, XP and raid trophy shards are not halved anywhere; the owner can add a kind to `ECON_HALVED_KINDS`.

The server's `reward_rules` table is empty (read-only check), so the browser pays every fight and the halving needs no server change. If rules are ever copied in, use the halved repeat amounts (noted in the proposed SQL).

### Days to level, Maps 0-20, before and after

The income is the Resources tab's week (`tools/resources_week_2026_10_23.js`). A dedicated player finishes all 6 notices a day plus the daily and weekly quest tiers, about 4,600 Dust a week (notices 3,405, quest tiers 1,133). A skirmish repeat pays no Dust, so on Maps 0-20 nearly all Dust comes from these. After the halving that is about 2,300. A casual player does 3 notices a day and the daily tiers, about 1,800 a week, now about 900. Dust is the binding currency: a step costs 1.5× as much Dust as Leaves, and Leaves also come from every win. The road's first clears still pay their full one-off Dust, about 7,300 by Map 20.

| What | Dust | Dedicated: before / after / after + Murgle | Casual: before / after / after + Murgle |
|---|---|---|---|
| One Common card, Lv 0 → 10 | 759 | 1.2 / 2.3 / 1.7 days | 3.0 / 5.9 / 3.2 days |
| A 20-copy map deck, Lv 1 → 3 (Village Forge, Map 11) | 1,270 | 1.9 / 3.9 / 2.9 days | 4.9 / 9.9 / 5.4 days |
| The same deck, Lv 3 → 4 (Iron Forge, Map 13) | 854 | 1.3 / 2.6 / 1.9 days | 3.3 / 6.6 / 3.6 days |
| The same deck, Lv 4 → 8 (Ember Forge, Map 16) | 5,334 | 8.1 / 16.2 / 12.2 days | 20.7 / 41.5 / 22.5 days |

"+ Murgle" adds the normal-resource dungeon's 759 Dust a week, about 108 a day. The dungeons are still hidden (`DAILY_DUNGEONS_HIDDEN`), so until they open the "after" column is what players get. In short, the grind to each Forge gate takes twice as long. Murgle Mayhem gives back about half of the loss for a dedicated player, and nearly all of it for a casual one.

## 3. The first two daily dungeons

`DAILY_DUNGEONS` lists Murgle Mayhem (Map 6) first and the Undercroft (Map 11) second. **Murgle Mayhem pays normal resources; the Undercroft pays the bramble resource.** The Undercroft sits under the Arena's vaults, which suits the brambles' stonework Reinforce. Every won floor pays `DAILY_DUNGEON_PAY`, whether or not it is the floor's first clear, and mini-boss floors pay the same. This comes on top of each floor's own first-clear reward, which is unchanged. The pay is not halved and gets no rank bonus, so a week's total is the table's number. A lost floor pays nothing from the table (a loss in the Undercroft pays no Blossoms either).

**Attempts:** keys fill up to the cap each day (3), and one more comes on Monday (`loadDungeonTickets`): **22 keys a week**. Both dungeons draw on the same keys, so grinding both each day means **11 won floors in each** a week. Key ring upgrades (4 and 5 keys) give more.

**Murgle Mayhem, the normal-resource dungeon:** a Common card from Level 0 to 10 costs, from `canonical/upgrade-costs.json`:
- Level 0 → 1: one Materia Mote, 675 / 45 = **15 Dust**, 450 / 45 = **10 Leaves**.
- Levels 1 → 10: `forgeStepDust` 35 + 44 + 53 + 62 + 71 + 80 + 120 + 133 + 146 = **744 Dust**. Leaves are two thirds of each step, rounded: 23 + 29 + 35 + 41 + 47 + 53 + 80 + 89 + 97 = **494**.
- In all: **759 Dust, 504 Leaves.**

Pay per won floor: 759 / 11 = **69 Dust** (exact) and ⌈504 / 11⌉ = **46 Leaves**. A week: 11 × 69 = **759 Dust**, 11 × 46 = **506 Leaves**, one Common card from Level 0 to 10 (2 Leaves over).

**The Undercroft, the bramble dungeon:** **63 Blossoms** a won floor, so a week is 11 × 63 = **693 Blossoms**. In bramble levels, from Level 0:

| Bramble | A week of the Undercroft (693 💮) | Also needs (normal share) |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Levels 0 → 10 (690 💮) | 175 Dust, 120 Leaves |
| Uncommon | Levels 0 → 6 (540 💮) | 138 Dust, 90 Leaves |
| Rare | Levels 0 → 4 (528 💮) | 132 Dust, 88 Leaves |
| Epic | Levels 0 → 2 (462 💮) | 115 Dust, 78 Leaves |
| Legendary | Levels 0 → 1 (315 💮) | 79 Dust, 53 Leaves |

The higher levels cost more. Levels 10-19 of a Common bramble take 2,070 Blossoms, about three weeks of the Undercroft alone. From Map 28, fights add more: a dedicated player's ~28 wins a day at a Map 28 skirmish (15 each) come to about 2,900 a week.
