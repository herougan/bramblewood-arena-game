# ROADFIX4 (2026-11-25): the road after DAUNT2, MAPEXEC1 and CARDVET1

DAUNT2 (Scare -> Daunt on 6 cards, Intimidate on Booming Bittern, Puffball's Bloom, Keen Sense), MAPEXEC1 (57 list edits; enemy decks unchanged, 99 fights now hold off-list cards) and CARDVET1a/b/c (52 cards redesigned or swapped to real animals on Maps 0-20) moved the road from 177 in band to 122. This package measures the road (Maps 0-20, 400 games a fight, seeds 9100), finds which card change moved the biggest movers, and re-fits the out-of-band fights by enemy-deck swaps from each fight's own list. Rules: leaders (showcase or Auto pick), first-clear cards, showcase and deck size kept; copy caps, K14 (side maps: Carried x2, SIDE2-2 e), the 5-7 distinct-card rule and the map lines (usage, family, mix) as tests/skir1 reads them, with no new problem against the live decks; no Shield, Health or enemy-level change; no card stats changed; side fights use only their own list; an off-list card (TESTFIX9-2 open) never gains copies (11 fixes take some out, below). 8-7 (REFIT20-1) and 20-A-4 (TESTFIX9-1) left alone.

## How

1. `curve 100 --from=post1125 --fixes=roadfix4/curve-raw --decks-only --keep-leaders --keep-first --keep-size --pool --strict --distinct --wide --skip=8-7,20-A-4,20-B-2` on the 84 out-of-band fights (tune seeds 7300; logs `roadfix4/curve-0.log`, `-1.log`). `roadfix4/sort_2026_11_25.js` takes a proposal that changes the deck, is 2+ points nearer on the tune seeds, breaks no deck rule anew (a lesser form of a live problem, e.g. K14 x3 from x4, is not new) and gives no off-list card a copy: 33 taken.
2. Validated at 400 games on seeds 9100 and 5100 (`skirmish-pass-roadfix4-v9100`, `-v9100b`, `-v5100`, `-v5100b`; 5100 before = `skirmish-pass-roadfix4-pre-s5100`), kept only in band on both or 2+ points nearer on both (`roadfix2/compare_2026_11_24.js`): 26 kept.
3. The legal swap search (`roadfix4/search_2026_11_25.js`, ROADFIX3's copy: an off-list card may lose copies, never gain; seeds 11300 / 12300) for the 58 left and the three kept but still out (12-5, 19-4, 11-A-3), in two lanes by map (`roadfix4/lanes_2026_11_25.sh`, `lane1.log`, `lane2.log`): 48 decks found, validated the same way (`-v9100c/d`, `-v5100c/d`, before = the live decks, `skirmish-pass-roadfix4-base9100/5100`): 41 kept.
4. tests/skir1 then caught three kept decks (15-6, 19-2, 19-6) that changed the fight's Auto leader (a stronger card came in). The search now keeps the leaders (`leadOf`); the three were searched again from their old decks (`lane3.log`, `lane4.log`) and validated (`-v9100e`, `-v5100e`): all three kept with their leaders. All 64 changed fights keep their leaders (checked with `skirmishLeadersOf`), and the 64 together add no deck-rule problem (`roadfix4/newprob_2026_11_25.js`).

Each changed deck in `arena_app.js` carries a `/* 2026-11-25 (ROADFIX4): ... */` note with the swap, target and both reads (`roadfix4/comment_2026_11_25.js`, causes `roadfix4/why.json`). Fixes: `docs/balance/roadfix4-fixes.jsonl`.
