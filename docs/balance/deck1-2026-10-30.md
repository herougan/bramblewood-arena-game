# DECK1: focused PRO decks per map (2026-10-30)

Package DECK1, step 4 of the owner's balancing plan (`docs/action-items.md`, "10-28 21:40"): "Several hard optimised decks per map with different focuses; at most 6 different cards, at most 4 copies each." Data: `docs/balance/deck1-2026-10-30.json` (pools, focuses, results), the searches `docs/balance/deck1/bench-deck-map*.json`, the ease measure `docs/balance/pro-ease-deck1-2026-10-30.json`. Tool: `tools/opt/deck1_2026_10_30.js` (`plan`, `run`, `apply`, `check`).

## How the decks were built

- **Rule:** at most 6 different cards, at most 4 copies (and never above the rarity cap), 20 cards, Level 1, the map's rarity cap, no faction cards, the showcase leader from Map 2 (the existing PRO conventions).
- **Pool:** the map's current list (map-pools.json MAP tier, or the side map's own list) + SPECIAL, as the anchor: every deck holds at least 10 of its 20 copies from it. Plus a held shortlist (cards a player holds by then under the PRO map rule): 6 by phase-2 relatedness to the map, 6 by measured contribution per copy (`bench-contrib-global-2026-10-30-final.json`, after RECOST1). Road-deck-only cards (cut from the list by THEME3) are not in the pool.
- **Focuses** (the owner's "high-cost cards, cost generators, normal creatures"): economy (6+ copies of resource makers), big (8+ copies of the list's costliest step), swarm (14+ copies of the cheapest step), or the list's main skill family where one of those cannot be filled under the 4-copy rule; then open (the strongest deck at least 6 copies from the others). The map's resource (Stone / Metal costs): only 4 cards in the game pay Stone or Metal, so no map supports that focus.
- **Search:** `tools/bench/bench.js deck` (TOOLS1 T3, extended with the anchor rule): 12,000 games a map (24,000 for Maps 1 and 3), the deckopt gauntlet (a fair mirror against the map's fight decks and reference decks under each fight's field), 24 games an entry for the reported score. A deck under max(50%, the map's best - 15 points) is left out as not hard; at least 2, at most 4 a map.
- **Measure:** `node tools/opt/pro_ease.js --kind pro --games 100 --tag deck1-2026-10-30`: each PRO fight as the game builds it (the map's castle, the average field, the showcase leader, Smart CPU, Level 1 or PREF-3's late-side level) against the map reference (`map`, at `referencePlayerLevel`) and Reference-2 (`ref2`, the owner's Ant Deck at its schedule). 100 games a pairing (about +/-10 points at 95%). The previous PRO decks were measured the same way (100 games) for the "before" columns.

## Maps whose PRO decks are still easy, and why

- **Map 3 Gannet Cliffs** (the map reference wins 68-90%, 3-PRO-4 90%; Ref-2 68-85%): **pool ceiling.** The list is thirteen cards at 0-1 Lumber with no high end; a re-run at double budget and another seed gave the same best score (72% -> 75%, inside the noise). Only new cards (a 2-cost card, a second generator) would make it harder.
- **Map 0 Bramblewood Outskirts** (map reference 56-81%, Ref-2 97-100%): **pool ceiling** (Commons only, twelve cards, average cost 0.25). Ref-2 at Level 0 is simply far stronger than any Common deck; Map 0 is the tutorial-adjacent map, so this may be intended.
- **Map 1 Thistlewood** (map reference 44-50%, Ref-2 48-71%; not easy any more, but the weakest main-map PRO before Map 3): was **optimizer-limited**: the first run gave 58-75%, double budget found 11 points more (65% -> 76% on the gauntlet). The 6/4 rule is the other cause: its old 12-card PROs held the map reference to 21-62%.
- **Map 20 The Sundered Peak** (map reference 61-74%) and **Map 10 Coral Current** (60-61%): **the high-cost lists.** Their big decks (8 copies at 3+ Lumber) reach only 66-71% on the gauntlet and were left out; the kept decks are midrange/tempo. On Map 20 the reference also plays 3.9 Forge levels above the PRO's Level 1 (a level gap that grows from Map 11; decision DECK1-3). Coral Current: its gauntlet score (89%) and the game's result disagree most of any map, so the gauntlet (a mirror on your own bramble) may under-read the map's castle / field here.
- Map 2 (the owner's example, "Some maps' pros are super easy. Like Map 2"): no longer easy: the map reference wins 34-52% (before 37-73%), Ref-2 54-62% (before 52-82%).
- Side maps that open late (PREF-3): Lilypad Reach and Smugglers' Grotto (open at Maps 6-7, cap still Uncommon) now play their PRO fights at Level 1.6-1.7; Ref-2, at its Map 6-7 schedule, still wins 74-90% there.

## Each map, thought through

**0 Bramblewood Outskirts** (m1; cap common; list 12, anchor 12, cost 0-2, average 0.25; generators 1). Commons only (the cap), twelve cards and almost all 0 Lumber: the Ant Hill (2) is the one high-cost card and the Beaver Lumberjack the one generator, so "big" cannot carry a deck (42%, left out). The hard decks are wide 0-cost swarms; the flyers (bees, Owl Sentinel) give the third shape. Kept: open, swarm of normal creatures, flying (skill family). Left out: high cost 42%.

**0 The Old Bramble** (mo, side map of m1, opens on Map 21; cap common; list 13, anchor 0, cost 0-1, average 0.21; generators 4). Its THEME3 list is "advanced, as if Map 21" (Rares and up) but its four fights are Common, so under the PRO cap none of its own cards is legal: the decks are built from the held Commons only (no anchor). Generators (Talus Pika, Leafcutter Gardener, Nutria Reedcutter) make the economy and big decks. Owner call DECK1-4. Kept: high cost, cost generators + payoff, open. Left out: swarm of normal creatures 70%.

**1 Thistlewood** (mf; cap uncommon; list 15, anchor 19, cost 0-2, average 0.26; generators 1). Nineteen cards, cost 0-1 except the Ant Hill: a normal-creature map. The search was budget-limited here (double budget: open 65% -> 76%), so its decks come from the double-budget run. Only swarm-shaped decks are hard; economy (one generator on the list) and big fell under the floor. Kept: open, swarm of normal creatures. Left out: high cost 54%; cost generators + payoff 48%.

**1 Clovermead** (mq, side map of mf, opens on Map 12; cap uncommon; list 13, anchor 15, cost 0-2, average 0.2; generators 1). Kept: open, swarm of normal creatures, cost generators + payoff. Left out: high cost 48%.

**2 Pebble Beach** (mb; cap uncommon; list 13, anchor 16, cost 0-2, average 0.38; generators 4).
