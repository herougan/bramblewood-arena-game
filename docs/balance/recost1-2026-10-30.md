Yardstick (the 80-fight run, 283 carriers): one budget point (2A + H) = 4.17 win-rate points; +1 Attack = 9.5 points, +1 Health = 3.0 (1 Attack is worth 3.2 Health here; the budget counts 2).

## What changed for players

- **64 skill prices** in `canonical/skill-costs.json` moved to their measured worth. Most expensive skills got cheaper (Tsunami, Sturdy, Lone Wolf, Sweep, Rage, Quick, Undead ...); shots and a few strong skills got dearer (Acid Bolt, Spitfire, Avalanche, Charge, Strangle, Swipe ...).
- **36 in-play cards re-fit**: 17 trimmed (over budget under the new prices, mostly -1 or -2 Health) and 19 lifted (under budget and measurably weak, at most +4 stat points, up to 80% of N). No skill, rarity, Wait or Lumber changed.
- 3 in-play cards stay over budget: stats cannot fix them (see "For the owner").

## Rule

- a price changes when the 200-fight worth differs from the carriers' price by more than 2 standard errors and at least 0.5 budget points; value = worth / the scale factor at the carriers' N, floored at 0 (a drawback keeps its negative value); owner-set, logic-rule, field-conditional, same-card-synergy, partner-resource and trigger entries are reported, not changed.
- Applied entries follow the table's convention (`prevValue`, `appliedAt`, `measuredS` = `value`, `measuredConf`, `measuredSE` in table units, `measuredHow`); an entry that had an older application keeps it under `recost1Was`.
