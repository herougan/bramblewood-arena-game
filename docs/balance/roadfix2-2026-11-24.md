# ROADFIX2 (2026-11-24): the Maps 0-20 road after ENG16 and CARDS1

ENG16 (Corrode refreshes instead of stacking) and CARDS1 (starters at Level 0, resistances trimmed, Fork / Trident rationed, Gallery Orb-Weaver redesign) moved the road: `post1124` (seeds 9100, 400 games a fight) read 139 in band / 160 within 10 / 13 beyond 20 of 208. This package retunes enemy decks only, under the owner's 11-22 21:28 rules (REFIT13..18): list swaps from the fight's own list, leaders / first-clear cards / showcase kept, deck sizes, copy caps and the 5-7 distinct-card rule as the game has them, no Shield, no enemy level, no Health change, no new off-list card, no re-carding of fights that already hold another map's card (TESTFIX9-2 open). No card stats changed.

## How

1. `tools/skirmish_pass.js curve 100 --from=post1124 --only=<68 off-band fights> --fixes=roadfix2-fixes --decks-only --keep-leaders --keep-first --keep-size --pool --strict --distinct --wide` (tune seeds 7300). Raw output: `roadfix2/curve-raw.jsonl`. Its proposals were checked with the full deck rules (`tools/legal1/lib_legal_2026_11_15.js`: copies, distinct cards, pool, K14, the map usage / family / mix lines; `roadfix2/legal_2026_11_24.js`); 16 legal ones kept (`roadfix2/curve-accepted.jsonl`).
2. The rest: a legal-only swap search, `roadfix2/search_2026_11_24.js` (BAL-LEGAL1's search, copied; "legal" = no new rule problem against the live deck, off-list cards may not gain copies, no side-deck shrink, no levers), seeds 11300 (60 games) / 12300 (300 games), two lanes by map (`roadfix2/lanes_2026_11_24.sh`), a move kept only if 2+ points nearer. Log: `roadfix2/search-2026-11-24.jsonl`.
3. Every candidate applied (`apply --fixes=roadfix2-fixes`), then measured at 400 games on seeds 9100 and 5100 (`skirmish-pass-roadfix2-v9100 / -v5100`, round 2 `-v9100b / -v5100b`; before on 5100: `skirmish-pass-roadfix2-pre-s5100`). Kept when in band on both seed sets, or 2+ points nearer the target on both; else reverted (`roadfix2/compare_2026_11_24.js`, `roadfix2/validate-1.json`, `validate-2.json`).
