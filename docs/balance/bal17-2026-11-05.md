# BAL17: card flags on the walled maps, Maps 21-24 ladders and retune, road re-measure (2026-11-05)

Decision BAL16-1 option a (card flags), plus Maps 21-24's reference ladders and road retune. No card stat, skill, list, reward, bramble or Supabase row changed. Decks changed: 13 road decks on Maps 21-24 only.

## Summary
- **The walled maps are carried by their enemy brambles, not their cards.** For each of BAL16-1's 38 walled fights, every enemy card was swapped for a neutral filler of the same cost (60 games each). Swapping all of them at once makes 7 of 8 maps *harder* (Savanna Reaches 21.7% -> 0.2%). No single card on Savanna Reaches, The Canopy, Wolfsbane Tundra or the Old Bramble moves its map 3 points. Turning the enemy bramble's ability off lifts these maps 15 to 51 points. Lowering only its ability numbers by 2 (undoing ENG24's "+2 on every bramble ability", which hit enemy brambles too) lifts Savanna Reaches 21.7% -> 53.6%, the Old Bramble 27.1% -> 50% and Caves & Alcoves 40.5% -> 51.7%. That is decision ENG24-1 option c. New decision BAL17-1.
- **Cards that do drive a map** (one per map at most): Stalactite Pill Bug (Caves & Alcoves), Clownfish Scout and Orca Vanguard (10-1), Mangrove Manatee (the Sunken Depths' leader) and Cinder Stoker (Basalt Foundry). None is over budget. Flags only: the owner re-tunes.
- **Maps 21-24 reference ladders:** 10 decks a map, built with BAL13's ladder tool and rule (m21-m24 in `docs/balance/ref/ref-ladder.json`, baked into the game). The balance summary now measures Maps 21-24. Their PRO / BPRO / WPRO decks were already built by BAL16 under its rules (21: 4 / 4 / 2, 22: 2 / 2 / 2, 23: 3 / 3 / 2, 24: 3 / 4 / 2; `refdecks_2026_11_05.js check` ok), so none were added.
- **Maps 21-24 retune** (each fight's own list, `--strict`, so K14 does not get worse; size, leaders and first-clear cards kept): BAL11's weak pass on the 10 fights 20+ points too hard (7 decks), then one curve round (6 decks; a second round found no move). Maps 21-24: 11 -> 18 of 30 fights in band, 14 -> 19 within 10, 12 -> 2 beyond 20.

## 1. Card flags (BAL16-1 a)

Tool: `node tools/bal17/flags_2026_11_05.js run | extra | steps | report --games 60 --workers 2`. Data: `docs/balance/bal17/flags-2026-11-05.json` (per fight and per card; raw readings in `docs/balance/bal17/state/`). Each fight is played as the road measure plays it: the Map reference at its level, no leader for your seat, and the enemy's own leader, bramble, Shield, field and CPU. Seeds are the retune seeds, 7300 + 37g, 60 games a variant, so a reading carries about +/-12 points (95%).
- **Neutral filler:** the same card (cost, Wait, rarity, types, level) with no skill and vanilla stats of a typical road card: 2A + H = 0.886 N. That is the median budget use of the road's 312 enemy cards. The card's shape (its share of Attack) is kept. **Moves** = filler win minus base win: + means the card makes the map harder. The map move is the mean over the map's walled fights (0 where the card is absent).
- **Budget:** the printed card's cap 1 and cap 2 use (`tools/skill_costs.js` budget, the game's sbCheck rule). Over 100% = over budget. No flagged card is over.
- **Step:** one 3A + H step weaker (-1 Attack, or -3 Health on a 1-Attack card) on every copy, in the map's walled fights that hold the card.
- **Bramble checks:** no Shield; plain bramble (its ability off, same Health); its ability numbers -2 (ENG24 undone); plain bramble and no Shield.
