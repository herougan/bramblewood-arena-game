## Notes
- **Dive** acts only on Flying, Tide, Quick or Sprinter units (engine `diveEligible`). On a farm-dog or the vanilla carriers it does nothing, so its reading there is 0 by construction. These readings measure Dive where it acts instead:
  - pipeline: Tide + Dive 2 minus Tide on the same carriers (-0.55 minus 0.25), against a price of -1.2 minus 0.5;
  - equivalence: E11, the Tide body (mudpuppy);
  - carrier x20: harbor-seal.
  - Result: the drawback is worth about -0.8 points in the pipeline and about 0 in the other designs, not -1.7. The pipeline interval adds the two items' widths in quadrature, a conservative bound.
- **Flying and evasive:** only the mirror designs read them dear. With 20 flyers (or evasive units) against 20 plain ground units, nothing can block them. In the pipeline's mixed random decks they read at or under their price. Not confirmed.
- **Corrode, trident and repair** lean the flagged way in the pipeline (each interval excludes the price) but by less than the 25% bar. The other three methods agree with the flag, so these are small mispricings, not large ones.
- **Keen Sense, Pulverise, Repair and Offspring** are situational: they need darkness or blindness, armour or Structures, damaged Structures, or a death. The pipeline's random decks hold those situations, and it still reads them far under price.
- **Agreement with the pipeline.** Rank correlation of (value - price) / max(1, |price|), over the skills both read: round robin 0.84, carrier x20 0.68, equivalence 0.65. The new design tracks the pipeline best of the three on these skills.
- **Anchored reading.** RR-anchored uses only the skill-vs-skill games, priced by the canon anchors. It is noisy: its anchor line is shallow, and the poison anchor reads -0.45 against 2.72. It stays in the JSON only.
