# Skill measurement registry: summary (2026-11-20)

Package SKM1. 111 methods with results (91 from 11 game designs played today, 20 offline) out of 212 registered; 17 independent families vote. 103 of 103 Maps 0-20 skills have 3+ family estimates. 169200 games played. Tables: `summary-2026-11-20.json`; per-method lines: `results/<method>.jsonl`; methods: `tools/skm/registry.js`.

- **How to read it.** Worth and price are budget points. Each method is first divided by its own median worth / price (its exchange rate), so the flags are about skills priced wrong relative to the others, not about the table's overall level: that level depends on the design (median raw worth / price 0.7 for the yardstick designs (gain / the gain of +1 Attack or +2 Health), 1.09 for the stat-equivalence searches), so these runs do not settle whether skills as a whole cost too much.
- Skills that need partners or an economy (Lumber makers, tribes, blooms, deliveries, weather) read near 0 in every combat design here; read their over-priced flags as "not measurable this way", not as a verdict.
- One vote per family (a game design, or an offline source): the estimators of one design share its games. Prices came from the pipeline canon and the designers' cards are fitted to them, so those sources are shown but do not vote.
