# Skill measurement registry: summary (2026-11-20)

Package SKM1. 17 methods with results (1 from 1 game designs played today, 16 offline) out of 121 registered; 6 independent families vote. 89 of 103 Maps 0-20 skills have 3+ family estimates. 2280 games played. Tables: `summary-2026-11-20.json`; per-method lines: `results/<method>.jsonl`; methods: `tools/skm/registry.js`.

- **How to read it.** Worth and price are budget points. Each method is first divided by its own median worth / price (its exchange rate), so the flags are about skills priced wrong relative to the others. Across today's game designs the raw median worth / price is **0.67**: the price table as a whole charges more for skills than they win.
- One vote per family (a game design, or an offline source): the estimators of one design share its games. Prices came from the pipeline canon and the designers' cards are fitted to them, so those sources are shown but do not vote.
