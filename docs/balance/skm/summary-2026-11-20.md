# Skill measurement registry: summary (2026-11-20)

Package SKM1. 142 methods with results (122 from 17 game designs played today, 20 offline) out of 212 registered; 23 families in 15 kinds vote. 103 of 103 Maps 0-20 skills have 3+ kinds' estimates. 296220 games played. Tables: `summary-2026-11-20.json`; per-method lines: `results/<method>.jsonl`; methods: `tools/skm/registry.js`.

- **How to read it.** Worth and price are budget points. Each method is first divided by its own median worth / price (its exchange rate), so the flags are about skills priced wrong relative to the others, not about the table's overall level: that level depends on the design (median raw worth / price 0.88 for the yardstick designs (gain / the gain of +1 Attack or +2 Health), 1.17 for the stat-equivalence searches), so these runs do not settle whether skills as a whole cost too much.
- Skills that need partners or an economy (Lumber makers, tribes, blooms, deliveries, weather) read near 0 in every combat design here; read their over-priced flags as "not measurable this way", not as a verdict.
- One vote per kind: estimators of one design share its games (a family = their median), designs of one procedure agree partly by construction (a kind = their families' median). Prices came from the pipeline canon and the designers' cards are fitted to them, so those sources are shown but do not vote.

## Proposals for the owner

1. Flying: all 14 kinds read it under-priced (worth about 5 points against 2.2). Re-measure it in the pipeline, then raise its constant (`run.sh price --set-const flying=...`). Your call.
2. Combat skills that 3 in 4 kinds call cheap: pack, corrode, evasive, poisonBarbs, trident, diurnal, sleepPowder, poisonPowder (and Dive's drawback reads about 0, not -1.7). Re-measure them at 1,000 fights (`run.sh measure --skills ...`), then raise the confirmed ones.
3. Combat skills that 3 in 4 kinds call dear: keenSense, parasite, overwhelm, offspring. Same re-measure, then lower the confirmed ones.
4. Skills that need an economy or partners (forestry, rainrunner, dry, bloomCopy, broodkeeper, bounty, herald, loyal, salvage, delivery, Deliver on Attack / on Healed) read about 0 in every fight design. Choose: keep them priced by the 4-round logic and mark them so, or fund a design whose decks pay Lumber and hold their tribe.
5. Price evidence: keep the stat-equivalence searches and the carrier x20 test as the check before a price change (rank agreement 0.8-0.9); stop using card-contribution regressions, the deckopt pick rate and the workbook figures for prices (under 0.4).
