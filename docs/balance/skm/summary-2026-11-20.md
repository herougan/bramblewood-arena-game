# Skill measurement registry: summary (2026-11-20)

Package SKM1. 134 methods with results (114 from 16 game designs played today, 20 offline) out of 212 registered; 22 families in 15 kinds vote. 103 of 103 Maps 0-20 skills have 3+ kinds' estimates. 260420 games played. Tables: `summary-2026-11-20.json`; per-method lines: `results/<method>.jsonl`; methods: `tools/skm/registry.js`.

- **How to read it.** Worth and price are budget points. Each method is first divided by its own median worth / price (its exchange rate), so the flags are about skills priced wrong relative to the others, not about the table's overall level: that level depends on the design (median raw worth / price 0.79 for the yardstick designs (gain / the gain of +1 Attack or +2 Health), 1.17 for the stat-equivalence searches), so these runs do not settle whether skills as a whole cost too much.
- Skills that need partners or an economy (Lumber makers, tribes, blooms, deliveries, weather) read near 0 in every combat design here; read their over-priced flags as "not measurable this way", not as a verdict.
- One vote per kind: estimators of one design share its games (a family = their median), designs of one procedure agree partly by construction (a kind = their families' median). Prices came from the pipeline canon and the designers' cards are fitted to them, so those sources are shown but do not vote.
