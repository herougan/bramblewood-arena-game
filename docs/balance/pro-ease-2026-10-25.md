# PRO and BPRO ease against the reference decks (2026-10-25, package PREF / P3)

Owner, 17:53: "the pro decks are strong. Some maps' pros are super easy. Like Map 2." 22:36: "I like the BPRO decks. I wonder which are too 'ez'?"

Every PRO and BPRO fight in `canonical/pro-skirmishes.json` (36 entries: Maps 0-20 and 15 side maps, 87 PRO + 100 BPRO decks) was played 80 times against each reference set. The fights were played the way the game plays them (`tools/opt/pro_ease.js`): the PRO's deck at Level 1 with its leader, the map's castle and Shield, the entry's average field, and the CPU on Smart. Your seat is also Smart, with the plain bramble and no leader.
- **Map reference:** the road measure's deck (`referenceDeckFor`, the Map N road deck at `referencePlayerLevel`: Lv 0 up to Map 10, then Lv 3 to 3.9).
- **Reference-2:** the owner's Ant Deck (`docs/balance/ref/ref2-owner-main-deck-2026-10-25.json`), unlevelled at Map 0, then +5 levels a map, seeded. A side map counts as the map it opens on, as the game counts it (the Old Bramble = Map 17).

Raw result: `docs/balance/pro-ease-2026-10-25.json` (374 pairings, 80 games each, with 95% intervals). Rerun: `node tools/opt/pro_ease.js --games 80 --tag <tag>`. Nothing in any skirmish, PRO or BPRO deck was changed (owner: "PRO is not to change any existing skirmishes").

## Summary
- **Too easy** (a reference wins more than 85%): 47 of 374 pairings. 12 are against the map reference, 35 against Reference-2. 42 of the 47 are BPRO decks.
- **Mean win rate:** PRO 35% (map reference) and 58% (Reference-2); BPRO 54% and 73%. The PRO decks hold up and the BPRO decks are the soft ones, as you suspected.
- **Easy against both references** (the surest flags): 0-B-BPRO-1, 0-B-BPRO-2, 1-A-BPRO-1, 2-B-BPRO-3, 5-A-BPRO-1 to 5-A-BPRO-4, and 17-BPRO-1.

The road's reference player almost never beats Map 2's PROs. Your Ant Deck beats them about half the time and nearly always beats the BPROs. Three causes are visible:
1. **The rarity cap.** Map 2's fights may hold Uncommon at most, and its PRO / BPRO decks are commons and uncommons: Otter Guard, Pincushion Urchin, Grey Seal Bull, Conch Herald. Reference-2 brings Tunnel Ant (Rare) and Quicksand Queen (Very Rare, a reward of 18-A-9) at +10 levels. In the game it also brings its Legendary leader, Old Willow of the Ford, which the sims leave out (the harness has no player leader), so in play it is easier still.
2. **A thin pool.** The map's own pool is 15 cards (BPRO). Maps 3, 4 and 12 have 15 too, Map 17 has 14, and Maps 16 and 19 have 11. The BPRO decks are nearly the whole coastal pool, so the optimizer had little to choose from.
3. **Level 1 enemies.** Every PRO deck is Level 1 on every map, while both references level with the schedule. That matters little on Map 2 and a lot on the side maps (below).

## Side maps that open late
A side map counts as the map it opens on, but its PRO / BPRO decks were built under its own cap at Level 1. The Old Bramble (0-B, Common cap) counts as Map 17. Clovermead (1-A) counts as Map 12, the Sunken Grove (5-A) as Map 13, Smugglers' Grotto (2-B) as Map 7 and the Pale Desert (18-A) as Map 19. Their BPROs are the easiest fights in the file for both references. This is the structure, not a bad optimizer run. Whether to change it is a question for the owner (PREF-3).
