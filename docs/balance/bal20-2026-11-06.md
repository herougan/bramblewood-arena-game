## What walls The Canopy (and what does not)
`tools/bal20/diag_2026_11_06.js`, 100 games a variant, one factor at a time (`docs/balance/bal20/diag-2026-11-06.json` on the decks before BAL20; `diag-2026-11-06-part.json` on the Canopy decks after, with the field split).

- **The level is not the cause.** The Canopy opens after Map 18 (`shieldMap: 18`, entry after `md`), so its fights count as Map 18: the reference is Map 18's road deck at Lv 3.54, the enemy is at the schedule's Lv 4 (`roadEnemyScheduleLevel`, no `enemyLevel` override). That is the half-level gap ENG26 saw, the same every Map 18 fight has. Putting the enemy at the reference's level moves the Canopy fights by 0 to +6 points. Even the reference at Lv 10 wins 0-3% against 14-A-1 and 14-A-5. The schedule is a settled rule (BAL2-1), so no level was changed.
- **Not the leaders.** Taking the CPU's leader away makes the Canopy fights harder (14-A-2 31 -> 9%, 14-A-3 22 -> 6%). On Savanna Reaches it helps 14-5 (25 -> 41%) and 14-8 (24 -> 52%), which the tuner then used.
- **Not the Shield** (0 to +7 points at Shield 0).
- **The bramble is the main wall on 14-A-2, 14-A-3 and all of Savanna Reaches.** A plain 30-Health bramble: 14-A-2 31 -> 81%, 14-A-3 22 -> 66%, Savanna 14-1 42 -> 94%, 14-8 24 -> 71%.
- **The decks carry 14-A-1, 14-A-4 and 14-A-5.** With a plain bramble they stay at 3%, 27% and 5%; with the field off too 15%, 23% and 25%; with everything off (level, leader, Shield, field, bramble) 39%, 28% and 45%. The Canopy's decks are 0-1 Lumber swarms (Hoopoe Herald 2/12 for 0, Weaver Bird 3/6 Tangle, Pangolin Roller 5/5 Sturdy) that end the game in 5-7 rounds. `tools/bal20/cross_2026_11_06.js`: the 14-A-1 deck in a Savanna fight reads 14% where Savanna's own 14-1 deck reads 42%; Map 14's reference deck does no better than Map 18's against them.
- **K14 locks the decks.** The map-unique cards on these lists are exactly the strong ones (Hoopoe Herald, Weaver Bird, Pangolin Roller, Jaguar Stalker, Hercules Beetle, Kinkajou Lurker); the rest may hold 1 copy each, 6 in all. So the weakest legal deck is still a wall. That is decision BAL20-1.

## Method
1. `tools/bal20/road_tune_2026_11_06.js` (BAL19's tuner and its rules: the fight's own list, rarity cap, theme, no other fight's reward, size and first-clear cards kept, K14 no worse per card, Shield 0 to the rule, namesake leaders kept). One change: at 0-10% the reading is flat (BAL19 stopped at the start on 14-A-1, 14-A-4 and 14-A-5), so while a fight is more than 25 points too hard the score also counts the rounds your bramble lasts. Round 1 at 60-game re-reads (`docs/balance/bal20/tune`), round 2 from the applied round 1 at 120 (`tune-r2`). 12 fights (14-4 is in band).
2. `tools/bal20/confirm_2026_11_06.js`: each changed fight against the fight at HEAD, 200 games, road seeds; kept only if 2+ points nearer the target (`docs/balance/bal20/confirm-2026-11-06.json`). All 4 kept.
