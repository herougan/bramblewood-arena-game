# PVP1: 30 PvP test decks (2026-11-06)

Owner 2026-11-06 20:28 #5: "PVP: Create more varied decks (Slightly optimised) for my experimentation. Create 30 decks." Data: `canonical/pvp-test-decks.json` (raw results and the full win matrix: `docs/balance/pvp1-2026-11-06.json`). Tool: `node tools/pvp1/pvp1.js build --workers 2`, then `apply` (bakes them into the game) and `check`.

**In the game:** Camp > Simulator (Admin): each deck builder has a "PvP test deck" picker that loads one into Deck A or B (the Simulator plays without leaders). To play one yourself in PvP, paste its `code` from the JSON into Camp > Import; it becomes your deck with its leader.

**How:** each deck seeds 25 different in-play cards from its seed (up to 10 from its anchor tribe, the rest from the whole pool of 413; the last two are pure random), picks the best core of 7 (4 random cores + 8 swaps), fills from the other 18 (10 moves of 2 copies), then the best of the 16 valid leaders by successive halving. Search: 2 games an opponent a candidate, 29316 games in all (about 977 a deck); then 96 fresh games a deck for the gauntlet win. Games: Standard PvP: 20 cards, rarity copy caps and top-rank caps with the leader counted (bramblewood-ghosts.js validateDeck), every card at Level 1, the Standard ruleset (day / night cycle), brambles 15 Health, +1 Shield for the second seat, both seats the CPU (skill 1), leaders dealt (G.dealPlayerLeader), seats swapped every other game.

**Gauntlet (12):** Abyssal Aster (PvP stranger, stage 4); Frostbite Fern (PvP stranger, stage 5); Redwood Rook (PvP stranger, stage 5); Marching Mags (PvP stranger, stage 6); Gilded Juniper (PvP stranger, stage 6); Honeycomb Hazel (PvP stranger, stage 6); Reference-2 (the owner's Ant Deck) + Old Willow of the Ford; 20-PRO-1 (The Sundered Peak); 21-PRO-1 (The Quiet Summit); 22-PRO-1 (The Rimefire Caldera); 23-PRO-1 (The Goldfeather Siege); 24-PRO-1 (Goldfeather Keep).

**Variety:** the most copies any two decks share is 6 of 20 (limit 10).
