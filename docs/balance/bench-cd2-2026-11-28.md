# BENCH-CD2 (2026-11-28): what the CARDDESIGN2 redesigns do to card power and the road

Question: what would the eleven redesigns of `docs/design/carddesign2-2026-11-28.md` (decisions CARDDESIGN2-1..3) do to each card's power rating and to the Maps 0-20 road? No card data or game code changed. `tools/bench_cd2/bench.js` applies the doc's skills and Health changes only inside its own process and the processes it starts. Quillguard's L5 💀 Rend form (`rendAround`) is included. It needs CARDDESIGN2-2, because SKILLS3-3 holds 💀 forms off Maps 0-20. `bench.js verify` confirmed that every new skill fires in the engine.

## Card power (`tools/card_balance.js --v3 --only=<the 11>`, 400 games, same seeds, before and after)
Power is the mean of the basic and advanced win rates. Expected comes from the fit in the full report (`bal25/card-power.json`: 28.1 + 0.471 N). For budget = power - expected. The advanced opponents draw from that report's top pool, so the redesigned Shellwright and Marmot show up in other cards' advanced games too. That is why Quillguard's advanced rate moves even though its Level 1 card is unchanged (its basic rate is identical, 92.0).

Reading: two cards land within 5 of expected (Wolf +3.1, Sapper +3.9). Shellwright and Marmot come down a lot but stay well over (+21.6, +25.0). The four far-under cards (Willow, Hoarder, Yak, Vixen) stay 17-29 under. Gila, Leopard and Quillguard stay 19.7-34.3 over.

## CD2b refined proposals (2026-11-28)

The nine cards outside ±5 were reworked in `tools/bench_cd2/bench.js` (sets `r1a`..`r3b`, then `r4final`). Stats stay as the doc's (Yak 5/28, Sapper 0/14). Only skill numbers, the level a skill arrives at, or which built skill changed. Each rating is 400 games on card_balance v3's seeds (`power --variant=<set>`), with the settled rows of earlier rounds in the advanced pool. The power column is the final set's run: all 11 cards together, Wolf and Sapper as the doc. One round can test several candidates (up to 3).

| Card | Final proposal (Level 1 card unless a level is named) | Power vs expected (gap) | Rounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elder Willow | Forestry 1 + On Play 5 Shield and On Ready 6 Shield to every ally | 48.4 vs 51.0 (-2.5) | 3 |
| Shellwright Apprentice | On Play: Grant Armor 1 to an ally beside it | 42.3 vs 36.5 (+5.7; +5.0 measured alone) | 1 |

- Eight of the eleven land within 5. Shellwright sits at the line (+5.7). Armor 1 is the smallest Armor step, and the skill-less body rates -10.2.
- These two cannot land without a stat change:
  - Leopard: its skill-less 3/13 body already rates +12.2, and Ambush 1 rates +18.9.
  - Quillguard: the skill-less 2/18 body rates -6.5 to -7.2, and the smallest skill, Thorns 1, rates +14.7. With Thorns 1 and Rend both at L1 it rates +12.8, and with Rend alone at L1 -11.4.
- The power measure gives Lumber and card draw almost no value: the filler costs 0-1 and the side plays one card a turn. Hoarder (4 Lumber or 3 draws: under +1) and Vixen (2 Lumber: +0.3) only reached the line through a combat piece: the 💀 Shield, and Evasive plus the Wait.
- Marmot's facing-ally Shield at L1 rates +9.0 even at 1 Shield, so it waits for L3. The decision arrives at L3.
- Willow's forecast idea is diluted: the 6 Shield On Ready alone rates -23.3, so it also shields on play.
- Every candidate per round: `docs/balance/bench-cd2/refine-summary.json`.

### Road (Maps 0-20, paired vs today's cards, seeds 9100 + 37g, nice 19, 2 shards)
Screen: 100 games a fight. Fights beyond 2 SE got 400 more on games 100-499, which replace their screen.

**Final set** (`cd2b-r100`, `cd2b-r400`; 111 fights, 55 rerun, 27,600 paired games):
- Mean change in player win rate: **-4.5 ± 0.2 SE**.
- Curve fights in band (105): **82 → 63**. 23 leave, 4 enter.
- Most of it is Quillguard's L1 cut. 86 fights hold Quillguard only in the reference deck, below Level 3, so without Thorns; those fights average -5.4.
- 12-6: 55.3 → 16.3 (-39.0, target 60).
- 12-5: 62.3 → 27.8 (-34.5, target 66).
- 17-5: 65.5 → 82.3 (+16.8, target 70).
- Other large moves:
  - 12-4: +26.3 to 98.0 (enemy Marmot loses its On Play Shields below L3).
  - 11-A-4 boss: +20.5 to 61.8.
  - 18-4: -19.5.
  - 12-2: -17.7.
  - 12-7: +18.5.

**Alt** (`cd2b-alt-r100`, `cd2b-alt-r400`): the final set with Quillguard kept as the doc (Thorns 2; L5 💀 Rend). It separates Quillguard's share. 111 fights, 11 rerun, 14,400 paired games.
- Mean change: **-0.2 ± 0.1 SE**.
- In band: **84 → 77**. 8 leave, 1 enters.
- 12-5: 62.3 → 11.8 (-50.5).
- 12-6: 55.3 → 16.3 (-39.0).
- 17-5: 65.5 → 86.5 (+21.0).
- 11-A-4: 41.3 → 78.5 (+37.3).
- 12-2: -17.8.

Reading:
- The redesigns that bring under-budget cards up also make the CPU's Hoarder and Vixen stronger. That puts the Map 12 elites 35-50 points too hard under either Quillguard form.
- The Wolf and Sapper changes leave 17-5 too easy.
- Gila's Poison 1 makes the 11-A-4 boss much easier.
- If any of this is adopted, 12-2, 12-4, 12-5, 12-6, 17-5 and 11-A-4 need swap retunes.
- Quillguard's L1 cut fits the budget better but takes 5 points off 86 reference-deck fights. Keeping Thorns 2 at L1 leaves the road flat but the card +34 over.
