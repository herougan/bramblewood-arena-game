# Selfish boons: the library, their worth, where they are (package DF3, 2026-10-24)

A selfish boon is a field effect that helps the enemy side only (the CPU, side B). It is on while at least half the enemy deck as dealt shares
its theme, and it counts only the enemy's deck, so a player who copies the deck gains nothing. The Environment card is red-framed, its ribbon
says "Theirs" and its line gives the count ("On: 11 of 16 enemy cards share the theme"). Owner: DF-3 (10-23 17:34), narrowed by 22:23 #6
("only in challenge skirmishes ... 1 per 2 maps, or 1 per submap ... reused in similar biomes again and again or raids ... strong decks with
synergy with the field's effects") and 01:57 ("not too obvious and simple like +stats ... like blind cards do 2x dmg if most the cards
inside are blind").

The rules are in `bramblewood-engine.js` (`SELFISH_BOONS`, `selfishBoonCount`, the hooks marked "selfish boon"); the words are TX keys
`ui.df3-<id>-name` / `ui.df3-<id>`; the placements and their decks are made by `tools/df3_boons_2026_10_24.js`; the measurements are in
`df3-boons-2026-10-24.json` (this folder).

## The library and its worth by the 4-round rule

Yardstick (owner 10-23 20:44): +1 Attack or +1 Health held over a typical 4-round life. Worth is per unit the boon touches; "deck" is what a
typical challenge deck of 14-18 cards gets out of it.

| Boon | Theme (half the deck) | Who it helps | What it does | Worth (4-round logic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🦇 Blind Fury | Cave | every unit of theirs | a unit that fights blind (Blind, Purblind, or blind in the Darkness, by day for Sun-shy, at night for Night-blind) hits for 2x | in Darkness (1 in 4 miss) a unit's expected hit goes 0.75A to 1.5A: about +0.75 Attack per Attack point; a 2-Attack unit +1.5 Attack. Only where its units can't see, so a light source (Illuminate) turns it off |
| 🐜 Hive Chorus | Insect | Swarm units | Swarm counts every ally twice | +k Attack for k type-sharing allies; with 2 allies +2 Attack a Swarm unit |
| 🌊 Rising Tide | has Tide | Tide units | Quick and +1 Attack while the tide is in (Flood, High Tide; +1 Attack since BAL3, owner DF3-3) | +1 Attack plus Quick (a first strike, about +1 Attack in a trade): about +2 while the tide is in, which is about half the rounds, so +1 (+2 under River Rapids); Quick alone was +0.5 |
| 🕳️ Second Burrow | Rodents, hedgehogs, rabbits, moles, worms, badgers | the burrowers | the first death sends it back to their hand (once a card) | a second life for the card, less a play and its Lumber: about 2/3 of the card's points, once |
| 🐗 Wild Rage | has Rage or Berserk | Rage units | Rage wakes below 3/4 Health (was 1/2) | about one more round of doubled Attack: +A/4 a round, +1 Attack for a 3-4 Attack unit |
| 🌨️ Snow Veil | Tundra, Taiga or Frost | Evasive units | Evasive dodges 2 in 3 (was 1 in 2) | single-target hits taken fall from 1/2 to 1/3: about +50% Health against them, +2-3 Health on a 5-Health runner |
| 🏜️ Sand Veil | Desert | Desert units | in a Sandstorm / Glass-storm attacks at them miss 1 in 2 (was 1 in 4) | hits taken 3/4 to 1/2: +50% Health, +3-4 on an 8-Health unit; nothing without the storm |
| 💀 Restless Dead | Undead | Revive units | Revive brings it back at full Health (was 1) | +(Health - 1) once: about +9 Health on a Cinder Skeleton |
| 🌋 Ember-born | Volcanic | Volcanic units | immune to heat: lava, Scorching, fire arrows | the volcano's every-round hazards deal about 1 a round to a unit: +2-4 Health over 4 rounds, more against a fire-arrow deck |
| 🏰 Siege Train | has Siege | Siege units | Siege counts double | +N damage to the bramble and Structures for Siege N: +N/2 Attack |
| 🐍 Venom Glands | Reptile | Poison units | its Poison gives twice the Poison | +N Poison a hit for Poison N: about +1 Attack for Poison 1-2 over 4 rounds |
| 🦅 Sky Ambush | has Flying | Flyers | Quick | a first strike before arrows, bolts and non-Quick units: about +1 Attack a Flyer |

None is a plain +stats: each needs a deck built around its theme, and most need the field too (Darkness for Blind Fury, the tide for Rising
Tide, the Sandstorm for Sand Veil, the volcano for Ember-born). Each is reusable in any biome of its kind and in raids (Edit skirmish and the
raid editor list them under "Selfish boons (the enemy only)").

## Where they are

Only challenge skirmishes (`node.challenge`, a "⚔️ Challenge" pill in the fight panel) carry one: never a normal skirmish, an elite, a
mini-boss or a boss. On the main road one every two maps from Map 5 (not Maps 7, 11 and 14, which DF-4 retunes without boons), and one on
each side map from Map 5 that has skirmishes (not the Eye of the Volcano, elites only, or the Canopy, whose pool has no skill family deep
enough for half a deck). The challenge target is the fight's road-curve target minus 10 points (owner call DF3-1); where the deck alone
could not get there, the fight's Enemy level goes one up (owner call DF3-2). The measured list (with and without the boon) is in
`df3-boons-2026-10-24.json` and in the package report.
