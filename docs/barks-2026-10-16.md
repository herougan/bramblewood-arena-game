# Voice barks (2026-10-16)

Every card that speaks in a fight, with its lines. Edit the lines here or in the card editor (More → Voice lines); the game reads them from `barks` on each card in `canonical/cards.json`.

**Rules.** At most 6 words a line, shown about 1.6 s above the card, one bubble on the board at a time, one bark per card per round.
On play about 1 in 3, the killer on a kill about 1 in 2, on death about 1 in 3, on a skill only for leaders and bosses (about 1 in 2), idle only when you hover the card in the Codex or a deck pool (about 1 in 2).
Leaders and bosses (a Leader or King card, a leader on the board, a boss) always speak on arrival, on their first kill and on death.
Settings → Voice lines: On / Leaders only (the default) / Off. With Leaders only, the other cards below stay quiet until you pick On.
Lines in `*stars*` are sounds (wild animals, dragons, objects), shown in italics.

Each bark is read out as a short Animal-Crossing-style babble in the animal's voice (high and quick for small animals, low and slow for big ones). It follows the Voice and Sound sliders and is silent when either is 0.
