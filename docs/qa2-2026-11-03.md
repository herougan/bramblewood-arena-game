## Ranked findings

### Blocker
None. A fresh offline player can get through the opening, both tutorials, Maps 0-1 and the start of Map 2 without a crash or a dead end.

### Major

**M1. The Camp (deck editor) can be skipped, and then you cannot get back to it until Map 1 is fully cleared.**
- Steps: win 0-1 and 0-2, then keep pressing "Next: ..." on the result screen through 0-4. You land on Map 1 (Thistlewood).
- The Camp spot (`deck`, Map 0, after 0-1/0-2) only unlocks when you tap it on Map 0. Nothing points the player at it. The result screens and Home's Play button go straight on to the next fight.
- On Map 1 there is no way back: `body.world-hidden` hides the map list and the ‹ › edge buttons (`display:none`) until Map 1 is fully cleared (`worldRevealed`).
- So a player who follows the game's own Next buttons fights the Map 1 boss with the starter deck. The defeat screen then says "Try building a new deck around one plan…" and the player has no deck editor.
- Screens: `screens/qa2/21-map1-no-way-back-to-camp-1366.png`, `06-defeat-0-2-deck-advice-before-deck-editor-1366.png`.

**M2. Early reward packs hand out the same card again and again.**
- After Map 0: four "Level N: A card" level-up packs and the first two packs gave House Cat 6 times. Rivergate faction packs gave Shell-Cracker ×2 and Rivergate Recruit ×2, then ×2 again. Rivergate Recruit reached 5 copies, and the deck holds 1 copy of each card.
- Likely cause: level packs use `purchasePackOnce(..., {newFirst:true, gated:true})`. The gate leaves almost no cards that early, so "new first" cannot find another new card and repeats the one card left.
- Faction rewards are meant to be new-first too (REWARD_NEW_FIRST, RW5), but they also gave duplicates while new Rivergate cards were left.
- Screens: `09-faction-pack-duplicates-1366.png`, `10-open-all-summary-new-card-counted-twice-1366.png`, `11-level-packs-all-house-cat-1366.png`.

**M3. Pelican Point (2-4) is a hard wall for a starter deck, and Map 2 is a straight line.**
- 2-4 lost 3 times out of 3 on CPU Auto, dealing 2, 0 and 0 bramble damage (enemy bramble 15 + 1 Shield; 48, 77 and 42 damage taken).
- The Old Shell (2-5) then lost 0-17.
- 2-4 is required for 2-5, and Map 3 (where the Nest opens) is behind both, so the player cannot go around it.
- For comparison, the 2-SK and 2-WK reference decks won easily (11 and 15 HP left).
- Before this, Map 0-1 had been close: 0-4 won with 2 HP, 0-2 lost once, the Thistle Baron 1-5 lost once, Gull Gang 2-2 lost once.
- Screen: `20-pelican-point-2-4-wall-1366.png`.

**M4. "Win your first match" stays at 0/1 after about 20 road wins.**

- Two other achievement names are misleading. "First Steps in the Forge" and "Forge Regular" pop after 0-1 and 1-3 because duplicate pulls level cards, but the Forge is Map 11. "Campaign Cleared" pops for clearing Map 0.
- Screens: `32-achievements-first-victory-0-after-road-wins-1366.png`, `07-map0-end-result-overflows-campaign-cleared-1366.png`.

**M6. A Level 5 pack card showed as a raw id.**
- The pack summary read "bramble-sprout@lv1 undefined NEW!".
- `@lvN` ids are enemy-only match overlay cards (`matchDefsOverlay`). The level pack was paid at a fight's end while that overlay was live, and the pack pool seems to have picked from it.
- The collection held no `@lv` copy afterwards, so the card was either lost or never real. The text was captured but the screenshot was not.
- Repro idea: reach a new player Level at the end of a fight against `@lv` enemies, then open the level pack.

## Rewards and pacing (as seen)
- First clear: 40 Maple Leaves + 20 Magic Dust + 1 Kibble, plus a card or pack. Map 0 end: Hedgerow Pouch + Rivergate Pack. Level-ups (Levels 1-6 by Map 2): one card each.
- After Map 0 (5 fights) the player had 360 gold and 160 dust. At the 2-4 wall: 850 gold, 453 dust, Level 6, 48 cards collected (with many duplicates, see M2).
- Energy: 20 max; fights cost 1⚡ on Map 0 and 2-3⚡ by Map 2. Level-ups refilled it twice. The first wall came after Map 2's third fight, about 20 fights in, with 2⚡ left for a 3⚡ fight. The "not enough energy" bubble is clear, and the popover offers "+5 free Energy" once a day.
- Rough fight lengths on CPU Auto on this machine: 60-130 s for a 5-8 round fight, 200-340 s for a 9-14 round fight.

## First 30 minutes (narrative)
1. The splash is handsome and clear: one name field and "Enter the Grove", and you don't need an account.
2. The faction pick ("Do you support…") explains both sides, though the left preview panel slides under the dialog.
3. **Tutorial I** is five rounds. It is well paced: one tip at a time, drag a card, the enemy Bramble, initiative.
4. Its result calls itself "Tutorial Complete!" and shows the three cards won with padlocks, which reads as if they were still locked.
5. "Your first deck" then deals 11 nameless cards, and "Add to deck" flies them into a box. This is very slow here.
6. **Tutorial II** brings the Wandering Traveller as your leader and wins in four rounds. Its result again says "Tutorial Complete!", a second top-up window follows, and two lines of "training is over" end the tutorial.
7. **Map 0** is four fights, each a little harder: one Wait tip, then Lumber and the Graveyard, which you learn by being told twice. 0-2 was lost once. Each Next button sends you straight into the next fight, so I never saw the Camp spot that unlocked behind me.
8. Finishing Map 0 pays a burst: two pack queues, four level packs and a new map. The packs are almost all House Cats and Rivergate Recruits.
9. **Map 1** begins with no way back to Map 0 and no deck editor. Its boss beat the starter deck once before falling on the second try.
10. Only then does the world open. You can return to Map 0, find the Camp (a short, warm dialogue) and edit your deck. The deck editor is clear: right-click to remove, click to add, "Save deck".
11. **Map 2** brings Tide and crabs. Energy runs out three fights in. At Pelican Point the starter deck stops dead: three heavy losses, the Map 2 boss behind it, and the Nest on Map 3 behind that.
12. On a real device all of this would take roughly 30-40 minutes. The first real frustration comes at minute 25-30: no deck editor (M1), duplicate rewards (M2) and then a wall (M3).
