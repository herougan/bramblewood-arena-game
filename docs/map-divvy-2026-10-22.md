# The map divvy (package DV, 2026-10-22)

Owner, 00:43: "Assist me to now divvy creatures to each map", with thirteen rules (quoted where each is used below), plus at 00:51 a per-map card
pool file for the new map editor. This pass places every main map's creatures, sets the rarity ladder the road follows, re-ranks the cards that
were in the way, rebuilds the enemy decks from the pools, re-gates the side maps and moves the rewards.

Made with `node tools/map_divvy_2026_10_22.js plan` (the record: `docs/balance/map-divvy-2026-10-22.json`, every rarity change, every source
move in RP's format, every deck before and after, the side-map gates), applied with `python3 tools/map_divvy_apply_2026_10_22.py` (cards) and
`node tools/map_divvy_2026_10_22.js decks` (decks, gates, showcases), checked with `node tools/map_divvy_2026_10_22.js check` (all pass, run on
the final files). The pools are in **`canonical/map-pools.json`** (`node tools/map_divvy_2026_10_22.js pools`), in the coordinator's shape:
`maps.<mapId>.cards` is the map's pool, `special` the cards one fight needs outside it (named bosses, the raid bosses' own decks), `submaps.<id>`
each side map's new cards plus the others its fights use. Every card in every deck is in its map's or side map's lists (checked).

**"The Dragon Lair"** is the Volcano Lair (`mx`, the Caldera Wyrm's lair off Map 20). **"The other submap"** is the Eye of the Volcano (`me`),
Map 20's other side map. **Map 20's strongest submap** (rule 10's faction Very Rare) is taken to be the Volcano Lair, for its worldboss.

The ladder is the game's own now (`arena_app.js`, `ROAD_RARITY` block): `RARITY_INTRO` (Common 0, Uncommon 1, Rare 5, Very Rare 10, Super Rare
20, Epic and Heroic by Map 20's side maps, then **proposals**: Unique 30, Legendary 40, Mythic 55, Ancient 70, Divine 90). A skirmish's cap is
Common to Map 1, Uncommon 2-4, Rare 5-9, Very Rare 10-19, Super Rare 20-29, Epic 30-39, Heroic 40-49, Unique 50-59, Legendary 60-69, Mythic 70+;
an elite one step more and a boss two, never past what is revealed (so Map 1's skirmishes are Common and its last match, an elite, can hold an
Uncommon; bosses on Maps 22-24 can field Epic and Heroic; bosses from Map 40 field Legendaries). This is rule 13's "slower": a new rarity every
4-5 maps early, then every 10, and the low rarities stay in every deck.

What hands cards out follows it too: a basic-pack card comes out of a pack or the base track only once its map is reached and its rarity
revealed (`packCardReached`); the faction track has its own gates (below).
