## Your questions, my suggestions
- **The same leader for both:** already true (the Wandering Traveller), so nothing to change.
- **Two shared cards:** **Beaver Lumberjack** (+2 Lumber) and **Courier Pigeon** (draw 1). Both are neutral utility already in the base pool, and they teach the two economy ideas (Lumber from cards, card draw) to both sides.
- **Giving each side the other's cards gradually:**
  - Each first clear on the first two maps (Bramblewood Outskirts and Thistle Fields, 10 fights) also gives one of the rival faction's Basics. Strongest last, so the early fights still feel like your faction.
  - By the end of map 2 you own the rival's whole set.
  - Every rival card still shows "Not owned" in the Codex until it lands, and the reward screen already announces it.
  - Optional: the first Nest egg (map 4) hatches from the rival pool if anything is still missing.
- **Balance while you reorganise:** whichever Hummingbird cards you keep, B6's numbers apply. Roughly −1 Attack on the five 3-Attack flyers, or Reach on two Otter ground units, gets the mirror near 50%.
