# Accessibility audit, 2026-11-16 (package A11Y2, alpha checklist U7)

Method: Playwright on the built `index.html`, 1280 and 390 px, light and dark; axe-core (wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21aa, best-practice) on splash, home, conquest map, node panel, Camp deck editor, Codex, shop, settings, a pack opening (390, sealed) and a fight (390). Own checks: a Tab walk of 25 stops on five tabs (a visible ring on every stop), controls without a name, dialog role / focus-in / Escape / focus-return, `prefers-reduced-motion` (running infinite animations), text contrast on solid backgrounds. Not walked by keyboard: a live battle's board (hand tiles have `role=button`, name and `tabindex`; empty slots are driven by the existing Enter / arrow keyboard play), the results screen end to end (code and DOM only).

Already good (no change): no unnamed button or link on any screen walked; no axe contrast violation; `bramblewood-a11y.js` traps Tab, sets `aria-modal`, names dialogs from their heading, closes on Escape and gives focus back (settings, card dialogs, splash, pack opening all behave; splash / pack opening / VS intros are deliberately not Escape-closable); reduced motion already stops the Codex, shop and deck ambience (candles, dust, lamps); toasts are `role=status`; `html lang` follows the language.

## U7 verdict

**Pass with open items.** Keyboard reach, ring visibility, accessible names, dialog roles, Escape and focus return, reduced motion and axe are clean on every screen walked, after the fixes above (2 High, 4 Med, 6 Low fixed). Not yet a full pass for a screen-reader player in a fight: items 13 and 14 (no battle announcements; the board by keyboard unverified) are open, and 15 / 16 need the owner's tint call and a manual contrast pass on image backgrounds. Fix 13 first.

Test: `tests/a11y2-2026-11-16.js` (67 checks: ring and badge tokens, landmark, h1, skip link, names on four tabs at both themes, Escape, focus return, ring colour, reduced motion). Shots: `docs/screens/a11y2/`.
