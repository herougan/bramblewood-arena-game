## How this was done, and what it can and cannot tell you
- Built locally, driven with Playwright (headless Chromium) using the `vis3`/`vis5` helpers (`tests/e2e/`): a fixed 3 v 3 board, each animated event fired through `renderVfxForEvent` (or the real function: `playCardByUid`, `discardCardByUid`, `leaderThroneVfx`, `deathVfx`), game time slowed 10x and 6-8 screenshots stamped with game ms. Scripts: `tools/anim1/anim1_frames.py` (frames), `run_calm.py` (Low / reduced-motion runs), `event_coverage.py` and `kind_coverage.py` (which engine events have no front-end handler), `art_sheets.py`, `art_lists.py`, `art_doc.py`, `art_animals.py`.
- Frames are in `docs/screens/anim1/anim1-<moment>-<mode>-<width>.png` (mode `high`, `low`, `rm` = reduced motion at FX High; widths 390 and 1366). All 18 moments at High, both widths. Low: 390 only (bloom, brood, death, wind, hit, leader, remember). Reduced motion: 390 leader only. The other Low / reduced-motion runs timed out under load and were stopped; the Low and reduced-motion behaviour for the rest comes from the real-time probe (nodes left, 1366) and the code, not frames.
- **Limits (honest):** (1) the machine was at load average about 21 the whole session, so durations I measured in real time are upper bounds, and some Low / reduced-motion runs did not finish (the page timed out on screenshots under load); what is missing is listed in section "Not covered". (2) Synthetic events show the effect but not a whole round, so an attack's lunge and projectile were not judged in a real replay: the hit sheets show the damage number and impact decals only. Judge hits from a real round (the `card-play` sheet at 1366 starts one) before changing them. (3) Nothing here is a pass/fail check; nothing was run as a test. Page errors: none in any run.
