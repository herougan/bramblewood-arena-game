# REL1 audit: the Release 1.0 path, player's eye (2026-11-19)

Scope (owner 2026-11-19 00:02): tutorial to Map 20 end, Maps 0-20 and their submaps, the first daily dungeon, the first multiplayer dungeon, the first raid, PvP. Audit only: no game code, texts or data were changed.

## How it was done, and what it does not cover
- Build of `origin/batch-1023e` (index.html, 18.4 MB), Playwright, 390x844 and 1440x900, light mode; dark mode at 1440 only (codex, shop, home).
- Tutorial T-1 and T-2 were played from a clean save by hand (tap to place cards, coach tips clicked through). Everything later used a seeded save (`loadConquestProgress` completed list + `unlockFeature`, "Maps 0-N cleared"), the game's own Auto-play, and direct calls (`startConquestMatch`, `pl2OpenCoopPop`, the Arena cards).
- The sandbox is slow (software GL, 2 cores) and offline: screenshots use `animations: disabled`, so lag, doubled effects and timing were NOT judged. Supabase calls were refused, so the "Sign in" banner and shop state may differ for a signed-in player.
- Not done: levelling the Forge by hand, a real map-clear to the next map's unlock, the Effects Lab (`tools/effects-lab`, no `effects.html` at the repo root; not opened), live two-player co-op/PvP, Maps 11-20 fights beyond one seeded fight, touch gestures (all taps were mouse clicks), languages other than English. Hover-triggered card pop-ups sat over the board in two raid shots only because the test pointer rested on a card; not counted.
- Shots: `docs/release/rel1/<name>.jpg` (390 shots end in `-390`, desktop ones in `-1440`; desktop shots are scaled to 1100 px wide).

## Counts
P1: 3. P2: 14. P3: 8. VFX candidates: section 5.

## 1. First launch and tutorial

**P1 - Tutorial win screen: the only button is off-screen on a phone, and new-card badges hide the names.** Step: T-1 won, 390. The "Tutorial Complete!" panel stacks 3 CARDS WON, then NEW CARDS SEEN (9 more tiles), then the unlocks; "Claim Rewards" sits at y~1075 on an 844 px screen. The NEW!/GET! stickers cover the name plates ("Rivergate Recr", "Shell-Crack"), and a ghost label overlaps the third card. Why: it is the first reward a new player ever gets, the tutorial gate keeps everything else locked, and the CTA is below the fold with nothing hinting at scrolling. Shots: `tut-win-a-390.jpg`. Fix: pin the claim button to the bottom of the panel, show the 3 won cards in a row, collapse "new cards seen" to a count unless tapped, and move the stickers to a corner.

**P2 - Side-pick screen is shifted left at 390.** Text "One river-kingdom, one sky-court..." is clipped at the left edge and a dark stripe runs down the left 40 px; the panel measured x=-27. The splashIn animation may still be moving; re-check after the animation ends. Shot: `tut-c-390.jpg`. Fix: make the entrance animation end at `transform:none` and check overflow; test after 5 s.

**P2 - After T-1 the new player lands on a cold "Your first deck" step with 20 near-identical tiles.** `tut-after-claim`: 11 tiles of cards, an "Add to deck" button, and a toast-like "+250 XP / Level 2" pair of lines in the page flow. It works, but the XP and level-up moment is plain text lines, not a moment. Shots: `tut-after-claim-390.jpg`, `firstdeck-390.jpg`. Fix: one short level-up flourish (ring fill + sound) before the deck step; keep the deck step to "These 20 go with you. Add".

**P2 - First map view is mostly empty.** After T-2, Map 0 shows a tutorial marker, 0-1 with a glowing ring, three padlocked nodes and an NPC dialogue ("Training is over") with a 23 s countdown ring and "I'm ready." Clear, but the player is not told that 0-1 costs Energy, or what 0-2 and 0-3 unlock (the Camp). Shot: `map0-clean-390.jpg`. Fix: one line on the node panel: "Win 0-1 and 0-2 to open your Camp".

**P2 - Splash shows a hover tip on a phone.** "Tip: Hover a card on the board to see where it will strike." appears on the 390 splash and the faction screen. Touch players cannot hover. Fix: tip text per input type (`matchMedia('(hover:none)')`).

**P2 - Page weight 18.4 MB.** The built `index.html` is 18.4 MB with inline assets; in the sandbox the first screenshot timed out at 120 s (software GL, so exaggerated). For a first visit on mobile data this is the first impression. Fix: not for this audit; measure real first paint on a throttled 4G profile and split art out.

**P3 - Splash at 1440 is strong but the left column wraps the title to two lines.** `first-splash-1440.jpg`. Fine; the name field is focused and the primary button is obvious.

What already works here: the hand tour is four short coach tips (hand, play, castle, combat) and "Drag a card onto the field" also works with a tap; the tutorial fight resolves in 3-4 rounds; T-2 introduces the Wandering Traveller as a leader with a dialogue and a leader-slot tip; the faction pick has a clear Random option.

## 2. The road, Maps 0-20 and side maps

**P1 - The guest "Sign in to keep your progress" banner covers the bottom of every fight.** At 1440 it sits over the lower hand row and Pass; at 390 it covers the hand cards in a raid fight and the co-op fight. It reappears on every screen. Why: the hand is the game; a guest (everyone in the first session) loses the bottom third of the playfield. Shots: `fight-start-1440.jpg`, `coop-fight-1440.jpg`, `vfx-1-1440.jpg`. Fix: hide the banner while `matchState` is live (show it on the results screen), and make "It's okay" persist for the session.

**P2 - Guests see "Sign in to open packs: it's free" in the shop while the splash says "No account needed to play".** Shop at 390 and 1440 (dark): the sign-in box replaces the buy flow for packs; the pack items under it are visible. Free map-reward packs did open without sign-in (pack tear and reveal worked). Possibly sandbox-only (Supabase refused); verify signed out online. Open design question: should Pack A on Map 5 be buyable by guests? Shots: `tab-shop-390.jpg`, `dark-shop-1440.jpg`.

**P2 - Fight start is slow to read.** Tap Fight to a playable board passes through the map veil, a typed title card ("Gatorbac...") and a dialogue; in the sandbox it took 4-5 s (inflated by software GL). Shot: `prefight-1440.jpg`. Fix: let the title card skip on tap and cap the hold at 1 s on repeat visits.

**P2 - Node panel is dense on a phone.** 0-1 panel at 390: title, three tags, a first-clear block, "Possible drops: 2% / 1% B", six card tiles, and "THIS MAP'S CARDS" with eight "?" and names. The drop line and rank emblem follow the house style; the length is the issue. Shot: `node-0-1-390.jpg`. Fix: collapse "This map's cards" behind a tap on a phone.

**P2 - Energy pacing needs an owner look.** At Map 5, one fight is 5 Energy of a 20 cap; the top-bar timer read about 02:30 to 02:56 for the next point, i.e. about 4 fights per full bar and roughly 12 minutes per fight refilled (sandbox clock; not a UI bug). A player at Maps 5-10 will hit "no energy" inside the first session. Open design question for the owner: cost per fight by map, cap, and the first-session buffer.

**P2 - Deck editor on a phone puts charts before the cards.** 390: leader and bramble tiles, name, Details, two graphs, and only then "Your Loadout"; the help line says "Shift+click (or right-click, or its - corner)", which a phone has none of. Shots: `deck-editor-390.jpg`, `tab-deck-390.jpg`. Fix: list first, charts folded; touch wording for the remove gesture.

**P2 - Forge intro dialogue stacks two bubbles over the sub-tabs.** 1440: two "Cobble, the Forge Golem" bubbles overlap the Camp / Forge / Nest / Inventory tabs, and an "Achievement!" toast covers the level medal. Shot: `d-forge-1440.jpg`. Fix: dock the NPC bubble below the sub-nav and queue toasts behind it.

**P3 - A tooltip "Home safe, for now." floats at the left edge on Codex and Shop (dark, 1440).** Stale speech bubble that outlives its screen. Shots: `dark-codex-1440.jpg`, `dark-shop-1440.jpg`.

**P3 - Traveller dialogue sits over the map title** ("Only Heroic rarity cards and above can be leader cards." over "Gatorback Swamp"). Shot: `map-1440.jpg`.

**P3 - Map free pack is named "Level 10: Tidemire Chest".** Reads like a player level. Shot: `pack-torn-390.jpg`.

**P3 - A card-swap sheet ("Good Choice! Swap Crossed-Eyes for House Cat?") persists across tabs on a phone** and sat over Arena and Raid until dismissed (seeded Map 0 state, so it may not occur in normal play). Shots: `play-arena-390.jpg`, `raid-detail-390.jpg`.

Data point, not a finding: an Auto-played 0-1 with the post-tutorial deck ended in "Defeat! F" (`f01-end-390.jpg`). The defeat quip is good ("Eureka! I have found another way to lose"). Whether a real first fight is winnable by hand was not measured.

What already works: the map view with a left map list and dashed paths at 1440 (`map-1440.jpg`); the node panel with a rank hex, "Fight again 5 energy", a one-line drop chance (`map-node-panel-1440.jpg`); the Victory screen with a rank hex, rewards, "Next: <fight>" as the primary button (`win-b-1440.jpg`); pack tear and a three-card reveal (`pack-reveal3-390.jpg`); the Codex album at 1440 and its dark mode (`d-codex-1440.jpg`, `dark-codex-1440.jpg`); Forge layout with an empty-state anvil (`d-forge-1440.jpg`); home hub after the tutorial with Play, Camp, Achievements badges (`home-after-t-390.jpg`).

## 3. Daily dungeon, multiplayer dungeon, raid, PvP

**P1 - The first daily dungeon cannot be found.** The Dungeons tab is hidden from players (DAILY_DUNGEONS_HIDDEN, plus `PL2`). Murgle Mayhem is a small labelled node beside 6-4 on Map 6 that opens only after Map 10 is cleared, so the entrance is on a map the player left four maps ago; nothing on Home, Arena or the Map 10 clear points back. At 390 its label overlaps the frog sprite. Inside, the 50 floors are near-black padlocks and the "Way out" label sits on a floor node. Shots: `map6-dungeon-entry-390.jpg`, `murgle-map-390.jpg`. Fix: a one-time toast and a Home/Arena card ("Murgle Mayhem is open: 3 keys today") when Map 10 clears, plus lighter floor nodes. Open question: is Murgle the only daily dungeon in 1.0 (Undercroft and Long Burrow are hidden)?

**P2 - Co-op dungeon: works solo, but the fight HUD is crowded.** Opened from the Map 16 spot ("The Tidelock Vault", Open a room / Play with CPU allies / Join). Lobby: five parts, front row you, an Empty seat for the CPU ally, "Start the climb" (the start button was not clickable through normal Playwright clicks, an unstable element; JS click worked, likely re-render churn, worth a check). In the fight: the "Reserve" label is clipped at the top-left, the -5 energy floater overlaps the speed button, the Part 1/5 strip sits under the HUD buttons, and the enemy bramble shows a flat castle emoji instead of art. Shots: `coop-1440.jpg`, `coop-lobby-1440.jpg`, `coop-fight-1440.jpg`. Fix: give the Dripping Roost bramble real art, move the part strip to its own row, clip-check the Reserve label.

**P2 - Raid fight prep: the sticky "Start the fight" button covers the boss ability text.** At 390 the page is long (three-row side panel with BR numbers, boss, ability) and the button hides "Tentacle Smack...". Raid hub and detail are clear (49% health left, tickets 10/10, Fight cost with ticket and Energy icons). Shots: `play-raid-390.jpg`, `raid-detail-390.jpg`, `raid-fight-a-390.jpg`. Fix: pad the page bottom by the button height.

**P2 - PvP first match drops straight onto the board.** Standard PvP opens a ghost fight against "Cinder Marlow" with an ARENA ticket banner over the top lane and a dialogue bubble ghosting over the enemy bramble. The rating ("Heartwood 1, 1500") and tickets (10/10) are on the lobby with no explanation. Shots: `d-arena-1440.jpg`, `pvp-standard-1440.jpg`. Fix: one-line explainer on the first visit; clear the dialogue before the board.

**P3 - Raid detail teases Map 41** ("Win a fight on Map 41 or further to learn this") on a 1.0 screen. Shot: `raid-detail-390.jpg`. Fix: hide environment rows that are not in 1.0.

What already works: the Arena hub tiles (Collection PvP, Standard PvP, Today's field with environment cards) at both widths; the co-op lobby layout with part strip and seats; the Raid hub card with health bar.

## 5. Visual effects

Method limits: no Effects Lab session, no animation timing (see top). This section comes from the live board shots plus `docs/effects-inventory-2026-10-15.json` (1069 rows, statuses refreshed 2026-11-18): 442 live rows rated good, 105 live "basic", 60 live "missing".

Weak or missing on the 1.0 path (inventory ratings, priority 1 first; not re-checked in play):
- P1 moments rated basic or missing: ranged projectile (an emoji sliding across, `atk-ranged-projectile`), Explode (`sk-explode`), Mind Control (`sk-mind-control`, the unit just reappears), Player Stun (`sk-stun-player`, no cue for a skipped turn), Exile from board (`sk-exile-board`), Poison applied and Bleed applied (`st-poison-apply`, `st-bleed-apply`: a number only).
- P2 rows that new players meet by Map 10: shield soak and shield break (`sk-shield-soak`, `sk-shield-break`: no flare, no shatter), generic Heal (a number only), evade variants (flying, fog, shadow, smoke all share one hop), Decay (no look at all), Spell card cast, Equip (nothing on screen).
- Seen in play: damage types magic, water and true have no light colour (inventory); the board cards' status badges are small at 1440 but readable.
- Candidates for new VFX: a shield-shatter burst; a poison/bleed drip on apply; a "turn skipped" frozen-clock over the stunned player's hand; a banish swirl for Exile; map ambience for the swamp maps (fireflies exist; no water ripple under the board); UI feedback for level-up (ring fill + sound) and for "Claim" (coin burst) on the tutorial win screen.
- Doubled / laggy: not assessed here (no timing).
