# UI/UX review — 2026-10-03 (D16, open item)

**Status:** open. Nothing here is built yet; this is the list to pick from.

**How the screenshots were taken:**
- The live build in headless Chromium, with shader effects on.
- 1366×860 desktop and 390×844 phone.
- A guest player at level 2–7, Maps 1–2 cleared.
- The battle is a real Conquest skirmish (Map 1), with screenshots taken at turns 1, 4 and 8 and at the result.

Images live in `docs/ui-review/2026-10-03/`.

**The short version:**
- The art is now the best thing about the game.
- The screens around the art still look like forms: brown slabs, text walls and dead space.
- The battle screen is functional but doesn't say "lane battle".
- My strongest pushback:
  1. Stop adding modes and currencies until the core screens are fixed.
  2. Make the Play column wider instead of narrower.

**What works:**
- Card art reads well at this size.
- The speech bubbles ("Right at you!", "That one stung!") give it personality.
- The win screen is clear: rank, rewards, unlock, then stats.

**Problems, worst first:**
1. **The battle doesn't fit the screen.** At 1366×860 a panel peeks in at the very bottom; that's the Battle Log, below the fold. On phone the log sits under everything. Either fit the whole battle to the viewport, or make the log a drawer.
2. **There are no visible lanes.** The tagline says "lane by lane", but the board is two rows of cards packed to the centre. By turn 8 there are 9 cards a row and nothing tells you which card faces which. Proposal: faint lane columns (or slot outlines) on the felt, and attack lines during resolution.
3. **Coach-mark tooltips cover the fight.** "Flying…" and "Poison…" pop up mid-resolution over the enemy row, with a "Got it" you have to click. On phone the tooltip covers half the board. Proposal:
   - queue coach marks to the start of your turn;
   - make them a dismiss-on-tap chip at the edge, never over the board.
4. **The primary action is unclear.** The only button is "⏭ Skip", bottom-right, and the Graveyard sits in the prime centre spot. Proposal:
   - a clear **End turn** button (it's what Skip does after you've played);
   - Graveyard as a small pile next to the deck.
5. **Both castles are tiny.** Each castle is a 110px tile in a corner with an 8px HP bar. HP is the score of the game, so it should be the biggest number on screen: a big HP bar across the top for the enemy and across the bottom for you.
6. **The top-right is empty, and the HUD is cramped.** Speed, ⤢, ⚙, Forfeit and Quit sit in one cramped cluster. Forfeit and Quit side by side is risky; put Forfeit behind ⚙.
7. **Hand cards don't show cost and wait clearly.** Cost and wait are a tiny badge top-left; unaffordable cards are only greyed. Proposal: a cost gem that turns red when you can't afford it, and the current lumber (🪵) shown next to the hand.
8. **The result screen has 4 buttons.** "1: Next Battle!", Play Again, Back to board, Quit. "Back to board" and "Quit" read the same. Proposal: **Next battle** (primary), Replay, Map.

## Conquest

![Map](ui-review/2026-10-03/08-conquest-map.jpg)
![World atlas](ui-review/2026-10-03/09-conquest-world.jpg)
![Phone](ui-review/2026-10-03/31-phone-conquest.jpg)

- **Pushback on the margins change (done as asked).** Conquest now uses the same column as Arena, and the map got smaller: about 520px of map and a lot of empty brown below it. I'd rather **widen the Play column for every sub-tab** (Arena's cards would look better wider too) than shrink the map. ⛶ helps, but the default view is what most people see.
- **Phone:** the trail runs as one horizontal row in a tall canvas. Phones want a vertical trail, top to bottom, like the World atlas.
- **The World atlas works:** diamonds, progress and "you are here".

- **Arena:** six identical brown slabs, so nothing says "start here". Quick Battle should be the hero. The rest should be smaller, and Dungeon's slate colour should match the rest.
- **Raid:** the information is right but dense. The "Fight (1🎫 4⚡)" button has two currencies in it. The "Coming up" cards are blurred to the point of noise; use silhouettes plus a name instead.
- **Autobattler:** a rules list and a small button in a wide empty panel. Show a picture of a run (castle → picks → fights) and a big Start.

## Across the whole game

1. **Too many currencies (my strongest pushback).** Visible today:
   - Maple Leaves, Gold Leaves, Magic Dust, Metal;
   - Energy, tickets 🎫, Raid Points;
   - lumber 🪵 in battle.

   T4 (Materia) and T8 (Farms, potions) would add more. Every screen pays for it in chips and explanations. Before adding Materia, consider merging Dust and Metal, and folding tickets into Energy.
2. **Too many modes for the player count.** Conquest, 6 Arena modes, Autobattler, Raid with Trench, Market, Guild. Each new mode thins the others. My suggestion:
   - finish and polish Conquest, Quick Battle, PvP and Raid;
   - keep the rest behind discovery, as they are, but stop adding new ones for now.
3. **The header changes shape between screens.**
   - Non-Play tabs have the title on the left, then Home, ⚙, energy, profile.
   - Play tabs replace the title with sub-tabs and reorder the buttons (energy, Home, ⚙).

   Use one order everywhere.
4. **The brown-slab buttons are everywhere** (Arena, Home tiles, Shop buttons), so nothing stands out. Keep the brown for one primary action per screen; use the parchment surface for the rest.
5. **Shaders and polish vs. fundamentals.** The new WebGL effects look great, and I'm glad we did them. But the Deck and Autobattler screens are where players will bounce. I'd spend the next UI batch there, plus the battle layout fixes above, before more effects.

## Proposed order (if you agree)

1. **Battle:**
   - fit the viewport, with the log as a drawer;
   - lanes;
   - End turn;
   - big castle HP;
   - coach marks off the board.
2. Widen the Play column; vertical trail on phone Conquest.
3. Deck screen opens into the builder.
4. Arena hierarchy (Quick Battle hero); Autobattler start screen.
5. Codex toolbar collapse; one header order.
6. Currency consolidation (a design decision for you; see T4/T8).
