## Stat budget (rule of thumb)
Attack + Health: 0 lumber / wait 0 ≈ 6 points; +4 per Wait; +6 per Lumber; keyword ≈ 3 (Flying, Quick, Stealth, Evasive, Guardian); Armor/Bleed/Poison N ≈ 2N.
