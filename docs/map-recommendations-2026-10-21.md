# Map recommendations, Maps 0–28 (2026-10-21)

Package RP, the owner (Oct 21): "Creature recommendations (up to 10) per map is still welcome, skill recommendations too." For every main
map 0–28 and its side maps: up to ten creatures that fit its biome and theme, and the skills it introduces or features. Built from
`canonical/unit-themes.json` (each map's biome, every unit's habitat, family and faction), `canonical/animal-tags.json` (the animal a card
shows) and `docs/lore-bible.md`. Recommendations only: nothing here changed a card or a fight.

**Markers.** *existing (reward)*: the card is this map's reward now (after the rewards pass). *existing (fights here)*: it is in this map's
fight lists. *existing (held)*: a live card waiting for a source (`awaitingSource`), proposed for this map. *new idea*: no such card yet.

**The rules followed** (owner, recorded in `docs/decisions.json`, 2026-10-21):
- **Gentle and strict skill introduction:** a map introduces **two skills at most**, and each skill is introduced on **one** map. It is
  met first on an enemy in a fight you can win without it, then a later fight on the same map turns on it, and only then does a reward
  hand it to you ("Meet it, feel it, combine it", `docs/synergy-curriculum-2026-10-17.md`). Rewards on Maps 0–1 are plain cards; Map 1
  brings Lumber with one card.
- **Maps 21–40 use the skills already learnt:** they introduce nothing new; they feature combinations of the Maps 0–20 skills. New skills
  come again past Map 40.
- **The eras:** Maps 0–20 are today's animals. Maps 20–40 are **stronger, more advanced versions** of the 0–20 animals, and some
  **extinct animals that resemble today's** (Irish elk, cave lion, glyptodont, giant beaver). Map 40+ has **real dinosaurs**. Map 50+ is
  **magic**. Devilry is found on Map 66, Ecclesia on Map 77, Evomax from Map 30.
- **Side maps:** one unique card each, the rest of their fights built from cards the player already has; they open late (the gates in
  `docs/balance/rewards-pass-2026-10-21.md`).

A note on today's fights: Map 0's fight lists already show about twenty different skills, and most maps add five to ten more. The skills
column below is the target the fights should be brought down to (an owner call for the decks pass; the rewards pass changed no deck).
