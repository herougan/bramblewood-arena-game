# CUT3 audit: decks that still hold cut cards (2026-11-03)

Audit only. No deck, stat or leader was changed. Deck changes are balance work and wait for the owner's own pass (BAL-OWNER-1; decision CUT3-1).

## The cut
Glacial Ape-King (owner 11-03 23:12: "Cut Glacial ape-king from Map 0-20") is off the MAP lists of Map 17 Wolfsbane Tundra (`m6`) and Map 19 (`m3`) and off the Map 17 side map's list (`mi`, the 17-A fights). It was on no SPECIAL list, and on no other map or submap of Maps 0 to 20. The three review entries moved from `chosen` to `cut` in `canonical/map-review.json` (`by: owner`, "Owner 11-03"). It is no first-clear card and no fight reward on those maps (checked: `SKIRMISH_FIRST_PACK_CARDS`, node rewards, `canonical/card-obtain-maps.json` lists it at map 14 only for the budget term, unchanged).

**Owner call (not swapped):** Map 17 fight **17-8 The Glacial Ape-King** (kind miniboss, the map's ender) has Glacial Ape-King as its showcase leader (LEAD, 2026-10-23) and 4 copies in its deck. Its leader also shows on 17-1, 17-3 and the side-map fight 17-A-1. None of these fights' pools list the card now, and nothing was swapped. Say whether each leader becomes another card, or the card returns to SPECIAL for those fights (the LISTFIX2 way). The raid boss "The Glacial Ape-King" (`RAID_GROUND`, `canonical/raid-bosses.json`) is a raid, not a map list; untouched.
