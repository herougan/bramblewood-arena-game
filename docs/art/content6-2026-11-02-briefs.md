# Renamed elementals, package CONTENT6 (2026-11-02): art prompts

The owner, 11-02 14:10: *"Don't let there be any pure elementals. They have to have an animal-affinity like bear or something, representing
its form and element."* Twelve cards whose art showed no animal (a spark, a cloud, a wave, a golem, a wisp, a hooded shadow) now take an
animal form; their names show it and their ids are unchanged. Stats and skills are unchanged. Each card is marked **Art rejected**
(`artReview`, reason "CONTENT6 ...") so it shows in the admin's Rejected art list until it is repainted. Nothing was generated.

Rules for every prompt (CLAUDE.md): a full background, no weapon words, none of the banned words. Style: `docs/style-guideline.md`,
`docs/art-style-prompts.md` (paste the base prompt first). The element stays the body; the animal is the shape.

Cards whose art already shows the animal were left as they are: Gale Sprite (a swift), Alpine Avalanche (a snow bear), Wound Reaver (a lava
lizard), the salamanders, the Storm Heron, the shrine spirits, Glass Golem, Malachite Golem, Quarry Troll, Granite Ox Golem, Magma Wraith and the
cat- and fox-shaped fiends. Hazel Coppice (a dormouse, plant) and Coffer Mimic (a hermit crab, structure) were already set (CARD5, owner OK'd).
