## 1. The game in one page

A web card game of woodland animals. Two peoples, the Otters (Rivergate) and the Hummingbirds (Sunfeather), play units into lanes against an enemy bramble (the castle). Units have Attack, Health and Wait, cost Lumber, and fight in rounds (rules: `bramblewood-engine.js`). The meta-game is a road of maps. Each map has skirmishes, elites and bosses, side maps and places that unlock features. Cards are collected and levelled to 10 (11 after a limit break) and built into 20-card decks.

**Release 1.0 scope** (owner 2026-11-19 00:02): Maps 0-20 and their side maps, the first daily dungeon, the first multiplayer dungeon, the first raid, and PvP. Tuning and reporting cover Maps 0-20 only (BAL17-2).

### 2.3 Transitions in use

- **Page turn** (`view-turn-fwd` / `view-turn-back`, `arena_template.html` 7864): 380 ms, a 3D rotateY of 16 degrees plus opacity 0.35 -> 1, on the incoming view only. Direction comes from a fixed tab order. It never runs on Play, and a place's entrance replaces it.
- **Place entrances** (`bramblewood-ui-places.js`, UIFX1/UI52): Shop, Premium, Nest, Codex, Camp, Stomping Grounds, Inventory. A layer at z 590 is added over the already-redrawn page. It starts fully closed and only opens (`begin()`): full take on the first visit each session, the short take after (decision UIFX1-1 a). A tap, key or wheel skips it. There is **no closing half**: the old screen is gone at once and the new one appears behind a closed curtain.
- **Forge flare** 0.9 s once a session (`dressForgePlace`); **map veil** (`showMapVeil`, ~66604); **world zoom**; **pre-fight** veil, title card and dialogue; **Notices** fly-up (GSAP); **bwConfirm / bwChoice** pop in 0.26 s and out 0.22 s. Every other modal (`.modal-overlay`: card detail, sign in, feedback, Tavern, Map cards, editor) and every panel (settings, energy) appears and disappears instantly.
- Motion inventory in CSS: 667 distinct `@keyframes`, 135 distinct durations, 80 distinct cubic-bezier curves.
