# Balance pass, 2026-10-12

How it was measured: each card's win rate in simulated matches (8 copies, fewer for costly cards, in a deck of reference filler, against the same filler), 200 games per measurement, compared with the band for its rarity (Starter 20–48%, Common 35–60%, Uncommon 45–68%, Rare 52–75%). For each card outside its band I took the smallest change to Attack and Health that brought it into the band, within limits: Attack never below 1 or moved more than 2, Health never below 2 or moved more than about a third.

**Left alone on purpose:**
- Starter cards are untouched: the tutorial is built from them (both your side and the rival), and you asked for its cards not to change. Their spread is still yours to settle (B6).
- Cards whose value comes from a deck plan the test can't see: Ecclesia (Prayer, Worship, Healing, Lightning, Satiety, Retribution, Midas), Devilry (Sacrifice, Desecrate, Beware), Remember and Changeling cards.
- Leaders and Heroic+ cards, cost-3+ cards, Lumber makers and walls.
- The five reference cards the test is measured against (Warren Scout, Fawn Scout, Bee Sentry, Mouse Sapper, Poison Dart Croaker): changing them moves every other card's number.
- Quarry Mole (still waiting on D46) and Glass Golem (glass is the point).

## Still out of band

Eagle Diver, Puppeteer Imp, Pond Paddler, Barrow Leech Bat, Talon Archer and Upside-Down Bat are still a little strong; Duckling, Shieldbearer Hound, Weredog and Octopus Tactician are still weak. They need a skill change rather than stats, so they're on the list for your next look.

Whole-set result (60-game run, every card): 264 of 436 cards in band (before this pass: 186 of 418).
