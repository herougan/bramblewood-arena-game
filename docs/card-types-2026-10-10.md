# Card types: species and phyla (2026-10-10)

Your rule: every unit is an animal or an Elemental, and a card can be a hybrid of two. Each card now carries `species`. The card details show it as, for example, "🧬 Canid & Primate · Beast", and the card editor has a Species field that warns when it's empty.

## Judgment calls (worth a look)

- **Elemental** covers plants and treants (Ent, Elder Willow, Fungal Colossus), spirits and golems, the Devilry imps and horrors, the shrine priests, and Magma Titan, Deep Cave Troll and Wound Reaver. All of these were your "animal or elemental, your choice" cases.
- Structures go with whoever built them. Ant Hill, Colony, Nest, Termite Mound and Scraper of Skies are Insect; Dam Wall and Riverworks Bastion are Rodent (beavers).
- Wyrms are Dragon. Bats are Beast (Bat). The Tiny Cave Dweller is a Crustacean (it's a hermit crab with a gold shell). Dust Hyena is Hyaenid.
- A few imps and fliers are deliberate hybrids: Magma Salamander, Frog Imp and Fly Imp (with Elemental), and Storm Heron (Bird & Elemental).
- New tonight: the Ironmaw Warwolf is Canid & Primate (the wolf and Kip, the monkey riding it), and MissingNo. is a Bird (a nod to the original).

`archetypes` (biome and faction tags like River, Tundra, Ecclesia) are unchanged; Swarm still counts those.
