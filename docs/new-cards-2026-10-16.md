# New cards for the no-card skills (2026-10-16)

The ask: "Add 1 card for all our no-card skills. Art can wait I think. Make sure to fill the animal slots we haven't yet."

- **94 new cards**, one for every skill (not retired) that was on no real card (test cards and tokens did not count), apart from the three below. Each animal is one the game did not have yet.
- **No art yet**: they show their emoji, like the Sheep. **Not on any map**, so no one can get them yet (no source). The map column is only a suggestion.
- **Stats** fit the stat budget at Level 1 for the suggested map: Max(2×Attack + Health + Skills, Attack × Health + Skills) ≤ N, with the half-miss ×1.5 where it applies. Penance and Blood Tithe count as a discount (`-v`).
- The on-no-card triggers and actions (On Pitch, On Exile, On Heal …; Fire on hit, Missile, Bleed …) are not in this list: they are trigger parts, not skills.
- **Left off on purpose (3):** Swift (10-15: "Quick and Swift is the same right now. Remove Swift from all enemies."), Crab Claws (10-14: the crabs Fork instead) and Restless (10-14: "I didn't like this, but good to have this option."). Say if they should get a card after all.
