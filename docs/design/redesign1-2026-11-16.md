# REDESIGN1: Maps 0-20 cards against your skill priority (proposal, 2026-11-16)

**A proposal only.** It changes nothing in `canonical/cards.json`, `arena_app.js` or live data. Tick or edit it in `/home/claude/out/Redesign1 - Bramblewood.xlsx` (Change? column). The machine copy is `docs/design/redesign1-2026-11-16.json`: id, effects, attack, health and wait for each card, ready for a fold tool.

- **Source of the plan:** your workbook `G6 - Bramblewood_1.xlsx`, sheet Skills, column II (1 = must have), with K as the tie-break and category flag (brief change of 11-16 17:43). `SKMAP_PICKS` is not used for Maps 6-19. The Maps 0-5 II-1 picks are kept.
- **Cards:** each main map's MAP list (`canonical/map-pools.json`, post-LIST1) and `canonical/cards.json` (post-FOLD5). A card is designed on the first map whose list holds it, so 205 cards in all. No card moves map, rarity or name.
- **Budget:** `bramblewood-budget.js` through `tools/skill_costs.js` `budget()`, at Level 1. Fill = the larger of cap 1 (x1/N) and cap 2 (x2/N2). Band = 85-100%. A card whose skills change, or which sits outside the band, gets the closest legal stats (fewest 3A + H points moved, Attack within 3, a 0-Attack card stays 0).
- **Owner-set cards** (`tools/pipeline/config.json` refit.ownerCards): their skills change only where your new rules require it. Their stats change only when their skills do. 20 owner cards sit outside the band and are flagged, not refitted.
- **Your naming notes, used here:** Shell = Turtle, so a shell becomes Armor here. Electricity (with its Shock) = **Bioelectric**, one skill on key `electricity`. Chain Lightning is S, for bosses only. Fire Arrow = Arrow (Flaming), `arrow {type: fire}`. Heal is **Nursing**. Cure is Cure 1 only. Felling becomes On Kill: Deliver (Lumber). The Fire Arrow / Fire Bolt sub-variants, Dirge's duplicate, Laceration, Bulwark's 0/5 to 0/8 scaling, Daunt-on-attack and Illuminate are not on any proposed Maps 0-20 card. The engine package handles the renames; this plan only uses the names.
