## The flow in five lines

1. **Maple Leaves 🍁 are what you spend.** They come from first clears, which now grow +10% a map, plus dailies and repeats. They go to packs (the main sink) and to the Forge's Leaves share.
2. **Magic Dust ✨ is the Forge's currency.** It comes from first clears (+5% a map), notices, weekly quests, Murgle Mayhem, the co-op dungeon, and the copies past a card's copy cap. Five-card packs make more of those copies.
3. **Packs hold 5 cards and cost Leaves only.** Hedgerow Pouch 80, Tidemire Chest 100, Highcrest Case 125, Mystery Booster 120. The map-end pack also goes to 5 cards. Faction packs stay at 3.
4. **Gold Leaves 🍂 stay the premium anchor (ECON2-12), but leave the pack prices.** In 1.0 they buy Raid Tickets. A Gold-Leaves-only pack price waits for a store.
5. **Energy, keys and tickets are timers, not currencies.** They are unchanged (REL1-1 a, BRUP-3).

## What exists today, and what is wrong with it

Read from `arena_app.js` (no doc quotes):
- **Packs** (`SHOP_PACKS_DEFAULT`): 3 cards each.
  - Hedgerow Pouch 50 🍁. Tidemire Chest 72 🍁 + 6 🍂. Highcrest Case 94 🍁 + 11 🍂. Mystery Booster 80 🍁.
  - A map ends with a 3-card pack of its series plus a faction pack (`MAP_END_PACK_CARDS = 3`, RW5-1).
  - There are 9 free packs on the road (`MAP_FREE_PACKS`).
  - The packs go on sale on Maps 5, 8 and 13 (`PACK_SALE_MAP`).
- **First clears are flat** (`CONQUEST_NODE_REWARDS`): a skirmish pays 40 🍁 / 20 ✨ on Map 0 and on Map 20. An elite pays 70 / 68 and a boss 120 / 169. A mini-boss map ender pays as an elite (70 / 68). The Map 20 world boss pays the raid-boss row (200 / 270).
  - 41 side-map fights have hand-set pay that is heavy on Leaves and light on Dust (for example 5-A-4: 200 / 30).
- **Repeats** (ECON3) pay 4 🍁 + 1 ✨ a point of Energy after the halving. Elites and bosses pay half their table. All repeat income is halved (`ECON_INCOME_MULT`, BRUP-1).
- **Gold Leaves arrive at about 0.18 a notice** (S notice and up): about 0.5 a day for a 3-notice player.

What the sim finds with today's numbers (section 4, rows "current"):
- **Gold Leaves lock free players out of Packs 2 and 3.** A free player can only afford Hedgerow Pouches, which hold Pack 1 (Map 0-6 cards).
  - So on Maps 6-20 a free player holds only **17-20%** of the pack-card copies in the next map's BPRO-1 deck, and by Map 20 has seen 55-64% of the Pack 1-3 cards.
  - REF5-2 flagged this risk in October.
- **First-clear pay per Energy falls from 13.9 🍁 (Map 0) to 3.6 (Map 20)** (ECON2), while the Forge bill rises.
  - A player who only clears each map once ends **5.5k-16k Dust short** of the reference level by Map 20. The range covers a player who keeps most of the deck and one who rebuilds it every map.
- **Leaves pile up when nothing spends them** (ECON2: 12k at Map 20). Packs are the sink that turns that pile into cards.

- The step leaves out daily-dungeon floors, the Spire, PRO and tutorial fights.

## 4. Packs per map and the pace (sim)

**Model.** Expected values, not dice:
- A fight won with chance p costs 1/p tries.
- A pack adds its expected copies to every card in its pool, with the game's own pools and shares. The pools are P1 53, P2 63, P3 55 cards.
- The map-end pack spreads over the cards of maps already reached.
- Leaves left after next map's Forge step go to packs of the newest series on sale.
- From Map 11 the Forge keeps a 20-card deck (13 Common, 6 Uncommon, 1 Rare) at `referencePlayerLevel`. Forge tier 2 is bought when a Level 4 step is needed.
- Copies past the copy cap melt to Dust.

**Two bounds on deck churn**, from Map 12:
- **8 new Level-1 copies a map**: the player keeps most of the deck.
- **20 a map**: the player rebuilds to each map's reference deck, which is what ECON2 modelled.

**The reading:**
- **"Next-map BPRO cover"**: the share of the next main map's BPRO-1 pack-card copies the player can field when they arrive.
- **The target**: at least 50% for a player who clears each map once. The road itself is tuned for the reference deck, which has no packs, so cover measures how close the player is to the map list's best deck, not whether they can win.

**Players:**
- **road**: first clears only.
- **casual**: 2 sessions a day, 3 notices, weekly quests, 3 PvP, Murgle from Map 11 at 70% of keys, a co-op run a day from Map 16.
- **dedicated**: Energy never capped, 6 notices, 10 PvP.
- **pass**: casual plus the Bramble Pass as priced in D35 / D75. The Pass is not built and has no store: 330 🍂 up front, 15 🍂 and 1 pack a day. 🍂 buy packs at 40 / 60 / 75 (proposed).

**What the tables say:**

## 8. Risks

- **A published pack table wins over the code.** The admin row `__cfg:shop-packs` (`cloudShopPacks`) overrides `SHOP_PACKS_DEFAULT` for every player. Phase 2 must read it (read-only SQL). If it exists, the new sizes and prices go in `supabase/proposed/` and wait for the owner's "apply it".
- **Five-card packs speed up collections.** The whole Map 0-20 pack pool is seen within a month (D35 aimed at 3-4 months for a complete collection). Full playsets of the commons are still far off: 51-69% of a BPRO deck's pack copies.
- **The reference ladder (BAL24 / REF6) buys packs with its Leaves.** Its decks get stronger under this plan. The "PRO vs the reference ladder: 15-30%" marks should be re-measured after phase 2 (`tools/bal24/ladder_sim_2026_11_07.js`, then `pro_ease.js`).
- **Metal still piles up** (60-86 at Map 20). It is outside this plan. Watch it.

## 10. Owner questions (the defaults are what phase 2 does)

**ECON1-1: Gold Leaves out of the pack prices?**
- a) **(default)** Leaves only: Pouch 80, Chest 100, Case 125 for 5 cards. A free player can buy the current series, and the next-map BPRO cover is 51-69%.
- b) Keep a Gold Leaves part (Chest +10 🍂, Case +18 🍂). Free players earn about 0.5 🍂 a day, so they buy Pouches only, and the cover stays near today's 17-20%.

**ECON1-2: Faction packs: 3 cards or 5?**
- a) **(default)** Keep 3 (2 of your side + 1 general). The faction track is paced so its first Rare lands on Map 14 (RP).
- b) 5 cards (3 + 2). Faction-pack cards come about 1.7x as fast, so the faction Rare gate would need retuning.

**ECON1-3: First-clear growth by map?**
- a) **(default)** +10% Leaves and +5% Dust a map: Map 20 pays 3x / 2x Map 0. A skirmish pays 40 / 20 on Map 0, 80 / 30 on Map 10 and 120 / 40 on Map 20.
- b) +10% for both. A player who rebuilds every map waits less (casual 0.7 days, not 2.4), but one who keeps the deck holds 19-34k unspent Dust at Map 20.
- c) Flat, as today.
