## What changed for players
- Cure always cures 1 (4 cards were Cure 2).
- Log Walls are 0/5, growing to 0/8 at Level 10.
- Daunt lands on the unit it attacks, not on Pounce, Fork prongs, Cleave's side hits or counters.
- A Counterattack unit attacks the enemy bramble when no enemy faces it.
- Illuminate no longer keeps Diurnal units' day bonus at night (it still lights the dark).
- Offspring needs 1 more Lumber as it arrives, or no copies hatch (on no card).
- Names: "Arrow (Flaming)" (was Fire Arrow), "Fortify: +1 Armor" (was "Fortify (Armor 1), while it lives"), "Against All Odds" (was Foe's Plenty).
- A "Fog" pill beside the time-of-day pill on foggy fields.
- The card editor: Cure takes no number; Fire Arrow / Fire Bolt have no Bramble box and no bleed / venom riders.
- Card rule: Electricity with an electric attack, or Venomous with a venom attack, counts as one skill toward the two-skill limit.
