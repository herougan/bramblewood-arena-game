### Rend and Embrittle: On Play vs On Hit (outcomes)

| Form | What doubles | How long | Who gains | Why it is (or is not) on Maps 0-20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rend On Hit (Fossa Ambusher, Map 22) | the next N hits on the target, any source | refilled by every landed melee hit | itself every round (its own next swing doubles) and every ally on that target | **Kept off Maps 0-20 (ENG50: "2x too strong")**: a self-feeding doubler, almost every hit doubles while it lives |
| ⬆️ Rend On Play | the next N hits on the target | once, until used or Cleansed | the allies that strike that unit this round | one burst you can see coming: fair for Maps 0-20 |
| Embrittle On Hit (Glacier Wolf Pack, Map 22) | every hit on the target (Fragile) | N rounds, refreshed by each hit | everyone, while it keeps hitting | a lasting focus-fire aura on one unit: Maps 21+ |
| ⬆️ Embrittle On Play | every hit on the target | N rounds from arrival | multi-hitters and arrow lines this round | a window, not an engine: fair for Maps 0-20 |

Rend is tokens (counted hits, stacks, x4 with Fragile); Embrittle is time (rounds, never x4, refreshed not stacked).

### Arrow check (against `rides` in `canonical/skill-delivery.json`)
Confirmed: an arrow (any ranged hit) carries **no** Rend, Daunt, Bleed or Venomous: they ride `melee` (Daunt the `swing` only). What still differs:
- an arrow's own rider parameter (Arrow {poison / bleed: N}, "Arrow - poison") still gives counters, except Arrow (Flaming) (SKN1: no rider);
- any-hit riders still ride arrows: Crit, Cull (so Dogfighter), King Slayer, Sunder, Shatter, Mighty / Feeble x2 / x0.5;
- Fletcher and Weapon Master add to arrows only;
- tokens sitting on the target (Vulnerable, Fragile, Exploit, Exposed) are spent by an arrow like any hit: ⬆️ Rend + an archer line is a combo.

### On Pitch today (checked in the engine)
- `onPitch` = **the board unit's reaction**: every live unit of yours runs it when you pitch any card (`firePitchTriggers`); on no card; the text reads "Scavenge - {}".
- The pitched card's **own** trigger is `onDiscard`, limited to resource gains and Rebuild (`pitchCard`); on no card.
- UI note for the coordinator: `triggerSpent` in `arena_app.js` marks `onPitch` as spent on a board unit (UIQ2 meant On Discard: "a unit on the board can never be discarded again"). Harmless today (no card), wrong once a Cache card exists.

### C2. Simplified Wet and the shock chain
- Wet is yes or no: one 💧 badge, no number. Today's 0-3 counters, the 1-a-round drain and Rain's +1 go.
- A shock hit (Electricity's arc, a Chain Lightning jump, Shock Bolt, Lightning, Thunder) on a Wet unit deals +1, then 1 shock to every Wet unit linked to it through Wet neighbours (left / right; front / behind on raid boards), each once, on the target's side. A dry unit breaks the chain. The chain's hits start no new chain and do not dry anything.
- Wet ends with a fire hit or dry weather (default a), or also at the next round's start out of the water (option b).
- Road impact: Maps 9-10 (Tide units are Wet; Voltaic Eel, Electric Ray) and Map 2 (Tide). Balance-analyst re-measures after the build.

### C5. Doublers and halvers
- **x2:** Vulnerable tokens (Rend: each hit taken x2, one token a hit), Fragile (passive, or Embrittle for N rounds: takes x2), Mighty (passive, or Embolden for N rounds: deals x2), Weakness to the damage type, Crit (1 in 2, a round's damage), Pulverise vs Structures; Shocked is x1.5.
- **x0.5:** Feeble (passive, or Enfeeble for N rounds: deals half), Sturdy (passive, or Harden for N rounds: takes half), Resist to the damage type, Petrified (takes half), Bound by Constrict (swings at half). A timed form never stacks with its passive (x2 / x0.5 at most); Vulnerable tokens do multiply with Fragile (x4).
