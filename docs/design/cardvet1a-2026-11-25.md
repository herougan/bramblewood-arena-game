# CARDVET1a: vet of the cards on Maps 0-6 (2026-11-25)

Owner 2026-11-25 00:22: "Vet through each card after - if it doesnt fit the theme - for example a late-game animal with too simple an effect - maybe we have to change it or consider doing trigger->actions instead ... we want more active skills (on play or immediately this combat, after play e.g. 0 wait) - allows players to surprise each other etc. High wait cards are like building for payoffs. Change the card designs for cards with skills we dont use for 0-20 anymore. ... for all creatures from map 0-20 including submaps - can we do more realistic animals b4 the elemental animals and magical ones ... Replace magma rhino ... the caldera wyrm can be an oversized komodo dragon" (the last two are Maps 20-22, a later package).

Package files: `tools/cardvet1a_2026_11_25.js` (the change list `DES`, stat fit, then `tools/lvl10/gen_2026_11_18.js` per card for the Level 1-10 table; the generator got an env `CARDVET_CORE` to name which skill stays at Level 1), `tools/i18n_2026_11_25_cardvet1a.json` (8 names, 5 flavours, 12 packs), `docs/art/real1-requests.md`. Method: the skill `.claude/skills/card-level-design`; the owner's table rows (`MAP_FOCUS_SKILLS`, `MAP_SPECIAL_SKILL_NAMES`); `canonical/skill-costs.json` trigger and action lists.

## Scope and counts

Cards on the main lists, the side-map lists (`mo mq mp mg ms mm`) and Special lists of Maps 0-6 (`m1 mf mb mc m2 mw m8`), read from `canonical/map-pools.json`: **116 unique cards**. The T-3 to T-5 tutorial list (`mtut`, any card for teaching, owner THEME3) is out of scope; the two scripted tutorials (`mtut12`) are in. Road enemy decks: the changed cards are listed per map under "Road fights" below.

| Verdict | Cards |
|---|---|
| keep | 94 |
| tweak (a table or a rename) | 5 |
| redesign (new skills / concept) | 12 |
| real-animal swap (3 new animals, 2 renames to the real animal the art already shows) | 5 |

**Active designs** (an On Play / On Ready trigger, Pounce, Arrow, Intimidate, Delivery or Parasite; a skill that acts the turn it is played or as soon as the card is Ready): **12 of 116 (10%) before, 21 of 116 (18%) after**. By map (cards on that map's lists, side lists included): Map 0 1 to 4 of 33, Map 1 0 to 1 of 23, Map 2 1 to 3 of 29, Map 3 5 to 6 of 17, Map 4 4 to 4 of 15, Map 5 0 to 2 of 23, Map 6 3 to 4 of 16. The nine new active designs: Barn Owl (On Play strike), Strangler Fig (On Play Wait +2), Hedgehog Matron (On Play Shield x2), Lucky Hopper (On Play draw), Lodge Engineer (Delivery), Trash Panda (On Play Lumber), Hawk Arrowkeeper (Arrow), Cottonmouth Matriarch (On Play Poison), Puddle Hopper (Intimidate). Most kept cards are owner-edited or starters (early rewards are vanilla by rule), which caps the share; Maps 0-2 are mostly starters and common animals by design, so no active conversion was forced there.

Skills in no Maps 0-20 row that left these cards: Fog, Herbalist, Immunity (Hedgehog Matron), Harden, Rainrunner (Puddle Skimmer, Puddle Hopper), Timbered, Bounty, Salvage, Venom Master, Enfeeble, Crepuscular, Cure, Dive. Kept on purpose: Leader (the skirmish leaders), Immunity on the Gaboon Viper (a boss), Hatchling (Level 1 rule), Rampart on Riverworks Bastion (a structure), Taunt on Proboscis Monkey and Herbalist on Newt Doctor (MAPEXEC1 placed both on a Map 21-23 row on purpose).

New! rules: none of the redesigned cards carries a skill from a later row than its list (the exceptions that were already there are in "Not changed"); `docs/map-skill-table.md` was regenerated after the build: New! with 2+ carriers 44, with 1 carrier 17, shown earlier 0, on no card 6 (same as after MAPEXEC1; only carrier names changed: Black Caiman, Honey Fungus). Fork stays on octopi, Trident rare, no new resistances. A trial of Pounce on Raft Spider was reverted: it moved Venomous to Level 6 and Map 5's Venomous row fell from 2 carriers to 1.

Budget: every redesigned card is inside 85-100% of N at Level 1 and its cap 2 use is at most 41% (`bramblewood-budget.js` through `tools/skill_costs.js budgetAt`; the Level table below). Dips below 85% at single levels are the generator's own step shape (the same on the untouched cards: Phoenix Mushrooms 83%, Rabbit Kit 71%); House Cat (owner-edited, vanilla now) runs 80-89%.
