# W2PLAN: New Skills for Maps 21-40 (proposal, 2026-11-25)

This is a proposal only: no card, map, price or engine change. It answers the owner's 11-25 00:22 note: "The vibe for 21 to 40 is, 30% less new skills"; "we want more active skills (on play or immediately this combat, after play e.g. 0 wait) - allows players to surpise each other etc. High wait cards are like building for payoffs"; and the 0-20 realism note (realistic animals on Maps 0-20, so elemental and magical creatures come after Map 20). The 11-25 call replaces the 10-21 line "Maps 21-40 use the skills already learnt" (project-state section 2). This doc does not edit project-state.

## Count and target

- **Maps 0-20 (your table, `MAP_FOCUS_SKILLS`):** 67 new skills on 21 maps (43 on Maps 0-10, 24 on Maps 11-20), 3.2 a map, plus 13 special / faction skills on 12 maps (`MAP_SPECIAL_SKILL_NAMES`). Counted as named skills only (no light flags Flying, Tide, Diurnal, Nocturnal; no actions or damage types Nursing, Delivery, Electricity, Illuminate, as SKR1-1 and DES1-2 count), it is 59.
- **Target, 30% fewer:** 47 new skills (2.35 a map).
- **The cap:** two new skills a map at most (project-state rule; your 11-14 rule "advanced maps 0-2") allows 40 on 20 maps, so 47 does not fit. Owner question 1.
- **Proposed:** 40 new skills, 2 a map (38 keywords + 2 trigger lines; Map 40's two are placeholders, since you build Map 40 yourself, N22-1). That is 40% fewer than 67, or 32% fewer than the 59 named skills (36% if the two trigger lines are left out too). Plus **9 special / faction skills** (13 less 30%).

## How the picks were made

1. **Active first.** 25 of the 40 work on play, as a trigger, in the moment, or change the next exchange on a hit; 5 are high-Wait payoffs; the rest are elemental skills or gear.
2. **Payoffs are few** (Hoard, Spawner, Molting, Evolute, Stem). Carrier rule: active skills on Wait 0-1 bodies so they act the turn they land; payoff skills on Wait 2+ bodies.
3. **Elemental from Map 21:** cold (21, 22, 33), storm (28), water (35), acid (38), and fire from a real bird (Chilli, 25); fire itself stays on Maps 19-20 as your table has it. The MAGIC- and GOD-tag skills and Undead wait for 50+ (owner question 2).
4. **Your lists first:** most picks are on your pass-2 list (`docs/skill-ranking.md`, column II) or on the holds already set for 21+ (Rend and Enfeeble, held back by OWN-1117-2; Exhaust, SKAUD1-1; Helmet, Pitchfork and the rank 3s, SKR1; the rainforest holds for Map 25, RP5; Evomax from Map 30).
5. **No new keyword.** All 40 are built and priced in the engine (rule SK1: build from triggers and actions first). Seven have no card yet: Revive, Warcry, Exhaust, Rebuild, On Enemy Played, Chain Lightning, and Spawner as a keyword (Ant Hill still uses the plain trigger line).
6. **Two carriers each** on its map, one the player can win there (DES1-2).

Kinds: **P** On Play · **T** trigger or reaction · **C** this combat · **H** on hit (changes the next exchange) · **W** high-Wait payoff · **E** elemental · **G** gear or passive. Ranks: your pass 2 as r1-r7; pass 1 as p1-p7 where pass 2 is blank. **†** = on a Maps 0-20 card today and flagged To remove (MAPRM1), or on a Maps 0-20 side-map card. It is new here once MAPEXEC1 / CARDVET1 take it off; otherwise it counts as carried. Carried skills give the map's Map 0-20 skill, with the map number where it is first New!.

## Retire or merge (short list)

1. **Scare: retire.** It has been on no card since DAUNT2, and Intimidate does the same thing (-N to the attacker facing it). You prefer that word.
2. **Render: retire the keyword.** It is On Play: N Daunt on the enemy facing it (3 cards, none on a list). The On Play: Daunt line or Intimidate covers it.
3. **Embrittle into Rend {lasts N rounds}.** Both make the target take double.
4. **Frenzy into Encore {while it has a status}**, with its 1-Bleed rider: one "acts again" family.
5. **Renewal into Cure {itself, every status}:** one cure family (Cure is your r1).
6. **Heavy Metal and Lumberjack: one skill**, with the resource as a parameter (+1 on each gain).
7. **Vulnerable N (the old "takes +N" passive) into Fragile**, so Vulnerable means only Rend's token (ENG50-1).
8. **Puncture into Siege {through}.** Both damage the bramble on a hit; it is your r7; the Battering-Ram carries both.
9. **Hatchling and Chrysalis as kinds of Stem**, so Map 30 teaches one base skill beside Evolute.

**Held, not placed:** Lone Wolf, Herbalist, Pulverise, Mountain Climber, Durian, Plunder, Immunity, Sequestration, Coffee, Masonry / Refining / Pearl (the Stone loop runs on Delivery {Stone}, carried), Ice / Poison / Shock Bolt, the MAGIC and GOD tags, Undead.
