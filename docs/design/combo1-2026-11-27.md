### Engine notes: what blocks or limits a combo
- **A. Bleed is wasted on a held unit.** Bleed ticks only when the bleeder acts (owner 2026-10-10; BLEED1), but its stack still fades 1 at every round's end (`bleedFadeAtEnd`). A Stunned, Asleep or Waiting unit takes no Bleed damage and loses a stack anyway, so Bleed + Paralysis / Sleeping Powder / Bulldoze work against each other. Poison has no such limit (combo 14).
- **B. Swing-only riders:** Electricity, Daunt, Wave and Stampede ride the swing, never Pounce, Ambush, arrows or Cleave side hits (`canonical/skill-delivery.json`).
- **C. Melee riders:** Exploit, Web, Venomous, Bleed, Bulldoze ride melee hits (swing, Pounce, Ambush, Cleave side hits), never arrows. Tokens already on a unit (Exploit) are spent by arrows too.
- **D. Fletcher** boosts arrows only, not bolts (combo 7).
- **E. Trap** springs on a played card only (combo 28).
- **F. Webbed** units can't be moved: Web cancels Wave's push and Map 10's Awash on that unit.
- **G. Harbinger** gains 3 a round at most, however many die.
- **H. Swarm** reads Types, not species (combo 10).
