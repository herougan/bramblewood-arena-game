# NEWCARDS1: 14 new species cards (2026-11-26)

Package NEWCARDS1. The 14 ART16 species (docs/art/art16-new-cards.md) as cards, designed with the card-level-design skill. All 14 sit at the end of `canonical/cards.json` with `awaitingSource: true` and `unattainable: true`: on no map list, pack or reward. The home column is a proposal; placing is the owner's call (the coordinator files it). Budget: cap 1 `f x ((3A + H) x m + S) <= N`; each card is fitted at Map 0 (what the tools see while it is unplaced) to 93-100%, and the second figure is the fill at its proposed home map, where the Map term raises N. Every level 1-10 is inside both caps at the home map (cap 2 use 14-35%).

Why these effects: the owner wants "a series of fun effects" (2026-11-26 21:32). Nine of the 16 Undercroft-only effects now have a carrier that fits its animal: Lone Wolf, Heat, Plenty, Confuse, Gash, Cull, Pack, Rage, Throng. Not given (no animal fit): Volley, Pulverise, Decay, Embrittle, Fast Forward, Pack Howl, Storm. Thin trigger effects: one On Death (Kakapo draws a card) and one On Play (Tapir grows a Growth Sprout), plus Revive (an On Play return) on the Coelacanth. Two skills at most (Flying, Tide, Diurnal, Nocturnal are light and do not count); the Tube Worm learns Throng only at Level 9 because Throng is too dear to afford earlier.

## Notes for the owner

- Placing a card changes the road only if it goes on a main list; side maps and booster cards (SKILLS3-8) do not move road fights. Homes in this table are one proposal each, with a booster or side-map alternative where the card is a rare.
- Coelacanth is Epic: the road ladder lets Epic in from Map 21 (`RARITY_INTRO`), so its home is Map 21+ or a side map.
- Saiga (84% at Map 14) and Red Panda (84% at Level 1, Map 11) sit just under the 85% line at their home map; a Level 1 Health +1 each when placed (re-fit by formula).
- The Saiga carries Pack 1 alone (Swift on top left it 2/4). Its `Herd` type shares Pack only with other Herd cards: today none, so it chains with a second Saiga until a Herd family exists.
- Mudpuppy and Plague Gnat Cloud: re-art imported (ART16 variant A, the only variant staged); the prompts sit beside the art.
- Tools: `tools/newcards1_2026_11_26.js` (fit and append), `tools/newcards1_gen_2026_11_26.js` (levels), `tools/i18n_2026_11_26_newcards1.json` (names and flavour, 12 packs; Tagalog, Tamil, Japanese and Korean are drafts until a native reads them).
