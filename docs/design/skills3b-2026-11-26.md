# SKILLS3b (2026-11-26): x2 / x0.5 statuses, elements, effects over all cards (design only)

Owner 2026-11-25 23:59, a follow-up to `docs/design/skills3-2026-11-25.md`. This is a proposal only: no code or data changed. The engine and `docs/decisions.json` win over it. Trigger icons: ⬆️ On Play, 💀 On Death, 🃏 On Pitch (SKILLS3-1).

## 1. The four x2 / x0.5 statuses

| Status | Sits on | Does | In the engine today | Laid by | Permanent passive (kept) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mighty** 💪 | an ally | deals x2 | passive Mighty (Map 14); Embolden N gives it for N rounds (FOCUS2) | **Embolden N** | Mighty |
| **Sturdy** 🗻 | an ally | takes x0.5 | passive Sturdy (Map 16); Harden N, for N rounds | **Harden N** | Sturdy |
| **Vulnerable** 💔 | a foe | takes x2 | Rend's tokens (one spent per hit taken, never wear off); Embrittle's Fragile (N rounds) | **Rend N** (Embrittle merges into it, W2PLAN merge 3) | Fragile (drawback) |
| **Feeble** 🪶 | a foe | deals x0.5 | Enfeeble N, for N rounds (FOCUS2; was tokens) | **Enfeeble N** | Feeble (drawback) |

**Names.** The two buffs keep the engine's names, **Mighty** and **Sturdy**. The statuses then read like the passives, as Feeble already does. The skills that give them stay Embolden and Harden.

**Token rule (owner):** one token comes off at each upkeep (3 Vulnerable -> 2). As I read it, while a unit has any token, every hit on it (or by it) counts at x2 / x0.5. Hits spend nothing, and only the upkeep removes a token, so tokens = rounds left, shown as icon + number.

What changes from today:
- **Vulnerable:** today one token is spent per hit taken and never wears off. Now every hit counts while any token is left, and one token comes off per upkeep. Embrittle's Fragile becomes the same status, so Embrittle becomes Rend (Glacier Wolf Pack, Glasswing Moth).
- **Feeble, Mighty, Sturdy from skills:** today they last N rounds, refreshed to the larger value and never stacked. Now they are tokens. Proposed: new tokens add, up to 3, so an On Hit unit that hits every round can't build an endless x2 (SKILLS3-9).
- **Never x4 from one side:** a token plus its passive stays x2 / x0.5. Today Vulnerable tokens multiply with Fragile; that goes. An attacker's Mighty times a target's Vulnerable still makes x4 (a stratagem, part 3).
- **Flag:** should per-hit spending stay as well? Two clocks (each hit and each upkeep) are harder to read and weaker. Recommended: no.
- **Passives** (Mighty, Sturdy, Feeble, Fragile) stay permanent. The old "Vulnerable N" passive (Newly Moulted Crab) becomes Fragile (W2PLAN merge 7).

### The ladder: plain On Hit first, then ⬆️ On Play, then 💀 On Death
The plain form: each landed hit lays N tokens, debuffs on the unit hit and buffs on an ally beside it (flag: or on itself). Maps 0-20 keep only the passives (Mighty 14, Sturdy 16) and Exploit (14), as OWN-1117-2 holds.

| Status (skill) | Plain On Hit (first) | ⬆️ On Play (later) | 💀 On Death (last) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vulnerable (Rend) | Map 22 Rimefire Caldera: Fossa Ambusher (there today) | Map 28 Stormcoast: Osprey Plunger (On Play on Map 22 today: moves later) | Map 34 Tar Pits: Porcupine Roller (quills stay in the killer: 💀 its killer gets 2 Vulnerable) |
| Feeble (Enfeeble) | Map 23 Goldfeather Siege: Tsetse Drainer (there today; W2PLAN had Map 36) | Map 31 Hollow Farmsteads: Skunk Gasser (⬆️ the enemy facing it gets 2 Feeble) | Map 35 Drowned Reef: Sea Cucumber (sticky threads: 💀 its killer gets 2 Feeble) |
| Mighty (Embolden) | Map 23: Meerkat Sentry (there; its round-start aura becomes On Hit) | Map 25 Rainforest Floor: War-Drum Mandrill | Map 39 Last Savanna: Marching Elephant (the matriarch falls and the herd charges: 💀 allies beside it get 2 Mighty) |
| Sturdy (Harden) | Map 23: Sworn Shieldbearer (there; aura becomes On Hit) | Map 33 Mammoth Steppe: Musk Ox Matriarch (⬆️ the ring: allies beside it get 2 Sturdy) | Goliath raid: Reefback Turtle (💀 allies beside it get 2 Sturdy) |

Map 23 would then hold three plain forms. Under W2PLAN's two-a-map cap, Embolden can move to Map 26 (The Ant Empire). The later ⬆️ / 💀 forms are kinds of a skill already introduced, so they don't count as new skills (proposed, SKILLS3-10).

### What this does to the SKILLS3 Rend / Embrittle plan
- **Dropped:** ⬆️ Rend on Map 14 and ⬆️ Embrittle on Map 18. The ladder puts On Play after the plain form, and Embrittle merges into Rend. Map 14 keeps Exploit; Glasswing Moth becomes a plain Rend carrier on Maps 21+.
- **ENG50's call holds, and more strongly.** Under per-hit spending, one token doubled one hit. Under the upkeep rule, one token doubles every hit on that unit until the upkeep: the Rend unit's next swing plus every ally's hit and shot that round. On a unit hit 2-3 times a round, that is 2-3x the old value.
- **Default:** On Hit Rend stays off Maps 0-20. Rend needs a new price, roughly x1.5-2 (theoretical). Enfeeble, Embolden and Harden already lasted rounds, so they barely change.
- **Option (SKILLS3-3 b):** lift the hold. Plain Rend 1 on Map 14 in place of Exploit (Stoat Cutthroat), ⬆️ Rend on Map 18 (Glasswing Moth), 💀 Rend on Map 20.

## 2. Elements

### The owner's table, with what a hit does (SKILLS3 C model: damage + at most one status)
| Element | Status | A hit does | The status | How it ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | **Burn** (counter) | damage + 1 Burn | 1 fire at each round's start, one counter off a tick | ticks out; water; Cleanse |
| Ice (frost) | **Frozen** (yes / no) | damage + Frozen | loses that round | melts at that round's end |
| Acid | **Corrode** (on the Attack pill) | damage + 1 Corrode (today only Acid Bolt adds it) | -N Attack, refreshed not stacked | -1 a round; Cleanse |
| Poison | **Poison** (counter) | stacks only, no damage | N at the round's start, then -1 (Deadly Poison never wears off) | Cure |
| Water | **Wet** (yes / no) | damage + Wet | electricity's partner (below) | a fire hit; dry weather |
| Electricity | none | damage only | reads Wet | - |
| (no element) | Bleed | the Bleed rider | bleeds before its own actions | -1 at the round's end; a heal stanches it |
| (no element) | Paralysed | Paralysis Powder | stun counters | -1 a round |
| (no element) | Vulnerable, Feeble, Mighty, Sturdy | part 1 | x2 / x0.5 | one token per upkeep |

### How they combine
| This | meets | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| water hit | Burn | Burn out | today |
| fire hit | Wet | Wet burnt off; that hit lights no Burn | owner (today: 1 water counter dried a point, the rest lights) |
| electricity hit | Wet | +1, and 1 electricity to every Wet unit chained to it | owner's simplified Wet (SKILLS3 C2) |
| fire hit | Frozen | thaws | proposed |
| ice hit | Burn | puts it out | proposed |
| ice hit | Wet | frozen solid: Frozen 2 rounds, Wet ends | optional, Maps 21+ where ice starts |
| Cure / heal / Cleanse | Poison / Bleed / the physical statuses | removed | today |

Fire and water cancel each other, and so do fire and ice; water feeds electricity (and ice, if the option is taken). Nothing else is proposed: poison, acid and Bleed stay outside the cancels so the board stays readable.

### Elemental Mastery: +N to all of that element's attacks
Rule: while it is on the field, **your units'** attacks of that element deal N more (a side aura, like Fletcher for arrows and Venom Master for Poison and Bleed). It is a payoff, so one rare card per element (SKILLS3-6). Engine: missing; it would copy Fletcher's pattern, read in the hit by damage type. Price: Fletcher's 3.19 a point (measured), scaled by how many of your units share the element; theoretical until measured.

Names (one family, the element as a parameter, as ENG35 asks):
1. **Attunement N – {element}**, read "Fire Attunement 1" (recommended: short and clear, and it reads as an aura)
2. {Element} Mastery N (the owner's working name)
3. Affinity N – {element} (risk: it reads like a resistance)
4. One word per element: Stoke (fire), Deep Chill (ice), Caustic (acid), Undertow (water), Overcharge (electricity)
5. Job names like Fletcher: Firekeeper, Frostcaller, Tidecaller, Sparkwright (Stormcaller clashes with the Stormcaller Owl card)

Poison already has its form: **Venom Master** (Poison and Bleed hits +N) stays as poison's kind.
