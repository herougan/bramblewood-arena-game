Four card sizes

Package CARDSIZE1, 29 Oct 2026. Today the game draws cards at about 50 different sizes. This mock shows the four that would replace them. Every card on this page comes from one render function, renderCard(def, size). The size only sets the card's width, and the card decides for itself what fits.
