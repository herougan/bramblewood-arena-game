# ONBOARD2: the first 15 minutes (Maps T-3), 2026-11-28

Docs only. Read from `arena_app.js` (GUIDED_STEPS 49790, passiveTipCandidates 49877, PITCH_LESSON 49954, TRIGGER_LESSON 50001, STATUS_TIPS 49863, lumberDrawNudges 16346, pitchingUnlocked 16285, kwPop 3234) and phone shots.

## What is taught now, in order
1. **T-1 (guided, blocking, 4 steps):** "These are your cards" (g-hand), "Drag a card onto the field" (g-play), "The enemy Bramble. Break it to win" (g-castle), "Cards hit the card in front of them. No card in front? They hit the Bramble" (g-combat; the CPU is scripted to set up one blocked and one open lane). Then a 5 s tip: draw 1 card a turn (g-deck). Shot: `docs/screens/onboard2/tutorial-first-step-390.png`.
2. **T-2:** the leader joins by dialogue, then Leader slot, Wait (g-leader-wait, g-wait-tick: watch the number drop), Initiative coin (g-initiative). Shot of the map entry: `docs/screens/onboard2/map-t1-t2-390.png`.
3. **Map 0-1 (passive, one tip a round, first match wins):** `wait` ("rounds until this card can attack"), castle Shield, hand limit ("extra cards turn into Lumber"), then a skill tip the first time each skill is on the field, then a status tip.
4. **Lumber and pitching:** nothing in the tutorial (`pitchLessonTip` skips mode tutorial). It starts only when `pitchingUnlocked()` (after Map 1 is done) and you hold a card you cannot pay for: a dialogue line, then pl-need / pl-do / pl-after, then up to 8 "Needs Lumber" tags.
5. **Triggers:** one tip, "This chip is a trigger, read the card" (tl-what), first time any card with a trigger shows. Never in the tutorial fights.
6. **Strategy ("why this card"):** the Unique-enemy peek gate (g-peek) from your second match only. Nothing says why one card beats another.

## Where a new player is likely lost
- **Lanes, then Wait, are far apart.** The lane rule comes in T-1 but a T-1 card never has Wait, so the fight shows no ring until T-2. First tap in T-1 lands on a shield over the whole hand (shot 1): the cards are hidden behind the bubble and the dim.
- **Lumber is invisible until Map 2.** T-1 and T-2 decks are all cost 0, so "your Lumber" is a pill the player was never shown (hand-limit tip says "turn into Lumber" before the Lumber pill is explained). The Graveyard drop zone has no name until pl-do.
- **Triggers are one line and no meaning.** The icons ⬆️ (On Play), 💀 (On Death), 🃏 (On Pitch), ⏱️ (timed) changed on 11-27 (trigLeadIcon) but no tip says which moment each is. The player has to open the card.
- **Statuses:** STATUS_TIPS covers 12 (Poison, Bleed, Frozen, Asleep, Paralyzed, Stunned, Tipsy, Confused, Blind, Smoke, Chained, Daunted). It has no tip for **Wet** (Map 4+, the first water statuses), Burning, Shocked, Exposed, Fragile or Weakened, although each has a glossary entry (`KW` list, UIPOLISH1).
- **Glossary hover (UIPOLISH1 #2, `b.kw[data-kw]` on mouseover):** desktop only. A phone has no hover, and no tip says words are tappable. Bubbles use `kw-jargon` colour without the pop.
- **"Why a card is strategic" is never said.** A 5/5 with Wait 3 and a 2/3 with Wait 0 look like bigger vs smaller; nothing introduces the trade (Wait vs power vs Lumber).
