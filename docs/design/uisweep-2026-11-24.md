The look is Conquest's, evolved, not replaced: dark "glass" panels with a thin gold line, cream round buttons with a tan ring, a wood-brown pill for the active choice, gold as the only accent, Baloo 2 for voice and Nunito for text. What changes is that every screen now speaks it.

## 0. Principles (what the sweep enforces)

1. One component per job: one bar (the dock), one back, one Home, one sub-tab control, one modal shell, one banner, one sign.
2. A parent owns the gap between its children. Children carry no outer margin (the merchant banner's 12 / 8 px came from `margin:10px 0 2px`).
3. Every place change closes and opens. Nothing only opens. A repeat visit takes at most 450 ms (UIFX1-1, 10-08 "instantly transit you").
4. The core of the bar never moves between screens. Position is memory.
5. Tokens, not literals, in every block we touch. Card tiles and board cards keep their own sizing system (`--match-card-w/h`, `.card-tile`) and skill VFX keep their own timings (style guide sections 2, 4, 7).

### 1.5 Breakpoints (33 widths in 515 rules)

The style guide (section 7) says one mobile breakpoint; the CSS has 33. Confirmed target: **one phone breakpoint, `max-width:560px`**, plus **one wide breakpoint, `min-width:900px`** for side-by-side layouts (Codex album, map list on the left, Arena two columns). Mapping: 380-520, 560, 600, 640, 700 become 560 when the block stacks or shrinks; 820 and above become 900 when the block adds columns; 1000-2300 become `@container` rules on `.content-wrap` or are dropped. CSS custom properties cannot sit in `@media`, so the two numbers are comments at the top of the style block plus a lint check (a changed block with any other width fails). Owner question 3 amends style guide section 7 to "one phone breakpoint plus one wide".

## 2. One navigation model: the dock

### 2.1 Shape

One component, `.dock`, drawn once and moved between contexts by `switchTab`, never redrawn per page. Order, left to right:

`[back] [context slot] ......spacer...... [Home] [Map] [Settings] [Energy] [Account]`

- **Back**: one round glass `↩` (STATE2-2 a), first control on the left, only off Home and off Play. It goes where the player came from (`pageCameFrom` already knows). Its tooltip and `aria-label` name the target ("Back to the map", "Back to Camp"). No text label, no `← Back`, `‹ Back`, `← Profile`, `← Arena` anywhere; screens that had two backs (S3) lose the second.
- **Context slot**: the page's own shortcuts, at most 4 icons, filling the space the back leaves on Play. Conquest keeps 🏟️ 🔨 🚐 🔑 exactly as today (owner is happy with it). At 390 the slot shows 🔑 and a `⋯` that opens a popover with the rest. Camp, Shop, Codex, Profile have an empty slot: their section choices are the sub-tabs under the sign, not bar icons.
- **Core**, always the same five, always in the same place: `🏠` Home (STATE2-1 a), `🗺️` Map, `⚙️` Settings, `20/20 ⚡` Energy pill, `👤` Account (icon only at 390, "Guest · Sign in" at 1366). The button for the place you are on is filled wood, not hidden, so nothing shifts: Home filled on Home, 🗺️ filled on Conquest. 💬 Feedback moves inside the Settings panel.
- The acorn brand tile leaves the bar (it was 35x46 and a second Home). 🌰 stays as the artwork above the wordmark on Home only, not a button.
- Size: 40 px round, 8 px apart, 12 px from the edges; 36 px floor at 390. The 7-icon Camp bar is replaced by sub-tabs (section 4.2), which fixes the 26x36 targets.
- `.topbar-wrap` width stays fixed per tab; the dock is as wide as the viewport minus 24 px on every page.

## 3. One transition language

Four tiers; each has an enter and an exit; none only opens. All durations are tokens; all move `transform` and `opacity` only.

| Tier | Used for | Exit (old) | Enter (new) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Curtain | Places: Shop, Premium, Nest, Codex, Camp, Stomping Grounds, Inventory, and **Shop <-> Premium** | cover (close): two halves slide in from the sides to meet, `--ease-in` | reveal (open): halves part, `--ease-out` | first visit of the session 880 ms (320 + 80 hold + 480); repeat 420 ms (160 + 260) |
| 2. Fade-through | Every other page change (Profile, Achievements, Community, Trail, Market, Promotion, Admin, Home to a page and back) | old view fades 1 to 0 and drops 4 px, 100 ms | new view fades 0 to 1 and rises 6 px, 180 ms | 280 ms. Replaces the 3D `view-turn` rotateY |
| 3. Modal and sheet | `.modal-overlay`, `bw-centre-dialog`, Notices, card detail, sign in, feedback, Tavern, Map cards | scrim fades, panel scales 1 to .98, 140 ms `--ease-in` | scrim fades, panel scales .96 to 1 and fades, 200 ms `--ease-out`; at 390 the sheet slides up 260 ms / down 180 ms | in 200, out 140 |
| 4. Panel | Settings, Energy, popovers, dropdowns | fade, 100 ms | drop 6 px, 150 ms | 150 |

Left alone: the Conquest map veil and world zoom (owner approved), the pre-fight title card, win / loss card, pack opener and the Notices board fly-up (they are moments, and each already has an exit when it finishes or is tapped).

### 3.1 The curtain primitive (in `bramblewood-ui-places.js`)

`BramblewoodPlaces.go(kind, swap)` replaces the open-only `begin()`:

1. **cover**: add the layer; the two halves translate in (never animate `width`). The outgoing place's skin: the tent's blue-cream stripes for Shop, the pavilion for Premium, the egg for the Nest, the book for the Codex, the gates, the satchel, the tent door (the mock shows the shop curtain: blue and cream stripes, gold edge posts, a scalloped valance on a rod).
2. At fully closed set `data-curtain="closed"`, run `swap()` (that is `switchTab` drawing the new page under the cover), and while covered switch the skin class to the incoming place. Between two places the skin change is hidden because the page is covered. Wait for `ready` (fonts and the first images decoded), capped at 150 ms.
3. **reveal**: the halves part to the incoming skin.
4. Remove the layer on `transitionend`; a 1.2 s watchdog removes it if anything fails.

Rules:
- **Shop <-> Premium** always plays the repeat take (420 ms), because it is one building, even on the first visit of the session; the skins swap behind the closed curtain. Today it snaps a closed curtain over the page and only opens it (S4).
- **First vs repeat**: full take once per session per place kind (UIFX1-1 a, as built), repeat take after. A repeat is at or under 450 ms by construction (420). The first take is 880 ms, a little under today's 0.76-0.9 s.
- **Skip**: tap, key or wheel during cover jumps to closed (swap now); during reveal it ends at once. Input to the page is locked while the curtain is up; the dock stays above the curtain, so Home, Settings and the Map button always work and a tap on Home during a cover cancels the pending swap.
- **Fast navigation**: a new request during a transition replaces the pending swap (latest wins), never queues.
- **Reduced motion** (`reducedMotion()`): tier 1 becomes a flat velvet cross-fade, 120 ms in and 120 ms out, no stripes, no slide; tier 2 and 3 become a 100 ms fade; tier 4 a 100 ms fade, no drop.
- **Effects gate**: `fxAtLeast('med')` for the skinned curtain; below that, the flat fade; Effects None is an instant swap (no layer). Still no open-only: with the layer absent there is nothing to open.
- **Test**: an e2e check that for each place the layer reaches `data-curtain="closed"` before the new page's first paint, and that the layer is gone within 1.2 s; a unit check that `go()` never resolves its swap before the cover has finished.

## 4. Component rules

### 4.1 Page header: the hanging sign

One sign per page off the map, hung from the same offset on every page (`--sign-top`: dock bottom + 12 px at 1366 and 390), 44 px tall (40 at 390), no tilt (today 7 offsets: 14, 52, 18, 16, 16, 18, 18 px and tilts from -1.5 to +1.2 degrees), two ropes 14 px, Baloo 2 `--fs-l` 800, 24 px side padding, text and one icon only. It is the page's one title: no `h2` repeating it (S7), and place skins decorate the backdrop, not the sign. Pages with none today get one: Profile, Achievements, Trail, Raids, Market, Promotion; the Flea Market's never-drawn plaque is replaced by the same sign; Community's ruled header becomes the sign. Wildpaths keeps its Caveat title as the named exception. Sub-tabs hang 12 px under the sign. A descriptive line, if any, goes in the panel, not under the sign.

### 4.2 Sub-tabs: one component

`.seg`: a pill track holding pill buttons, 36 px tall, `--fs-s` Baloo 2 800, active = wood-brown fill with a gold highlight, track = glass on art or paper-tinted on a cream panel (`.seg.onpaper`). Overflow scrolls horizontally with an edge fade; at 390 only the active tab shows its label when more than 4 tabs, the rest are icon-only (36 px square). It replaces about 10 components: Camp bar, Camp segment, Shop / Premium toggle, Codex chips (2 rows at 390), Community pills, Nest / Forge / Tavern capsules (three palettes), Notices folder tabs, Market segments, Arena toggle, Achievements chips. The Play bottom strip (Conquest | Test Lab, Raid | Dungeons) stays in place and is the same component in its glass skin (owner-approved position).

### 4.3 Modal: one shell

Centred at 1366 (max 440 px for forms, 640 for content), a bottom sheet at 390 with a grabber. Header row: icon + title (`--fs-xl`) left, **✕ always the glyph and always top right**, 36 px round, `aria-label` "Close". Body `--sp-5` padding at 1366, gap `--sp-3`; footer buttons share the row 50/50, the primary on the left. A 4 px gold tab on the top edge. Esc and scrim tap close; `:focus-visible` rings only (no ring on open, S19). `×` (3 places) becomes ✕; Victory's 👀 leaves the ✕ corner and becomes a footer ghost button. Notices keeps its board as a skin of the same shell; `bwConfirm` / `bwChoice` use it at `--z-dialog`. Enter and exit: tier 3.

### 4.5 Cards and tiles

Card tiles in lists keep `cardTileHTML()` and the `.card-tile` system (style guide 2). Tiles in one row share width and height. ### 4.6 Banners and stacks (the merchant sign)

A stack's parent sets `gap: var(--sp-4)`; the banner has no `margin`. The wandering-merchant banner measures 16 px above and 16 px below at 390 and 1366 (it was 12 / 8 from `.wander-card{margin:10px 0 2px}`, S5). It has a 64 px portrait, an `h3` at `--fs-l`, one caption line and, at 1366, its action on the right (at 390 below, full width). The cart sign sits at the shared `--sign-top` (it was 52 px while every other sign sits at 14-18). The check: any banner between siblings in the page, `getBoundingClientRect` gap above equals gap below.

### 4.7 Empty and coming-soon

Empty: a 64 px dashed round icon, a `--fs-l` title, one line of why (max 30 characters wide per line), at most one `sm` action (the Nest with no eggs: "Win a fight on Map 3 to find your first egg", "Open the map"). ### 4.8 Guest sign-in banner

Part of the layout: the last row of the page column (a 44 px glass strip: line, `Sign in` sm button, a small ✕ that hides it for the session), never `position:fixed` over content. Hidden while a modal, the Codex album, the deck builder, the Map cards window or a fight is open (S15). On Home and the Shop it docks beneath the content.
