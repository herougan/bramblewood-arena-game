## Unused keys left uncovered, and why
- Water Bolt: no engine key (BOLT_ELEMENTS is fire, acid, poison, ice, shock). Archerfish's bolt is acid. Needs an engine package before any water-bolt card. Shock and Ice bolts already have cards (electric-catfish, ice-petrel); only Maps 21+ placement is open.
- Sleeping, Poison, Paralysis Powder: already on cards (amber-cicada, fungus-gnat-cloud, nightshade-moth), no new card needed.
- Sturdy, Mighty, Feeble, Fragile: stay passives on cards (owner). Grant mighty / thorns / armor / regen / evasive need no card yet; add once the first two grants play well.
- Kingdom, Evolution, Magic, God and Economy sets (liege, evolute, socket, ritual, quarrying, mining, masonry, refining, plunder, barter, prospecting ...): owner-categorised faction skills, each needs a faction design, not an animal card.
- Engine-internal riders and grants (nocturnalHp, tideGrant, arrowCount, hazardIce ...): parameters, not skills.
- Sunder, Socket, Encore {conditions}, Darksight: weapon, magic or unclear semantics; left for a later phase. Removed or merged keys (SKAUD1) were not designed around.

Notes: Cuttlefish exists (animal), Fossa, Osprey etc. are new animals; Blue Crab is a sibling of crab. Caddisfly (Armourer) and Mole Cricket (Amplifier) are Special / submap carriers, not main-map list cards.
