1. Gaur (was Granite Ox Golem) runs 51-82% of N across Levels 1-10: the On Play stun on all foes is priced low by the current table, and fitting it to the band by formula gave a 6/29 rare, a big power jump across Maps 19-21. If the owner wants no fire on it, Cinder needs another carrier first (the Fire Drake Hatchling's fire arrow, on a Special list only, went away too).
4. Table items that are not card work: Pack (Map 14) and Momentum (Map 17) have no carrier on their main lists; Shield (Armadillo Guard) is listed "To remove" on Map 18. The Contradiction now carries Momentum on a Map 20 Special list only.
5. Balloon Archer (a mouse with a balloon), Barrel Cactus (a plant) and Quicksand Queen (an ant) are invented-looking but real organisms; kept. Ür-Elk, Glyptodon and Ancient Sloth Titan are extinct real animals.
6. Pre-existing test failures, unchanged by this package (same counts on the commit before it): `pale-desert-2026-10-19` (29 checks), `content10-2026-11-05`, `content2-2026-10-24` (named by 1a and 1b; not re-run here, outside the regex). `card-pass-2026-10-19` needed its old-name list extended (six renamed cards) and passes.
