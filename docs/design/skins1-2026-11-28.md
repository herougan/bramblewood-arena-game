# SKINS1: personalisation strategy pass (2026-11-28)

Package SKINS1 (design only, docs only). Sources: owner 11-27 10:21 and 11-28 (the eagle has two animated tiers), `docs/design/vfx1-2026-11-27.md`, ART18 / ART18b in `docs/action-items.md`, `FX.V.skinAura` (bramblewood-fx-v.js, Lab only: ember / frost / sparkle, `{moving}`), `canonical/cards.json` `animal` counts, the add-effect guardrails. Nothing is built or bought here. Packs never cost Maple Leaves (owner 11-27 09:55); no purchase path exists, so every skin is earned for now.

## 1. Skin tiers (a ladder, one card art at the bottom)

| Tier | Look | Cost to make | Example (Golden Eagle) |
|---|---|---|---|
| S0 Plain | the card's own art | none | still bird |
| S1 Static | still art + still effect (glow rim, speed lines drawn once) | one overlay, no loop | still bird, still speed lines |
| S2 Moving | still art + moving effect (lines slide past, glow breathes) | `skinAura{moving:true}`, transform / opacity only | still bird, lines moving past (ART18b V2) |
| S3 Animated | art frames (PixelLab, 8-frame loop) + the S2 effect | one frame strip per card | wingbeat loop + moving lines (ART18) |

Rules: each tier includes the one below it; a card may own several skins; only one is worn. S3 needs a transparent-background strip over the still backdrop, and a still fallback (reduced motion, low graphics). S1 and S2 reuse the existing art, so they scale to many cards cheaply; S3 is hand-picked (a few dozen cards at most).

## 2. Foil treatments (menu of 6, an overlay on any tier)

| Foil | Look | Earned by |
|---|---|---|
| Sheen | a slow white diagonal glint | the 0.5% shiny already in the rules (E6) |
| Prism | a rainbow gradient drifts with the pointer / tilt | pack foil roll (1 / 1.5 / 2%) |
| Gilt | gold border, warm glow | a card's first Level 10 |
| Frostglass | pale blue grain, snow flecks | the Frost / Hollow map first clears (seasonal) |
| Ember | rising embers along the edge (`skinAura` ember) | Map 20 / Dragon Lair first clear |
| Moonlit | dark vignette, a faint star trail | Night events and the Easter-egg chase |

Foils are `box-shadow`, gradients and pseudo-element overlays (no `filter`, no blend mode on a board card). Foil and skin stack.

## 3. Play and death animations (skinned cards only)

- **On play (Detail card, pack reveal, hero):** S1 a 0.4 s flash of the effect; S2 the lines or glow burst once, then settle; S3 the frames play once at double speed, then loop slowly. On a board tile the card flies in as normal; only a 0.3 s rim pulse shows (no new layer after the first frame).
- **On death:** S1 and S2 the effect fades with the card (0.3 s); S3 the last frame holds, then dissolves into the effect's particles (eagle: feathers; ember foil: ash). Cap 12 particles, one burst, never two skinned deaths in the same 0.5 s (queue the second).
- All timing is cosmetic: the engine does not wait for it (the existing VFX rule).

## 4. Tribe flourishes (the board as a whole)

Source: `animal` in `canonical/cards.json`. Counts are cards in the file; the share obtainable by Map 20 (map lists plus Packs 1-3) is a build-time check (SKINS3 reads it), so a tribe with fewer than 4 obtainable cards drops out. Trigger rule for all: 3 or more units of the tribe on one side, alive and unhidden; 4+ upgrades to the second look. It plays when the third arrives, then idles at the budget below. One tribe flourish per side at a time (the biggest group wins; a tie takes the one that arrived first).

| Tribe (cards) | Flourish |
|---|---|
| Ant (9) | a thin trail of dots marches between the ants' tiles, tile to tile |
| Bee (8) | a faint hex-grid glow pulses behind the row; 4+ adds a hum cue |
| Bat (15) | small silhouettes flutter across the lane in a loop, night boards only |
| Hummingbird (32) | a murmuration: 6 tiny glints swirl around the group in a loose ring |
| Otter (34) | ripple rings on the floor under each otter; 4+ a bubble stream |
| Frog (11) | lily pads float under the row, one ripple on each Tide change |
| Wolf (13) | one howl: a pale moon behind the board for 2 s, then a steady rim light |
| Owl (9) | two amber eyes blink in the dark band above the row, every 8 s |

**Performance budget:** at most 16 animated particles per side and 24 in all; transform / opacity only; 30 fps ambient; paused while a card animates a skill and while `matchState` runs a heavy VFX (the existing pause rule); off under reduced motion and Low graphics. Tiles stay flat: flourishes draw on one overlay layer behind the cards, not on them. Each is built through the add-effect skill (Lab demo, catalogue entry).

## 6. How a player gets them (earned only)

- S1 and foils: first clears, Level 10 of a card, pack foil rolls, achievements. A skin for a card unlocks when the card is Level 5+ (it is the reward for using it).
- S2: the first Level 10 of a tribe's card, a map's last-fight clear, seasonal events.
- S3: rare and named (a raid first clear, the Easter-egg chase). Never from a pack roll; a paid path is a later decision, never Maple Leaves, nothing implemented.
- A skin is a cosmetic flag on the account (card id, tier); it changes no stat, no rarity and no balance number.

## 7. Build order (four small packages)

1. **SKINS2** Data and Detail card: the skin record (card id, tier, source), a wear picker on the Detail card, S1 and S2 through `skinAura`, texts + 12 packs; uses the owned-skin store only (no Supabase write until the owner says "apply it").
2. **SKINS3** Foils and the pack reveal: the six foils, the reveal order, foil roll display; plus the obtainable-tribe count check.
3. **SKINS4** S3 frames: the eagle prototype (needs ART18 frames), the frame-strip player, fallback, play / death moments.
4. **SKINS5** Tribe flourishes: three first (Ant, Hummingbird, Wolf), the budget meter in the Lab, the rest after a measure.

Art prompts (if skins add art): always a background, none of the banned words in CLAUDE.md.
