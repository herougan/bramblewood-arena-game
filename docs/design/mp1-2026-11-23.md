# MP1: multiplayer balance for Release 1.0 (phase 1, design and measurement, 2026-11-23)

Owner, 2026-11-22 23:08: "After currency calculations -> multiplayer features balancing -> UIUX and look & feel improvements, vfx etc." Release 1.0 includes "the first multiplayer dungeon, the first raid, and PvP" (action items 11-19 00:02). ECON1 set the currency flow and left the co-op vault, raid pay and PvP pay to this pass (`docs/design/econ1-2026-11-22.md` section 6).

Phase 1 changed no game number. Section 4 lists the changes for phase 2, in build order; "Built" (after section 4) has what phase 2 built and its before / after numbers. Section 5 has the owner questions; their defaults let phase 2 build.

Every number comes from a run in this session, with three new tools: `tools/mp1_coop_sim.js`, `tools/mp1_raid.js` and `tools/mp1_pvp.js`. Commands are under "Files". All runs were small and niced. The machine was shared with another agent's `card_balance.js` run (load 2-4). Noise: 40 games is about ±15 points, 100 games ±10, 1,200 games ±3 (95%).

## In five lines

1. **The Tidelock Vault is too easy and pays too much.** A Map 16 deck on Auto clears all 5 parts 63-78% of the time. Parts 1-4 are won 95-100% of the time, in 3-8 rounds, without losing bramble. A run pays 200-299 🍁 / 152-227 ✨ for 5 Energy, which is 10-15x a road repeat's Leaves and 30-45x its Dust per Energy, with no daily limit.
2. **Bringing a friend doesn't help in the vault.** Solo with the CPU ally clears as often as two players or more often. With harder parts the CPU ally's deck is the stronger partner: 55-63% solo against 33-45% for a duo.
3. **The Goliath is easier than its targets.** This is not only because the gate moved to Map 21. Map 13 decks at +2 (RAIDBAL1's own setup) now win the warm-ups 75-94% (target 60) and the Core 88-100% (target 40). Map 21 decks win the warm-ups about 72% and the Core about 73%. The deck still barely matters.
4. **The raid pays less per Energy than a road repeat.** A raid fight pays about 10-12 🍁 / 8-9 ✨ for 3-5 Energy and a ticket.
5. **PvP against a stranger is lopsided by seat.** The stranger strikes first every round, so you score only 36% in mirror matches (1,200 games). In Live Ranked the first seat scores 45%. Thistle Baron leads 23 of the 30 PvP test decks and is worth +31 points as a leader.

## 1. How each mode works today

### The Tidelock Vault (co-op dungeon; `bramblewood-coop.js`, page glue `gd*` in `arena_app.js`)

**Setup and rules:**
- It opens after Map 16 (`GD_OPEN_AFTER_MAP`). It is a fixed 2-player type (COOP2-1). Solo, the second seat is a CPU ally that plays a ghost deck (`gdFillerDeck`: `generateGhostDeck(defs, 3, seed)`, stage 3 of the stranger power bands, no leader).
- It is the normal fight on the raid board (K12). Each player has a row, and the party shares one bramble: every seat's bramble added up (`gdPartyCastle`), 30 Health at the bramble scale.
- Every card fights at Level 1 (AR3-5, `buildStandardDefs`).
- A run is 5 parts on one board (DUNGEON1): Roost, Galleries, Pools, Drowned Stair, then the Tidewarden.
  - The parts' brambles are 24 / 26 / 21 / 25 / 28, x0.5 for the bramble scale and x1.7 for two players: 20 / 22 / 19 / 22 / 24.
  - Each part has a 20-round clock and no sudden death. The next part's enemy arrives with 3-5 free turns (`FREE_TURNS`).
  - The Tidewarden is Cave Warlord at x1.25 Health x1.7.

**Cost and pay:**
- **Cost:** 5 ⚡ a run (`ENERGY_COST`). It is refunded if no part is won.
- **Pay** (`runPay`, COOP2-6 / DUNGEON1-1): each part's raid-style figure.
  - A won part counts its overwhelm cap 2,500 / 3,000 / 3,500 / 4,000 / 5,000, times the rank (B 1.01 ... SSS 2).
  - It converts at 48 per 🍁 and 3 ✨ per 190 (`PAY`). ECON1 then halves it (`ECON_INCOME_MULT.coopDungeon` 0.5).
  - A partial run pays a band: 4 of 5 parts x0.4, 1-3 parts 5-15% (`RUN_PAY_BANDS`).
  - A full SSS clear is 750 🍁 / 567 ✨ before the halving. A clear at rank B is 379 / 288.
  - There is also a card from the vault's pool at the raid's odds, and an egg roll on a clear.
  - No daily limit: only Energy gates it.

**Tests:** `tests/dungeon1-2026-11-12.js`, `tests/coop2-2026-10-28.js`, `tests/coop3-2026-10-30.js`.

**Owner calls:** COOP2-1..7, COOP3-1..4, DUNGEON1-1..3. COOP2-7 left the floors untuned "for playtests first".

### The Goliath (first raid; `bramblewood-raid.js`, `canonical/raids.json`, `buildRaidFullFight` in `arena_app.js`)

**Setup and rules:**
- It opens after Map 21, through The Deep Dark (RAIDGATE, OWN-1119-1). It was on Map 13.
- Every raid fight is you plus two CPU allies against one part: Left Tentacle, Right Tentacle, Tail, then the Core once those three fall.
- Raid damage is the bramble damage capped at 1,000, or 1,000 when the part's bramble falls, times the rank.
- The fight's brambles are 780 / 930 / 560 / 100 (RAIDBAL1, 10-30), x0.5 in the fight.
- The parts' pools are 4 x 50,000 for each warm-up and 5 x 100,000 for the Core.
- Stand-ins take 14% a day, and the raid respawns after 24 h.

**Cost and pay:**
- **Cost:** 3 ⚡ for a warm-up, 5 for the Core, plus a Raid Ticket. Tickets: cap 10, +1 a day (`RAID_POINTS_REGEN_MS`). The shop ticket is 400 🍁 + 5 🍂 (RW5-5) and appears on 25% of days.
- **Pay per fight:** raid damage / 48 🍁 and 3 ✨ per 190 (`RAID_ATTEMPT_PAY`), halved (`raids` 0.5). The kill pays 400 🍁 / 60 ✨ / 3 🔩 (halved), plus the tiers, 6 trophy shards and 1 card.

**Targets:** warm-ups 60%, the Core 40% (RAIDBAL1-1). RAIDBAL1-3: your deck matters little.

**Tests:** `tests/raidbal1-2026-10-30.js`, `tests/raidgate-2026-11-19.js`, `tests/raid-rb-2026-10-19.js`.

### PvP (Arena, Map 11+; `arena_app.js`, `bramblewood-ghosts.js`)

**PvP against a stranger** (Standard or Collection; they share tickets and rating, AR-1):
- **Opponent:** a recorded or seeded stranger from your Elo tier (K 24). Collection strangers are levelled to your battle rating (ENG10-6). Standard fights at Level 1.
- **Board:** the daily ruleset's day / night cycle. Both brambles are the plain castle at the bramble scale.
- **Turn order:**
  - **The stranger strikes first every round.** `resolveRound`: `m.mode==='pvp' ? 2 : (m.round%2===0 ? 1 : 2)`.
  - You get the +1 Shield (D24). `startArenaMatch`: "the PvP stranger always plays first, so you're second there".
  - The stranger's leader is dealt (ENG27).
- **Tickets and pay:** 10 tickets a day. A win / draw / loss pays 4 / 2 / 1 🍁, halved, plus a 25% chance of 1 ✨ on a win, and Laurels 3 / 2 / 1 (owner 10-10: "Dust 0-1 ... Leaves < 5"). The weekly PvP quests carry the real pay.

**Live Ranked:**
- Standard at Level 1 (D74), no ruleset bonus, no leaders (not wired).
- Seat 1 acts first in each round. The first striker alternates by round, and seat 2 strikes first in round 1.

**Tests:** `tests/pvp1-2026-11-06.js`, `tests/pvp2-2026-11-06.js`.

- Acting first in a round is a small handicap (48%): the other side answers your play.

- **The decks** are built by `factionDeck`: each faction's own Factions-pool cards (Rivergate Otters / Sunfeather Tribes), the strongest by the ghosts' `cardPower`, 20 cards within the caps.

## 3. What is out of balance

**1. The vault's pay is uncapped and out of scale (urgent: it breaks ECON1).**
- 40-60 🍁 + 30-45 ✨ per Energy, against 4 + 1 for a road repeat and 13 + 4.5 for a Map 16 first clear.
- Solo farming works: a solo run needs no partner.
- 60 Energy a day in the vault out-earns all of Map 16's first clears every day, which floods ECON1's pack and Forge pacing.

**2. The vault is too easy.**
- Map 16 decks clear it 63-78%. Parts 1-4 are 95-100% with the party unhurt, and even the Map 10 reference clears it 88-100%.
- There is no protocol target. Against the road's spirit (first fight about 90%, the boss 40%) it is +25-40 points easy at the end, and the first three parts are not fights at all.

**3. A partner makes the vault no easier, and with harder parts a partner makes it harder.**
- Today: solo 73-90%, duo 73-100% (the same within noise).
- With the parts' brambles x2: solo 55-63%, duo 33-45%.
- The CPU ally's ghost deck (stage 3, any rarity) is stronger than a real Map 16 deck. Nothing rewards bringing a friend.

**4. The raid misses both of the owner's RAIDBAL1-1 targets.**
- Warm-ups about 72% (target 60, +12, "less liked").
- The Core about 73% (target 40, +33, "unacceptable" if it were the hard side; here it is the easy side).
- This holds at the new gate's decks and at the old one's. The week still completes for 20+ participants.

**5. PvP against a stranger favours the stranger by 14 points in a mirror.**
- You score 36.0% ±2.7, because the stranger strikes first every round.
- Live Ranked's first seat scores 45.3% ±2.8.
- Elo hides some of this (everyone is equally handicapped), but every player feels it.

**Flags for the owner's card step** (no change proposed here: BAL-ROLES, cards are the owner's):
- **Thistle Baron** is a Heroic 2/20 with Thorns 2. It is not in `refit.ownerCards`.
  - It leads 23 of the 30 PvP test decks and wins 81% against its own deck without a leader.
  - The likely reason the budget misses it: a dealt leader comes on top of the 20 cards for free, while the budget prices it as a deck card. Thorns on 20 Health is also a big bramble shield in a 15-Health PvP game.
- **Faction pairs** (crude decks): early Otters beat early Tribes 80-85%, and full Tribes beat full Otters 100%. The flyers over Otter ground lines decide it. This is worth a look with real faction decks before PvP opens wide.
- **The raid's pay** is below a road repeat per Energy (2b). It is listed in section 4 because pay is this package's brief.
