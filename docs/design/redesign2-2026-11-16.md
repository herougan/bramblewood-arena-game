# REDESIGN2: Maps 0-20 cards on your new skill list (proposal, 2026-11-16)

**A proposal only.** Nothing changes in `canonical/cards.json`, `arena_app.js` or live data. Tick or edit it in `/home/claude/out/Redesign2 - Bramblewood.xlsx` (Change? column). The machine copy is `docs/design/redesign2-2026-11-16.json` (effects, Attack, Health, Wait of each card, ready for a fold tool; `MAP_FOCUS` per map). **It supersedes REDESIGN1.**

- **Source:** your workbook `G6 - Bramblewood_2.xlsx`, sheet Skills, column II (owner 11-16 20:43), and your 20:48 call: "stick to 50+". The basics are all 45 II 1 skills plus the 6 II 2 skills that add the most (a coverage gap first, then the best fit). The other II 2-3 skills and all of II 4-7 sit on **one card each** on a fitting map, or move off Maps 0-20 when no card fits. Every unranked or category skill (S, GOD, MAGIC, EVO, KINGDOM, WEP, R) leaves the Maps 0-20 cards.
- **Kept although not on the list:** trigger x action pairs (On Hit / On Spawn / On Kill: Deliver Lumber, On Ready shields, the Carcass's On Death spawn), the drawbacks Injured (Hedgerow Shrew) and Purblind (Star-nosed Mole), and Bounty (Pack Mule, BAL2-4). They are not skills of the list.
- **Cards:** each main map's MAP list (`canonical/map-pools.json`); a card is designed on the first map whose list holds it: 205 cards. No card changes map, rarity or name.
- **Budget:** `bramblewood-budget.js` through `tools/skill_costs.js` `budget()` at Level 1. Fill = the larger of cap 1 (x1/N) and cap 2 (x2/N2); band 85-100%. A card whose skills change, or which sits outside the band, gets the closest legal stats: fewest 3A + H points moved, then the least Attack moved; never below half its Attack or Health; a 0-Attack card stays 0.
- **Owner-set cards** (`tools/pipeline/config.json` refit.ownerCards): skills change only where the list requires it; stats only when the skills do. Marked **O**.
- **Light skills** (Flying, Tide, Diurnal, Nocturnal) count toward the 51 but not toward a card's two skills.

## The result
- Cards: 205. 151 change: 117 get new skills, 34 only have stats moved into the band. 13 of the changed cards are owner-set.
- Budget band: **185/205** cards at 85-100% after the change (100 before). The 20 outside are owner cards whose skills did not change (question 8).
- **Basics: 51 skills** (45 II 1 + 6 II 2: Keen Sense, Illuminate, Electricity, Stampede, Stealth, Momentum), 4 of them light. Every basic has 2 or more carriers but Constrict and Cure (one each).
- **Single-card skills:** 30 placed on one card each (II 2-7); 14 move off Maps 0-20 (no fitting card).
- **Real gaps** (coverage check below): Stone on Map 7 (Phoenix Mushrooms), and Rain for Rainrunner. Blind was the other one; Keen Sense (II 2) fills it.
