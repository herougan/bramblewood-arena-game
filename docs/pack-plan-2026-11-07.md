# Pack plan, 2026-11-07 (package CONTENT16)

A proposal. Nothing in the game changed. The data is in `canonical/pack-plan.json`, which `tools/content16_pack_plan_2026_11_07.js` regenerates from the game. The calls you need to make are CONTENT16-1 to -4 in `docs/decisions.json`. UI29's Pack editor will read the plan.

## Zone split (CONTENT16-1)

| Zone | Maps | Pack | Submaps (counted from the map that opens them) |

| A: Hedgerow and tidewater | 0-7: fields, beach, cliffs, swamps, the Morel Hollow | Hedgerow Pouch | Lilypad Reach, Smugglers' Grotto |
| B: Caves, reefs and the deep wood | 8-13: caves, mangroves, reef, eyrie, briar, redwood | Tidemire Chest | Mycelium Deep, Murgle Mayhem, Sunken Depths, Undercroft, Clovermead, Sunken Grove |

Today's split is 0-6 / 7-13 / 14-20. I kept your 0-7: the Chest is first sold on Map 8, so Map 7's 15 cards were in a pack nobody could buy yet. I changed your 8-15 / 16-20 to 8-13 / 14-20, for three reasons:
- The Case is sold from Map 13, and the map-end pack turns gold at Map 14.
- The savanna and long grass are open country, like the taiga and dunes. They don't belong with the caves and reefs.
- 16-20 would leave the Case with only 5 maps.

## Top issues

1. **Orphan cards.** 370 of the 806 live cards come from nothing. 32 of those are enemy-only: they sit on a map list but give no reward (for example Badger Sapper, Hollow Oath-Keeper, Salamander Pyromancer, Marshal Tuskwell). The other 338 are on no list. The proposal places 79 of them. With the Tasmanian Devil taken out, 292 are left. Each one carries a `flag` in `sources`.
2. **Off-theme cards (CONTENT16-4):**
   - Tide Spirit is a water spirit, but it sits in the Case through the Volcano Lair.
   - Gangrenous Leech is a swamp leech, but it is in the Chest through Eyrie Heights.
   - Snapper Hatchling and Caiman Hatchling are swamp cards, in the Chest only because the Sunken Grove opens at Map 13.
   - The map review flags the Tasmanian Devil as "not a redwood animal".
   - Moving any of these needs a per-pack exclusion list. Today a card's first map list decides its pack, and the extras file can only add cards.
3. **Skirmish-unique cards in a pack:** none, today or under the proposal. The test checks this. The live Pack-editor rows were not read: Supabase can't be reached from here.
4. **Starter-only card:** the Farm Dog. It is in every starting deck, but it is on no map list, so the one starting copy is all a player can ever have.
5. **Tutorial rewards no longer paid:** the Rabbit Kit and the Riverwing Envoy keep a tutorial-reward source, but since RW5 the tutorial pays faction-pool cards, so nothing gives these two out now. The Meerkat Wayfarer is still the bonus for picking both sides.
6. **Pack 4:** 24 cards (Maps 21-27) are in a pool no shop pack sells. They come only from drops and the base track from Map 21. Not tuned now: you asked for Maps 0-20 only.
