## Left longer than 12 words on purpose

- Resource tips in `RESOURCE_TOOLTIP` (Stone, Lumber, Grace, Elemental Energy), 14-17 words: already under 20; each also teaches how the resource is earned.
- `ui.raid-part-rules` / `ui.raid-part-rules-lead` (13-15 words): they carry four numbers each; fewer words would lose the thresholds.
- Hard-coded tooltips of 13-19 words (admin card editor fields, raid admin fields, account/ritual/frozen chips): under 20, not changed to keep merges small.
- `ui.raid-total-rank-tip`, `ui.dungeon-sweep-tip`: placeholders only; the text is short.
- Not tooltips, so untouched: `dialog.raid-restart-body` (dialog), `coach.*`, `results.*` advice lines, `ui.e10-hardy`, `ui.wind-*`, `ui.en8-daunt-badge`, `ui.spell3-grave-call` (skills/status), `ui.raid-attacks-telegraph-title`, caged-leader lines (fight descriptions).

Translations: all 12 packs. TX keys are in `lang/<code>.json`; hard-coded strings are in `lang/ui/<code>.json` (`{vN}` placeholders as the harvester writes them). The old rows were removed.
