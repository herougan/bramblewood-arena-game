## 1. Lives of a raid: the respawn

A weekly cycle (7 days) can now hold several **lives** of a raid (`raidGenerations`):

- **A life starts** at the start of the cycle, at full Health.
- **It falls** at the attempt that takes its last Health. If the stand-ins finish it between two attempts, the fall is the first minute it reaches 0.
- **It comes back** `respawnHours` after the fall (24 by default, 1–168, editable). It returns as a new life at full Health, and that life has its own stand-ins clock and its own rewards.
- **The cap:** a respawn never goes past the cycle's end. A raid that falls on the last day returns with the new cycle, which is still within 24 h.
- **An admin restart** starts a new life at once. If the raid was down, it comes back at the restart. If it was standing, that life ends unbeaten (see the rewards below).

A life is fenced by time only: attempts in `[start, end)` count. The attempts table needs no new column. Every client works the lives out from `raid_week_attempts` plus `raid_restarts`. `raidRespawnAt(def, state)` returns `state.respawnAt`. The old weekly boss (`offlineRaidPanelHTML`) has no lives, so it still uses the cycle's end.

A caveat: each client counts its own unsent attempts and the stand-ins' share, so two clients can place the kill a little apart.

## 2. The admin restart

- **Where:** "🔄 Restart raid" sits in the raid tab's admin row and in the raid editor (*Tickets, Energy, respawn and instances*).
- **Confirm:** an in-page dialog (`bwConfirm`), not `confirm()`. It covers every instance of the raid.
- **Signed in as an app admin:** it calls `admin_restart_raid` (below).
- **Until the SQL is applied, or offline:** the restart is kept in this browser only (`bramblewood_raid_restarts_v1`), and the toast says so.

## 3. The Raid hub

The Raid tab opens on the hub:

- **Top:** your 🎫 tickets, as "7/10 🎫 · +1 in 5h 12m".
- **Claims:** any claim waiting, with its raid's name.
- **A card per live raid**, showing:
  - its art;
  - its stage;
  - alive: a Health bar and %; down: "🌊 The Goliath returns in 19h 4m", with the local time on hover;
  - your part in it ("2/3 fights · 640/1,000 damage", "✓ Taking part", "★ Competent participant" or "👑 Leading participant"; 2026-10-19, RV2);
  - your instance (when it isn't 1);
  - "🤖 Auto open".

Tapping a card opens that raid's screen. "← All raids" comes back.

**Decision: with one raid, the hub still shows.** It is one tap more, but it is the one place to read tickets, status and claims at a glance, and it is ready for the second raid.

The raid screen gets new chips:

- Raid tickets (with the next one's time);
- "A fight costs 1 🎫 + 3–5 ⚡";
- Taking part;
- Instance (when it isn't 1);
- Auto open.

Each part's Fight button shows its own price. With 0 tickets, the Fight buttons grey out and say why ("No raid tickets left. The next one comes in 9h 12m.").

## 4. Instances

- **Size:** each cycle, a raid has instances 1..N of `instanceCap` players (100 by default, 10–1000, editable).
- **What each has:** its own Health, lives, restarts (a restart with no instance hits them all) and rewards.
- **Placement** (`assignInstance`, mirrored in SQL), on your first fight of the cycle:
  1. a guild-mate's instance that still has room;
  2. else the lowest instance with room;
  3. else a new one.

  **Decision:** join order, with guild-mates kept together while there is room.
- **Attempts carry the instance in `boss_id`:**
  - `goliath:left` is instance 1, the same as before, so old rows and the admin damage RPC still work;
  - `goliath:left@2` is instance 2.
- **The client:** it reads your instance at the raid tab (`raid_instance_members`), and its first fight asks `join_raid_instance`. Until the SQL is applied, everyone is in instance 1, a single pool, as today.

## 5. Taking part, Competent and Leading participants, and rewards (per life)

2026-10-19 (package RV2, owner 08:46: "The previous requirement for competent participant at 5,000 should be changed to 10,000. Then leading
participant changed to 50,000."): the strong contributor is the **Competent participant** now, at 10,000 damage, and the new top tier is the
**Leading participant**, at 50,000. The damage is the raid damage counted for you, each fight's rank multiplier in (§ 5b).

| Rule | Numbers (Goliath, editable) |
|---|---|
| **Taking part** (participant) | ≥ 3 fights **and** ≥ 1,000 raid damage in that life |
| **Competent participant** (was the strong contributor) | ≥ 4 fights **and** ≥ 10,000 damage (was 5,000): +20% of every reward |
| **Leading participant** (new, a proposal) | ≥ 4 fights **and** ≥ 50,000 damage: +40% of every reward, and one more raid card for certain on a fall (instead of the 20% chance) |
| **It falls** (you took part) | Full rewards: 400 Maple Leaves, 60 Magic Dust, 3 Metal, 500 XP, 1 raid card (+1 for the top 10%), tier extras and the top 1% title. A Competent participant gets 480 / 72 / 4 / 600 XP and a 20% chance of one more card; a Leading participant 560 / 84 / 4 / 700 XP and one more card. |
| **It holds** (the life ends standing: the cycle ends, or an admin restart) | ½ of the general rewards (200 / 30 / 2 / 250 XP). The raid-specific rewards (its cards, the tier extras) are scaled to 0–10% by how much of its Health was taken off: the cards become that chance of one card, the extras are rounded down, and there is no title. |
| **Under the bar** | No claim. The chip shows what is missing. |

Each attempt still pays its small per-fight reward and its Abyssal Ink, as before.

- **Claims:** there is one claim per life and instance. The key of the first life in instance 1 is the old one (`goliath:<cycle>`), so nothing claimed before comes back.
- **New fields:**
  - `rewards.failPct` (50) replaces `compensationPct` (40) for the page; `claimableReward` keeps the old field for its test;
  - `rewards.failSpecialMaxPct` (10), `rewards.xp` (500), `rewards.cards` (1);
  - `participation {fights, damage, strongFights, strongDamage, strongPct}` (strongDamage 10,000 since RV2), and since RV2 `leadFights` (4), `leadDamage` (50,000), `leadPct` (40), `leadCards` (1), all in the raid editor.

### 5b. A fight's rank multiplies its raid damage (2026-10-19, package RV2, owner 08:46)

- A full-raid fight is ranked as any fight on your bramble's Health left (S 90%+, A 70%+, B 50%+, C 30%+, D 15%+, E below), SS with no damage
  taken, SSS with no damage and none of your side's units lost. No rank when your bramble falls or you give up.
- The rank multiplies the raid damage that fight counts for: B ×1.01, A ×1.1, S ×1.2, SS ×1.5, SSS ×2 (`RaidM.RAID_RANK_MULT`), on top of
  the per-fight cap (5,000), never past 10,000. A win (the part overwhelmed) counts 10,000 whatever the rank. The multiplied number is what
  the attempt stores and what goes to the server row (`raid_attempts.damage`).
- Players don't see the numbers: the raid tab's rules say "More raid damage means a bigger share of the rewards", "Winning a fight counts as
  10,000 raid damage" and "A higher rank in a fight counts for more raid damage".
- A win shows its rank **emblemed** (the same letter in extra rings, with a crest) on the results; the claim row and the claim screen show
  your best rank in that life, emblemed if you won a fight in it (this device's attempts carry the rank).

## 6. Auto after completing a raid

**Completed** means you took part in a life that fell. The claim of that fall marks the raid completed (`bramblewood_raid_auto_v1`, in the synced progress blob; two copies merge). From then on, the full raid's fights show 🤖 Auto-play (`autoplayAllowed`), and the CPU plays your row as it does in Conquest. Admins always have it.

## 7. Raid tickets

The raid's 🎫 tickets are the old Raid Points, re-ruled:

- **Cap:** 10 (was 5).
- **Regen:** +1 a day while under the cap (was 1 every 6 h). The clock starts when you spend from a full stack. A spend below the cap no longer resets it.
- **Weekly top-up:** at each new weekly cycle the stack tops up to 10. The owner said "10 per week AND 1 a day". **Decision:** both. Tickets above 10 are kept.
- **Cost of a fight:** 1 ticket + 1–6 Energy (`attemptCost`). The Energy is the part's own (`part.cost.energy`, else `cost.energy`) plus the stage's `energyAdd`, kept to 1–6.
  - Goliath outer parts: 3. Core: 5.
  - Thrashing +1 (Core 6). Exposed −1 (outer parts 2).
- **At 0:** blocked with the reason, and no Energy is taken.

Tickets stay client-side, as Raid Points were: `player_currencies.raid_points` (synced) and its clock. The cycle of the last top-up is kept per browser.

## 8. The raid editor

**The bug:** `.skirmish-editor` hides its overflow (a 10-10 fix) and expects a `.se-body` wrapper. The raid editor had none, so everything past the window's height was cut off. Its content now scrolls in `.se-body` above the footer, and a redraw keeps your place.

The fields are grouped:

- **General:** name, icon, turns, art path, trench, live, blurb.
- **Tickets, Energy, respawn and instances:** tickets per fight, default Energy, respawn hours, players per instance, 🔄 Restart raid.
- **Tuning:** boss unit bonus Health, leader cost cap, paying damage, boss Attack and Health %, raiders' bramble, allies a turn, Commons cadence.
- **Field effects** and the boss's Commons.
- **Parts:** name, icon, HP bars, HP per bar, fight bramble, turns, **Energy per fight**, locks, raid damage per HP, trench rules, special attacks, entities, the deck. Simulate stays.
- **Stages:** name, threshold, strip, boss Attack and Health, extra volley, your bramble %, field, **+Energy**.
- **Scoring:** damage per HP, the cap, overwhelm, stand-ins.
- **Taking part and rewards:** participation and strong thresholds, the strong bonus %, the kill's Leaves, Dust, Metal, XP and raid cards, the held general % and the raid-rewards max %, and each tier's extra Leaves, Dust, Metal and title.
- **Ally decks:** name, and `card-id:count` lists, with unknown ids flagged.

## 9. The SQL waiting on "apply it": `supabase/proposed/20261019_raid_ops.sql`

| Object | What |
|---|---|

| `admin_restart_raid(p_raid_id, p_instance)` | SECURITY DEFINER, `search_path ''`. Admins only (`is_app_admin`), this week's cycle. One restart a minute (a double click). |

| `check_raid_attempt_instance` trigger | BEFORE INSERT on `raid_week_attempts`: an attempt's instance (`@N`, none = 1) must be yours, and only instance 1 if you haven't been placed. Admins are exempt. Rows with no part (old weekly bosses) are untouched. |

**Order with the guild-skills SQL:** none needed. The two triggers on `raid_week_attempts` are independent (Seeds after insert, the instance check before).
