# TEXT1: alpha text pass (2026-11-03)

Owner, 2026-11-03 18:37: "Checking and rewording all our dialogue and descriptions."

This is a dated snapshot. The engine (`bramblewood-engine.js`) decides; the texts say what it decides. No game logic changed. Every
change below is applied in English and in the 12 language packs by `tools/i18n_2026_11_03_text1.py` (rows re-keyed in place; Tagalog,
Tamil and Indonesian want a native read, Japanese and Korean too).

How the pass was run: every skill line and Codex note was rendered from the real tables in a sandbox (the d-2026-10-19 harness) and
read against the engine; every TX text, card flavour, dialogue line and map blurb was scanned for typos (codespell), stale names
(CONTENT6, X19), US / UK spelling, length and repeated phrasing. The CONTENT6 renames left no stale name in any player-facing text
(only `canonical/unit-themes.json`, which is theme data). "Group dungeon" survived in one English text and in three rows' translations.

Counts: 14 skill and keyword texts (12 English + 2 translation-only), 2 UI texts, 3 dialogue lines, 1 map blurb, 10 card flavours.
30 changes in all; 1 owner call (TEXT1-1).

## Engine / text mismatches found

Fixed in the texts above: Fork's Codex note (centre + one side, not left + right), Transform's presets (Enraged gone), Season's Grant
note (6 rounds, not 3), Poison and Deadly Poison ("only Cure": Shedding too), Swift (half the attacks), Immolation (and the facing
enemy), Multiplayer's play rule (adjacent to your units; your centre is yours), the Tavern (Dust and materials, not 20 fights), PvP
tickets (+1 an hour, not 10 a day), the Sundered Peak ("final approach").

Flagged, not changed:
- `docs/project-state.md` said a Bloom with no free slot fails and gets +1/+1 (ENG9). The engine has tried again next round since
  ENG15 (FF5); the Codex says so. The line is corrected to the engine (a doc fix, no rule change).
- `ui.sk-fork` ("Strikes the two enemies to its left and right; if either is missing, just the one in front.") is the pre-ENG9 rule
  and wrong, but no code reads it (Fork shows `ui.eng9-fork`). Left in the table; delete it with the next text clean-up.
- The lore bible's "known mismatches" (the Dominion, the knight cards) are still open proposals: owner call **TEXT1-1**.

## Not done in this pass (for a later one)
- The Ritual and similar templates read "1 units" / "1 cards" at a count of 1 (the sandbox's default values; real cards use larger
  counts). A plural-aware template is a code change.
- Dialogue voice: the lines read in character (Shieldback clipped, the Traveller chatty, Whisper sly, Vesper boastful) and nothing
  repeats across maps beyond the "Show me." button labels, so no further dialogue changes.
