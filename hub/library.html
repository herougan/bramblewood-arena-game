Every screenshot Claude has taken of the game while building and checking it, grouped by area and by day, so the look can be reviewed in one place. Tap any shot to see it large; use ← and → to step through.

Fonts were missing until 5 Oct, evening

Claude's test browser opened the game from a local file, which blocked the game's fonts, so almost every shot before the “Card review” set shows fallback serif text. Players always saw Baloo 2 and Nunito. The fonts are now hosted with the game, and new shots are taken over a local web server.

Night tint

Some recent shots look dim because the live clock applied its dusk or night colour cast at the time. That is intended in-game; review shots now force daytime where it matters.

What changed on 5 Oct (card review)

  • Cost and Wait pills: dark enamel with a bright rim.
  • Attack and Health: dark backing chips.
  • Damage numbers: chunky face, lit gradient, separate outline.
  • Names a step smaller, more so on tablets.
  • Deck builder and card detail laid out like the Hero Hall.
